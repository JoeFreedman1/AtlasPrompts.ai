# ROUTINE.md: running Well Listed day to day

Two routines: **30 minutes every day** and **1 hour once a week**. The daily operator session (type "Run OPERATOR.md" in Claude Code each morning) prepares everything, so your time goes on the parts only a human can do: posting, replying and pressing buttons.

## The daily 30 minutes

| Minutes | What you do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | **Numbers.** Add yesterday's numbers as one row in `metrics/YYYY-MM.csv` (or type them to Claude). Guide: `metrics/HOW-TO-FILL-IN.md` | Gumroad, MailerLite, app analytics |
| 5 to 8 | **Plan.** Start a Claude Code session and type **Run OPERATOR.md**. Read the short plan it gives you, or open `dashboard.html` | Claude Code |
| 8 to 18 | **Make and post Slot A** (the video of the day) using the 10-minute method in `marketing/j-tools/free-tools-and-10-minute-workflow.md`. Post to TikTok, then Instagram Reels, then YouTube Shorts (upload the clean file to each, not a downloaded copy with another app's watermark). Copy captions from the dashboard | CapCut or Canva, then the apps |
| 18 to 23 | **Schedule Slot B** (carousel or second video) for 19:30 and the day's **Pinterest pin** for 12:00, using each app's free built-in scheduler | Instagram (Meta Business Suite), TikTok, Pinterest |
| 23 to 28 | **Reply.** Answer every comment and DM on the brand accounts from yesterday and today, helpfully and briefly. Never argue. Never send the same message to lots of people | The apps |
| 28 to 30 | **Tick off.** Tick today's tasks on the dashboard. If a comment asked a good question, note it: it is tomorrow's post idea | Dashboard |

**On blog days** (days 1, 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27) add 10 minutes: publish the article (instructions in `sales-site/README.md`) and make one extra pin for it.

**Community (3 times a week, 10 minutes, in place of the reply block):** answer one genuine question in a relevant community, following `marketing/h-community/community-plan.md`. No links unless the rules allow and someone asks.

## The weekly 1-hour review (every 7th day, evening)

| Minutes | What you do |
|---|---|
| 0 to 10 | Make sure all 7 days of numbers are in `metrics/`. Fill any gaps |
| 10 to 25 | Ask Claude: **"Run the weekly review from ROUTINE.md"**. It will: total the week (sales, money kept, sign-ups, views per platform), compare with last week, label every format, post and ad STOP, KEEP or SCALE (`STOP-KEEP-SCALE.md`), and list the 3 best and 3 worst posts with a reason for each |
| 25 to 40 | Decide the 3 changes for next week (for example: 2 new variations of the best post, drop one weak format on one platform, test a new hook style). Claude writes any new posts and updates the calendar |
| 40 to 50 | Read every buyer email, refund reason and comment question from the week. Anything that shows confusion becomes a product fix, an FAQ line or a post |
| 50 to 55 | Check the money: spend so far versus the ad cap in STATE.md, and whether any price change is due (only per the rules) |
| 55 to 60 | Claude updates `STATE.md` and commits. You check the dashboard shows next week |

## Things that are never part of the routine
- Buying followers, likes or views. Joining "engagement pods". Using bots or auto-comment tools.
- Sending unrequested DMs to lots of people, or copy-pasting the same comment on many posts.
- Posting anything that shows or names you, your home, your voice or your personal accounts.
