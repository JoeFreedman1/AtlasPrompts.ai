# GROWTH.md: week 2 and beyond

Only start on this once real sales are coming in (at least 10 sales, and a first read of refund reasons and buyer questions). Growth built on guesses wastes the weeks that the daily routine would otherwise use well. Everything here stays faceless and follows the same rules: no income claims, no fake reviews, no spam.

## 1. The next 3 products or upsells

Chosen because they solve the next problem our buyer has once listings are sorted, are buildable as files, and can be delivered the same way (Gumroad download). Build in this order, and only build the next one when the one before has sold at least 10 copies or buyers have asked for it.

### Product 2: "The Well Listed Category Packs" (£7 each, or £15 for all)
Deep versions of the kit for single categories where the rules and buyer questions are very specific: **Kids' clothes and baby bundles**, **Trainers and footwear**, **Retro games and consoles**, **Books, vinyl and media**, **Vintage and designer clothing** (including authenticity wording that stays within platform rules).
- Each pack: a category Listing Brief, 10 to 15 prompts tuned to that category, a flaw checklist, a photo shot list and 3 worked examples.
- **Why first:** cheapest to build (a session can write one in an evening), the buyer already trusts us, and it is a natural "order bump" on Gumroad (offer it at checkout).
- **How to choose which pack first:** the category buyers mention most in replies, comments and refund notes.

### Product 3: "The Well Listed Seller Sheet" (£12)
A ready-made Google Sheets template: stock log, cost and sale tracker, fees and postage calculator per platform (the seller types in today's fee rates, so it never goes out of date silently), a "what sold, where, how fast" summary, and a tax-year totals tab to make Self Assessment easier (with a clear note that it is not tax advice).
- **Why:** the number one thing sellers do after listing properly is try to work out whether they are making anything. The tracker in the kit is a starter; this is the full version.
- Delivered as a "make a copy" link to a Google Sheet owned by the brand account, plus an Excel version.

### Product 4: "The Well Listed Shop Kit" for small brands and makers (£29)
For the Etsy, Amazon and TikTok Shop sellers who sell **their own products** (not second-hand): product description frameworks, launch copy, product video scripts, customer service macros, a compliance check for product claims (green claims, safety wording, "handmade" wording), and a catalogue batch workflow.
- **Why:** a different, higher-value buyer. This is also where your Amazon and TikTok Shop expertise is most valuable.
- Build it only once the reseller kit is steady, and give it its own sales page.

**Bundle:** once all four exist, sell "Everything" at a price below the sum (for example £45), and keep each product available on its own.

**What we will not build:** courses promising income, "done for you" accounts, anything needing your face or voice, or tools that automate posting to marketplaces (they tend to break platform rules).

## 2. How to raise prices

1. **Day 15:** launch price £12 moves to £19, as announced (the £12 price ends at 11:59pm on day 14). It must actually happen on the date we said. Steps: `ROUTINE.md`, "Day 15".
2. **After 30 sales at £19** with refunds under 5% and no pattern of "not worth it" feedback, raise to **£24** for new buyers. Keep it there for at least 30 days before judging.
3. **Judge by money kept per 100 checkout page views**, not by number of sales. Example: 3 sales per 100 views at £19 keeps about £48; 2.5 sales per 100 views at £24 keeps about £51, which is better even though fewer people bought.
4. **Add value before adding price:** each price rise should come with a real improvement (a new module, category pack included, updated platform rules). Tell existing buyers about updates for free: it builds goodwill and honest word of mouth.
5. **Never** use fake "was" prices, countdown timers that reset, or "only 3 left" on a digital product. Only show a previous price if it genuinely was charged for a reasonable period (this is what UK pricing rules expect).
6. Consider **regional pricing** only if non-UK buyers turn up in numbers (the kit is UK-focused, so it may not matter).

## 3. Recruiting creators and affiliates at scale (still no spam)

Start with the programme in `marketing/g-affiliates/`. Scaling means more good-fit creators, not more messages.

**Stage 1 (weeks 2 to 6): 10 hand-picked creators.**
- Find them by using the hashtags in `research/keywords-hashtags.md` and watching, not scraping: UK reseller TikTokers and YouTubers with engaged comments (people asking real questions), roughly 1,000 to 50,000 followers.
- Send the outreach messages in `marketing/g-affiliates/outreach.md`, **personalised, at most 5 a day, one follow-up at most**.
- Offer: free kit and 30% commission through Gumroad's affiliate feature (40% for a dedicated piece agreed in advance, as in `marketing/g-affiliates/programme.md`). Optionally, a small, genuine creator discount code set up in Gumroad (no fake "was" prices); if you add one, add it to `programme.md` first so the rules cover it.

**Stage 2 (weeks 6 to 12): make it easy to say yes.**
- A public "Become an affiliate" section on the sales page with a simple sign-up (Gumroad affiliate link request, or a form).
- A creator resource folder: the brief, ready-made faceless clips and slides they can use or adapt, the rules on #ad disclosure, and a list of claims they must not make.
- Monthly email to affiliates: what is new, what content is working (general patterns only, never their private data).

**Stage 3 (after 3 months): partners, not just affiliates.**
- UK reseller newsletters, blogs and podcasts: offer a genuinely useful guest article or a free category pack for their audience.
- Reseller tool companies and packaging suppliers: cross-promotion (we mention them, they mention us), clearly labelled.
- Offer the 40% rate as standard to the top 5 affiliates by sales.

**Rules that never change:** every affiliate must disclose (#ad or "affiliate link", as the ASA and CAP Code require), nobody may make income claims or post fake reviews, and we remove affiliates who spam communities.

## 4. When to go beyond the £5 a day ad test

Paid ads amplify something that already works. Only increase spend when **all** of these are true:

1. The £5 a day test (`marketing/f-paid-ads/test-plan.md`) has run for at least 14 days.
2. At least one ad is labelled **SCALE** under `STOP-KEEP-SCALE.md`: cost per sale £8 or less at full price, across at least 3 sales in 7 days.
3. Checkout conversion on the Gumroad page is at least 2% over the last 100 or more checkout views.
4. Refunds are under 5%.
5. There is a second product or bump offer live (so each buyer is worth more than one £19 sale).

**Then:**
- Increase the winning ad's budget by 20% no more than once every 3 days (so £5, £6, £7.20, and so on), up to **£20 a day**. Re-check the SCALE rule at each step: if cost per sale rises above £10 for 3 days in a row, step back down.
- Add a second platform only after the first is stable at £20 a day.
- Beyond £20 a day, move to proper tracking: Meta pixel or TikTok pixel on the sales page and Gumroad's own tracking integrations. The owner sets these up in their own accounts (never paste pixel codes, passwords or keys into chats).
- Retargeting (showing ads to people who visited the page or watched most of a video) usually becomes worthwhile once the page gets a few thousand visitors a month.

**Never** exceed the ad budget cap in STATE.md without the owner changing it, and never spend money that has not yet been earned by the business in month one.
