# Phase 1: Every business model in this repo, scored

## What is in the repo

The repo is a small static website for a brand called "Atlas Prompts" (folder `public/` plus `css/style.css`). It sells, or plans to sell, AI "blueprints": packs of prompts, templates and guides delivered as downloads through Gumroad. Reading every page, these are the business models it contains or directly implies:

| # | Model | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Atlas LaunchPad**: a £60 "launch your AI business" blueprint (prompt library, roadmap, monetisation tips, email scripts) | `index.html`, `shop.html`, `product-1.html` |
| 2 | **Creator's Toolkit**: an AI content automation blueprint for content creators (waitlist only) | `product-2.html` |
| 3 | **Scale-Up Blueprint**: advanced "grow your AI business" strategies (waitlist only) | `product-3.html` |
| 4 | **General prompt packs**: selling prompt libraries as downloads ("turn prompts into profit") | `blog.html`, `how-it-works.html` |
| 5 | **Content and blog site**: a blog earning from traffic (affiliate links or ads) | `blog.html` |
| 6 | **Paid updates and support**: a customer list that gets ongoing updates and email support, the seed of a membership | `how-it-works.html` |
| 7 | **Niche version of the blueprint model**: the same "prompts plus templates plus guide" product, pointed at one tightly defined buyer where the owner has real expertise: **UK online sellers on eBay, Vinted, Amazon, Etsy and TikTok Shop** | Derived from models 1, 2 and 4 applied to your e-commerce background |

Model 7 is not a new idea bolted on. It is the repo's own product format (prompt blueprint sold on Gumroad) with the vague "AI business" audience swapped for a buyer you understand better than almost anyone making these packs.

## Scores (out of 10)

Criteria: **Build** = can be built entirely as files tonight. **Cost** = startup under £50. **Speed** = speed to first sale. **Profit** = realistic profit. **Faceless** = works with no person on camera. **Legal** = legal in the UK and within platform rules (no income claims, no fake reviews, no scraping, no spam). **Fit** = fit for an expert in e-commerce, Amazon, eBay and TikTok Shop.

| # | Model | Build | Cost | Speed | Profit | Faceless | Legal | Fit | **Total /70** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Atlas LaunchPad (AI business blueprint) | 8 | 10 | 4 | 5 | 8 | 4 | 4 | **43** |
| 2 | Creator's Toolkit | 8 | 10 | 5 | 5 | 9 | 7 | 4 | **48** |
| 3 | Scale-Up Blueprint | 6 | 10 | 2 | 4 | 8 | 4 | 3 | **37** |

### Why the scores land where they do

- **Model 1 and 3 (AI business blueprints)** lose heavily on Legal and Speed. Their whole promise is "turn ideas into revenue in days", which is an income claim. The Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) and every ad platform treat "make money" products strictly, and buyers of this category are sceptical because the market is flooded. They also have no expert edge: anyone can write "launch an AI business".
- **Model 2 (Creator's Toolkit)** is safer but crowded and not your specialism.
- **Model 4 (general prompt packs)** is easy to build but sells for pennies. Etsy already has listings with "650+ prompts" and "4,000+ prompts" packs, so a generic pack competes on quantity, which is a race to the bottom.
- **Model 5 (blog)** takes months of search traffic before earning anything.
- **Model 6 (membership)** needs an existing customer base. It becomes relevant later (see GROWTH.md).
- **Model 7 wins** because:
  - It is a finished product I can write tonight as files (guide, prompt library, templates, trackers).
  - Costs nothing to start: Gumroad, Netlify, a free email tool and free AI chat tools.
  - The buyer has a concrete, daily, painful job (writing listings, answering messages, pricing) and can feel the time saving on the first use, which makes the first sale faster than with vague "business" products.
  - It is naturally faceless: the best content for it is screen recordings of a prompt turning a bad listing into a good one, before and after slides, and checklists.
  - It is a pure information product, so there is no stock, no dropshipping and no food or FMCG.
  - It fits your background exactly. A UK-specific kit that knows eBay's 80-character title limit, Vinted's buyer-pays fee model, Amazon's title rules, TikTok Shop's banned claims and UK returns law is better than the generic American packs.

## The winner

**Model 7: an AI listing and selling kit for UK marketplace sellers**, sold under a new faceless brand (not "Atlas Prompts", see BRIEF.md).

The existing Atlas Prompts site in `public/` is left untouched. Nothing in the new brand links to it.
