# Writing rules for everything Well Listed produces

Every file, post, email, page and prompt must follow these. Future sessions and subagents: read this before writing anything.

1. **UK English** spelling and vocabulary (colour, organise, jumper, trousers, postage, £). Prices in pounds.
2. **Never use em dashes or en dashes** (the long and medium dash characters). Not even in ranges: write "5 to 10", not a dash between numbers. Use full stops, commas, colons or brackets instead. Normal hyphens inside words (e.g. "fill-in", "second-hand") are fine.
3. **Faceless.** Never mention or show a real person as the owner. No founder story, no "I" as a real human (the brand speaks as "we"). Content must work as text on screen, screen recordings, stock footage without recognisable "us", simple graphics or AI voiceover.
4. **No income claims.** Never say or imply buyers will earn a certain amount, "make money fast", "passive income", "quit your job", "6 figures", or that they will sell more. We can say the kit helps you write clearer, more accurate listings faster, and that clear, accurate listings are what buyers look for.
5. **No invented statistics, reviews, testimonials, sales figures, follower counts or case studies.** If a number is quoted it must be a real, sourced fact (put the source in research/). Example listings are clearly examples.
6. **Compliance.** Nothing that encourages breaking platform rules: no keyword stuffing, no misleading claims, no fake scarcity, no fake reviews, no review manipulation, no off-platform payment dodges, no scraping, no mass DMs, no counterfeit or replica items, no health or medical claims. Respect UK consumer law (buyers' rights on returns and faulty items).
7. **Marketplace trademarks.** Say "for eBay sellers" (describing use) but never imply we are affiliated with or endorsed by eBay, Amazon, Vinted, Etsy, Depop or TikTok.
8. **Voice:** plain, practical, seller to seller, dry British warmth, no hype. Banned words: hustle, grind, passive income, guru, secret, explode, crush it, game-changer, unlock, revolutionary, skyrocket, insane.
9. **Facts that change** (fee levels, character limits, rules): only state them if verified by a current source, and add "check the current rule in the platform's help pages" where relevant.
10. **Product name:** "The Well Listed Kit". Brand: "Well Listed". Launch price £12, full price £19.
