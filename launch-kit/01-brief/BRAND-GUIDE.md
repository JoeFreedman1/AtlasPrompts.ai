# Well Listed brand guide

## Personality in one line
A calm, experienced UK seller at the next table at a car boot sale, who tells you exactly what works and never oversells.

## Voice
- **Plain and practical.** Short sentences. Concrete examples ("80 characters", "size 12", "small mark on left cuff") rather than adjectives.
- **Seller to seller.** "We" means the brand. Talk about the work, not about us.
- **Dry British warmth.** A little humour about lowballers and "is this still available?", never sarcasm aimed at buyers.
- **Honest.** No income claims, no "passive income", no "6 figures", no "go viral", no invented numbers. We promise time saved and better-written listings, never sales.
- **UK English.** Colour, organise, jumper, trousers, postage, parcel, car boot, £.
- **Punctuation rule:** never use em dashes or en dashes. Use full stops, commas, colons or brackets.

Words we use: listing, item, buyer, postage, condition, honest, tidy, quick, checklist, template, prompt, sold.
Words we avoid (banned): hustle, grind, passive income, guru, hack (as a noun for cheating), secret, explode, crush it, game-changer, unlock, revolutionary.

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#1F2A44` | Main text, headings, dark slide backgrounds |
| Cream | `#FAF6EF` | Page and slide background |
| Kraft | `#C9A27E` | Parcel-label accents, borders, tape strips |
| Sold Green | `#1E7F55` | "After" examples, ticks, buy buttons |
| Label Yellow | `#F2C84B` | Highlighter marks, key words on slides |
| Returns Red | `#C4453B` | "Before" examples, crossed-out text (use sparingly) |

Contrast check: Ink on Cream and Cream on Ink both pass WCAG AA. Never put white text on Label Yellow or Kraft.

## Fonts (all free on Google Fonts)
- **Headings and slide text:** Archivo (weights 700 and 800). Bold, very readable on phones.
- **Body text:** Inter (400 and 600).
- **Prompts and "code" boxes:** JetBrains Mono (400).
In CapCut or Canva, if these are not available use: Montserrat ExtraBold for headings, Inter or Open Sans for body.

## Logo
Text logo only: "Well Listed" in Archivo 800, Ink colour, with a small Sold Green tick after the "d" (or a kraft parcel-tape strip underneath). Square avatar: a cream square with "WL" in Ink and a green tick. No mascots, no faces, no hands in the logo.

## Visual style for faceless content
- **Signature format: the parcel label.** Key text sits on a cream "shipping label" card with a kraft border and a dashed cut line. Use it for hooks and for the final slide.
- **Before and after:** left or top shows the "before" listing in grey with Returns Red strike-throughs; right or bottom shows the "after" in Ink with Sold Green ticks.
- **Screen recordings:** record a phone or laptop screen of an AI chat tool doing the work. Crop to the chat, zoom on the result, and add Label Yellow highlighter boxes over the parts that matter. Blur or crop out any account name, email, profile picture or browser tabs.
- **Stock footage:** hands-only or no-people shots of parcels, clothes rails, folding, tape guns, label printers, phones on desks. Only use licence-free sources (Pexels, Pixabay, Canva free library) and never footage showing a recognisable face as if they were "us".
- **AI voiceover:** one consistent voice for the brand: a calm, mid-paced British voice (for example a UK English voice in CapCut text-to-speech or ElevenLabs free tier). Never your own voice.
- **Text on screen:** maximum 12 words per slide. Archivo 800, Ink on Cream or Cream on Ink, with one word highlighted in Label Yellow.
- **Captions:** always on, burned in, centre of screen, above the platform's bottom UI.
- **Music:** only from the platform's own commercial-use library (TikTok Commercial Music Library, Instagram's licensed audio for business accounts) or royalty-free sources.

## Never
- Never show or mention the owner's name, face, voice, handwriting, home, pets, car, number plate or personal accounts.
- Never use screenshots from anyone's real listing without permission. Build example listings ourselves.
- Never show fake sales dashboards, fake reviews or "I made £X" content.
- Never use marketplace logos as if we are affiliated.
