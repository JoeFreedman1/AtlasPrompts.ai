# Faceless and mostly faceless accounts to learn from

Research date: 29 September 2026.

## Honest status of this list

- **We could not open TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or Pinterest pages directly in this session** (the platforms were blocked to our research tools), and our web search allowance ran out part-way through. So we could confirm that the accounts below **exist and post in this niche** (they appear in search results with their content), but for most of them we **could not watch enough posts to confirm they are faceless**. Each entry says which.
- **We could not verify 10 truly faceless accounts in the reseller niche.** As the brief allows, the list mixes (a) real accounts found in search, (b) adjacent faceless formats (ChatGPT tips carousel pages, Pinterest how-to pins, text-post threads), each clearly labelled.
- **No follower counts are quoted**, even where a search result showed one.
- Before we borrow anything, someone should spend 10 minutes on each account in the app, confirm the "faceless" label, and note the posting pattern first-hand. Anything marked "to confirm" is a lead, not a fact.

Labels used: **Faceless (confirmed format)** means the format is faceless by nature (text posts, carousels) and the search evidence shows that format. **Face status unconfirmed** means we know the account and topic, not whether a face appears.

## A. Accounts found in search

### 1. @chatgptricks (Instagram)
- **Link:** https://www.instagram.com/chatgptricks/
- **Label:** Adjacent niche (ChatGPT and AI tips). Faceless carousel page is how it is widely described; face status to confirm in the app.
- **Formats:** ChatGPT and AI tips and prompt posts, carousel-led.
- **What makes it work:** one narrow promise (ChatGPT tips) repeated daily; each post is a single saveable tip or prompt; a consistent template so posts are recognisable in the feed without a face.
- **Idea to borrow ethically:** the "one prompt per post" carousel. Slide 1 is the problem ("Your eBay title is 112 characters"), slides 2 to 4 are the prompt in a JetBrains Mono box, slide 5 is the result, last slide is our parcel-label card. Our prompts, our design.

### 2. @chatgptips (Instagram)
- **Link:** https://www.instagram.com/chatgptips/
- **Label:** Adjacent niche. Described in search results as focused on helping people understand AI and find ChatGPT tips and tricks. Face status to confirm.
- **Formats:** tips and tricks posts about ChatGPT.
- **What makes it work:** beginner-friendly framing ("understand AI"), not jargon. That matches our buyer, who is curious about ChatGPT but getting bland results.
- **Idea to borrow ethically:** a recurring beginner series with a fixed name (for us: "Prompt of the week for sellers"), so followers know what they will get.

### 3. @yourchatgptguide (Threads)
- **Link:** https://www.threads.com/@yourchatgptguide/post/DWEU9p5DdEZ/breaking-chat-gpt-can-now-build-you-a-viral-faceless-instagram-channel-like-a
- **Label:** Adjacent niche. **Faceless (confirmed format):** text posts.
- **Formats:** long text posts: headline claim, then "Here are 7 prompts", then numbered prompts.
- **What makes it work:** the numbered list promise in the first line tells the reader exactly what they will get and how long it is. Prompts are pasted in full, so the post is useful on its own.
- **Warning:** the example post opens with "BREAKING" and a dollar-value comparison. That hype and money framing breaks our Writing rules 4 and 8. Borrow the structure, never the tone.
- **Idea to borrow ethically:** "5 prompts for [one seller job]" text posts on Threads, with each prompt pasted in full and a plain first line ("5 prompts for writing Vinted condition notes. Copy and paste.").

### 4. @studiomdesignsco (TikTok)
- **Link:** https://www.tiktok.com/@studiomdesignsco/video/7192983638168882475
- **Label:** Etsy sellers niche (print on demand). Face status unconfirmed.
- **Formats:** short how-to video on using ChatGPT for Etsy titles, tags and descriptions, with tips in the caption.
- **What makes it work:** the caption carries the tips ("be specific in your direction", asking for SEO phrases rather than "tags"), so the video still teaches even on mute. A tight, relevant hashtag set (#etsyseo #etsysellertips #chatgpt).
- **Idea to borrow ethically:** put the key takeaways as a numbered list in the caption so each video is also a mini text post. Keep hashtags topic-specific.

### 5. @thriftysenmum (TikTok)
- **Link:** https://www.tiktok.com/@thriftysenmum/video/7533602733140561174
- **Label:** UK Vinted reselling. Face status unconfirmed.
- **Formats:** relatable Vinted seller posts (the example is about a day of lowball offers).
- **What makes it work:** starts from a shared frustration ("What is it with the low ball offers today?") that every seller recognises; uses UK community tags (#resellercommunityuk).
- **Idea to borrow ethically:** the "shared frustration" hook, answered with a solution: "Three lowball offers before breakfast? Here's a polite reply you can paste." Text on screen over a screen recording.

### 6. @myalturner3 (TikTok)
- **Link:** https://www.tiktok.com/@myalturner3/video/7485429212585790742
- **Label:** Vinted selling. Face status unconfirmed.
- **Formats:** how-to video titled "How to Handle Lowball Offers on Vinted".
- **What makes it work:** the title is the exact search phrase people type, which is how it surfaces on the TikTok Discover page for that phrase.
- **Idea to borrow ethically:** name each video with the literal search phrase from our keyword list, on screen and in the first caption line.

### 7. Faceless Resells (Gumroad storefront)
- **Link:** https://facelessresells.gumroad.com/l/oeuurw
- **Label:** Reseller niche, a brand that uses "faceless" in its own name. We saw the storefront in search, not its social accounts.
- **Formats:** digital product for resellers sold on Gumroad.
- **What makes it work:** the positioning itself: sellers who do not want to be on camera are a real audience. It is also evidence that faceless reseller products already compete on Gumroad.
- **Idea to borrow ethically:** none to copy; use it as a competitor to check. Make sure our product page says clearly what is inside (number of prompts, templates, checklists) rather than leaning on vague promises.

## B. Adjacent faceless formats (not single accounts)

### 8. Pinterest how-to outline pins (format)
- **Example:** "Outline for writing Depop descriptions", https://www.pinterest.com/pin/outline-for-writing-depop-descriptions--832110468647770400/
- **Label:** **Faceless (confirmed format):** a text outline graphic.
- **What makes it work:** a tall pin with a fill-in structure (what goes first, what goes last) that people save for later; the pin title is the search phrase.
- **Idea to borrow ethically:** "Outline for writing a [platform] description" pins for eBay, Vinted, Etsy and TikTok Shop, in our cream and ink parcel-label style, linking to the matching blog post.

### 9. Pinterest "ideas" topic hubs for Vinted selling (format)
- **Examples:** https://www.pinterest.com/ideas/vinted-search-tips/946178640106/ and https://www.pinterest.com/ideas/selling-on-vinted/923788358236/
- **Label:** Faceless by nature (collections of pins).
- **What makes it work:** Pinterest groups pins by searched topic. Pins that use the exact topic words in title and description are the ones gathered there.
- **Idea to borrow ethically:** name boards and pins with those exact phrases ("Selling on Vinted", "Vinted search tips") and keep one board per topic.

### 10. TikTok Discover "answer pages" (format)
- **Examples:** https://www.tiktok.com/discover/how-to-make-a-vinted-description-with-chatgpt and https://www.tiktok.com/discover/chatgpt-ebay-listing-prompt
- **Label:** Faceless-friendly format. The popular answers on these pages include "upload your item photos to ChatGPT and ask it to write your Vinted title and description", which is naturally a screen recording.
- **What makes it work:** TikTok builds these pages from search phrases and fills them with videos that answer the phrase directly.
- **Idea to borrow ethically:** one screen-recorded answer per Discover phrase, with the phrase as the hook text, the prompt on screen, and a before and after of the listing.

## Patterns to copy

1. **One job per post.** Each post solves one seller task (a title, a condition note, a lowball reply). No "everything about eBay" posts.
2. **The hook is the search phrase.** Put the literal phrase people search ("how to respond to lowball offers on Vinted") on the first slide or first second, and in the first caption line.
3. **Start from a shared frustration, finish with a fix.** "Is this still available?" at 11pm, then the reply you can paste. Dry humour about the situation, never mocking buyers.
4. **Show the prompt in full.** Posts that paste the whole prompt are useful on their own, which earns saves and shares. The paid kit adds the tested library, templates and checklists.
5. **Before and after, every time.** Grey "before" with Returns Red strike-throughs, Ink "after" with Sold Green ticks. It proves the point without a face.
6. **One recognisable template.** Same fonts, colours and parcel-label card on every post so the brand is identifiable in a feed without a person.
7. **Captions that teach on mute.** Numbered takeaways in the caption and burned-in captions on video, so the post works with sound off.
8. **Named recurring series.** "Prompt of the week", "Listing fix Friday". Followers know what to expect and it simplifies planning.
9. **Tight, topic hashtags.** Three to five relevant tags (#vintedtips #ebaysellertips #resellercommunityuk), no #fyp padding, no income tags.
10. **Borrow structure, never hype.** Numbered lists and clear promises work; "BREAKING", money comparisons and "viral" claims do not fit our rules and are exactly what UK regulators and TikTok's guidelines push against.
