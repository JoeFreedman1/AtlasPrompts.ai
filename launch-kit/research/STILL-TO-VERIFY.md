## Verified on 30 September 2026

### Money, legal set-up and email
1. **MailerLite free plan:** from 1 July 2026, 250 active subscribers and 2,500 emails a month, capped at 3 active automations, 3 forms, 1 landing page, 1 website and 1 digital product. https://www.mailerlite.com/help/free-plan-update-faq
2. **MailerLite footer address:** the postal address cannot be removed from email footers (Terms of Use); MailerLite says a nearest post office or private mailbox address can be used instead. The unsubscribe link cannot be removed. https://www.mailerlite.com/help/how-to-use-the-rich-text-editor , https://www.mailerlite.com/help/drag-and-drop-editor
3. **ICO data protection fee:** processing only for your own advertising and marketing, and for accounts and records, is exempt; Well Listed looks exempt (confirm with the free self-assessment). If due: £52, £78 or £3,763 by tier. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/data-protection-fee/exemptions/
4. **Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013:** 14 days to cancel; for downloads the right ends once supply starts only after express consent and acknowledgement (regulation 37). https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/37
5. **Consumer Rights Act 2015:** faulty digital content gets repair or replacement, then a price reduction up to a full refund; no right to reject. https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/42
6. **CAP Code rule 3.7:** hold documentary evidence for objective claims before publishing. https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/substantiation.html
7. **ASA affiliate disclosure:** affiliate content is advertising and needs a clear upfront "Ad" label; "#aff" or "#gifted" alone are not enough; brand and affiliate are jointly responsible. https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/affiliate-marketing.html
8. **DMCC Act fake reviews:** banned practices since April 2025; the CMA can fine up to 10% of global turnover. https://www.gov.uk/government/news/fake-and-misleading-reviews-5-businesses-under-cma-investigation

### Website and tools
9. **Netlify plan type:** accounts created on or after 4 September 2025 are on credit-based plans. https://docs.netlify.com/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/billing-for-credit-based-plans/credit-based-pricing-plans/
10. **Netlify Free allowance:** 300 credits a month, hard limit (never charged; projects pause if used up). 15 credits per production deploy, 20 per GB of bandwidth, 2 per 10,000 requests. https://docs.netlify.com/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/billing-for-credit-based-plans/how-credits-work/
11. **Netlify Forms:** on the Free plan, free and unlimited since 14 April 2026, using no credits; a custom success page is set with `action="/path"` (our `/free/thanks.html` fits). https://docs.netlify.com/manage/forms/usage-and-billing/ , https://docs.netlify.com/manage/forms/setup/
12. **Kit free plan:** up to 10,000 subscribers, unlimited broadcasts, forms and landing pages, one sequence and one basic automation. https://help.kit.com/en/articles/16627071-the-kit-free-plan
13. **Brevo free plan:** 300 emails a day, 100,000 contacts, "Sent with Brevo" on emails. https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208580669-FAQs-What-are-the-limits-of-the-Free-plan
14. **Canva commercial use:** free photos, music and video may be used commercially in Canva designs; no reselling the content itself; Popular Music not for commercial use. https://www.canva.com/policies/content-license-agreement/
15. **CapCut commercial use:** only items labelled "Commercial Use" may be used commercially; library sounds are personal, non-commercial only. https://www.capcut.com/clause/material-license-agreement

### Selling channels and ads
16. **Gumroad Discover fee:** a flat 30%, processing included. https://gumroad.com/pricing
17. **Gumroad affiliate cookie:** 30 days for affiliates the seller adds directly (7 days for Gumroad Affiliates programme links). https://gumroad.com/help/article/333-affiliates-on-gumroad
18. **Payhip:** free plan 5% per sale plus processing fees; Payhip acts as reseller and handles UK and EU VAT on digital sales. https://payhip.com/pricing , https://help.payhip.com/article/127-digital-eu-vat
19. **Lemon Squeezy:** 5% plus 50 cents per transaction, plus 1.5% outside the US. https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees
20. **KDP royalty band:** from 7 July 2026 the 70% band on amazon.com is $2.99 to $12.99, with equivalent changes elsewhere. https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644210
21. **TikTok Ads Manager minimums:** more than $50 a day per campaign and $20 a day per ad group, so no £5 a day test there. https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/budget?lang=en
22. **Meta ads minimums:** no fixed figure; set per ad set from currency and settings; Meta advises at least $5 over more than six days. https://www.facebook.com/business/help/203183363050448

### Marketplace details
23. **eBay description:** up to 500,000 characters including HTML; no active content. https://developer.ebay.com/api-docs/user-guides/static/trading-user-guide/item-description.html
24. **eBay search manipulation policy:** no unrelated keywords or other brands' names; every word must be accurate. https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/listing-policies/search-browse-manipulation-policy?id=4243
25. **Depop description:** up to 1,000 characters, no email addresses, up to 5 hashtags. https://partnerapi.depop.com/api-docs/reference/
