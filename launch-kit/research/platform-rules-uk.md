# Platform rules and UK law: fact sheet for Well Listed writers

Last checked: September 2026 (research session dated 29 September 2026).

This sheet is what product and marketing writers may rely on when they mention a platform rule, a limit, a fee or a legal point. Anything not on this sheet, or marked "not verified", must not be stated as fact. Every platform fact must go out with "check the current rule in the platform's help pages" (WRITING-RULES.md rule 9).

## How to read the confidence labels

- **VERIFIED (official):** confirmed from the platform's or regulator's own page (help centre, seller centre, newsroom, gov.uk). In this session the official pages could not be opened directly (the network blocks direct page fetches), so these were confirmed from the official page's own text as returned in search results. The official URL is given so it can be opened and rechecked.
- **SECONDARY:** reported consistently by two or more independent third-party sources (seller tools, trade press such as ChannelX, Value Added Resource, Retail Dive) but not seen on an official page this session. Safe for internal planning. In customer-facing copy, soften ("at the time of writing, eBay allows up to...") and add the "check the current rule" line.
- **NOT VERIFIED:** could not be confirmed this session. The figure given is background knowledge for orientation only. Do not publish it until someone checks the linked official page.

## Headline warnings for the product team (read first)

1. **Etsy does not allow AI prompt bundles to be sold.** Etsy's Seller Handbook article "What is Etsy's stance on AI creations?" says lists of prompts for generative AI may not be sold on Etsy. The Well Listed Kit is largely AI prompts, so it should not be listed on Etsy as a prompt pack. SECONDARY leaning official: the rule appears in text quoted from Etsy's own handbook page (https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1275449912004) and in several independent 2026 guides (e.g. https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-etsy-ai-policy-2026-digital-products). Recheck the handbook page before any Etsy decision.
2. **TikTok Shop UK treats digital downloads as unsupported.** Digital media and digital downloads are reported as unsupported products on TikTok Shop UK (a US invite-only virtual goods programme exists; the UK does not appear to have one). SECONDARY: https://tslagency.co.uk/can-you-sell-digital-products-on-tiktok-shop/ ; official policy page to recheck: https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753824408307457&lang=en-GB . So the Kit cannot be sold through TikTok Shop UK itself; TikTok can only be used for organic content or ads pointing to our own shop.
3. **Amazon titles have just changed.** From 27 July 2026, Amazon titles in all categories except media must be 75 characters or fewer, with a new 125-character "Item Highlights" field. Any prompt in the Kit that still says "200 characters" for Amazon is out of date. VERIFIED (official): https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/33f0a42a-17f1-46ef-b110-ba7512a3c881

## UK law and regulation

None of the items in this section could be opened on gov.uk, legislation.gov.uk, asa.org.uk or the CMA site this session (all blocked), and the search budget ran out before they were reached. They are therefore all **NOT VERIFIED** this session. The summaries below are well-established background points for orientation; each must be checked against the linked official page before it goes into customer copy.

1. **Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013.** Consumers buying from a trader at a distance (online) generally have 14 days to cancel. For digital content not on a physical medium (such as our Kit download), the right to cancel is lost once download starts only if the buyer gave express consent to immediate supply and acknowledged they lose the right to cancel. Our checkout needs that tick box. These rules apply to traders, not to private individuals selling their own things. NOT VERIFIED. Check: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/contents and https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds
2. **Consumer Rights Act 2015.** Goods sold by a trader must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described; digital content has equivalent rights (including "as described"). Private sales between individuals are not covered by the CRA in the same way, but goods must still match their description. NOT VERIFIED. Check: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/contents
3. **HMRC digital platform reporting.** From 1 January 2024, platforms (eBay, Vinted, Etsy and others) collect seller details and report sellers' income to HMRC, with first reports due by 31 January 2025. Sellers are not reported if they make fewer than 30 sales and earn less than about £1,700 (EUR 2,000) in a calendar year. Being reported does not by itself mean tax is due. NOT VERIFIED. Check: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/reporting-rules-for-digital-platforms
4. **£1,000 trading allowance.** Up to £1,000 of gross trading income a tax year (6 April to 5 April) needs no registration or tax return for that income; above that, register for Self Assessment. The Vinted Pro guide reflects the same rule (SECONDARY: https://marszalstudio.pl/en/vinted-pro-uk-2026-complete-guide/). Selling your own unwanted personal belongings for less than you paid is generally not trading. NOT VERIFIED on gov.uk. Check: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/tax-free-allowances-on-property-and-trading-income
5. **Hobby seller or trader: HMRC "badges of trade".** HMRC looks at factors such as a profit-seeking motive, number and frequency of transactions, whether items were bought to resell, modification or repair before sale, the way the sale is carried out, how the item was financed, interval between buying and selling, and method of acquisition. There is no single sales number. NOT VERIFIED. Check: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim20205 and HMRC's "selling online" guidance.
6. **ASA and CAP Code: misleading claims.** Marketing must not materially mislead, and objective claims need evidence held before publishing (CAP Code section 3). This covers our own ads and claims about the Kit. NOT VERIFIED. Check: https://www.asa.org.uk/codes-and-rulings/advertising-codes/non-broadcast-code.html
7. **ASA and CAP: influencer and affiliate disclosure.** Paid or affiliate content must be obviously identifiable as advertising, with a clear upfront label such as "#ad" or "Ad" (CAP Code rule 2.1). NOT VERIFIED. Check: https://www.asa.org.uk/resource/influencers-guide.html
8. **Fake reviews: DMCC Act 2024.** From 6 April 2025 the unfair commercial practices part of the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 bans fake reviews, commissioning or incentivising fake reviews, and presenting reviews in a misleading way. The CMA can enforce directly and fine up to 10% of global turnover. NOT VERIFIED. Check: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/fake-reviews-cma208 and https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/13/contents

## Facts most likely to change

1. Amazon title limit and Item Highlights (changed 27 July 2026; category exceptions and enforcement may shift).
2. eBay UK Buyer Protection fee tiers (changed July 2025) and the business rate card (changed early 2026).
3. Vinted Buyer Protection fee (Vinted itself says it varies by item and changes).
4. Etsy UK Regulatory Operating Fee (reported rise June 2026) and Offsite Ads terms.
5. Etsy's AI and prompt rules (Creativity Standards were rewritten in 2024).
6. TikTok Shop UK commission rate, new seller discounts and the list of unsupported products (including whether digital goods open up in the UK).
7. Platform AI listing tools (new features launch and roll out country by country).
8. Depop UK buyer fee level (stated as "up to" figures).
9. Photo count limits (Depop moved from 4 to 8).
10. HMRC thresholds and trading allowance (set by the Budget each year).
