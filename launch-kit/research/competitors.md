# Competitors and similar products

Research date: 29 September 2026.

## How this was researched (read first)

- Sources were found with web search. Direct page fetching was blocked for almost every site (eBay, Etsy, Vendoo, List Perfectly, Crosslist, Vinting, Gumroad, Reddit and others returned "blocked"), so the facts below come from **search result snippets and summaries**, not from reading each page in full.
- Every price below should be treated as **"seen in search results, check before quoting"**. Several tools price in US dollars. Where we show a pound figure it is a rough conversion at about 75p to 80p per dollar and is marked "approx". Exchange rates move, so do not put these conversions in public copy.
- Where two sources disagreed, both are noted.
- We do not quote any usage numbers, star ratings or sales claims from competitors in our own marketing. They are here for internal context only.

## 1. eBay "Magical Listing" (built into eBay)

- **Link:** https://innovation.ebayinc.com/stories/ebay-reduces-the-time-to-list-on-mobile-with-new-simplified-selling-tool-now-featuring-magical-listing-ai-technology/
- **Price:** Free, built into the eBay app and Seller Hub.
- **Positioning:** Take or upload a photo and eBay's AI suggests the title, category, item specifics, description, and (combined with other eBay tools) a price and postage. eBay announced in April 2025 that the simplified mobile flow with Magical Listing was available to private sellers in the US, UK and Germany across all categories. eBay's own story says early UK testing cut the number of listing steps by half (eBay's claim, not independently checked).
- **Also:** eBay has offered an AI description generator in its app since 2023 (https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-integrating-chatgpt-into-listing-flow-to-help-sellers-create-descriptions/) and AI suggested replies to buyer messages (mentioned in eBay UK Seller Centre messaging news: https://sellercentre.ebay.co.uk/news/2022-february/new-messaging-experience).
- **What they do well:** Zero extra cost, zero copy and paste, sits right inside the listing flow, pulls from eBay's own catalogue so item specifics can be filled quickly.
- **What they do badly (from seller reports):**
  - Wrong identification leads to wrong titles and specifics. One reported example: a mousepad identified as a ceramic plaque (https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-ai-magical-listing-revisited/).
  - Sellers say fixing the AI can take as long as listing by hand, and some asked how to turn it off (https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-ai-magical-listing-complaints/, https://unstar.app/blog/ebay-forced-ai-listing-cant-turn-it-off-seller-reviews-2026).
  - UK eBay Community members complain that AI descriptions are long but miss size, weight and condition, invent included parts, and reuse phrases like "a stunning addition to any collection" (thread: https://community.ebay.co.uk/t5/Seller-Central/Sellers-Stop-using-AI-nonsense-for-descriptions-you-are-losing/td-p/7580075).
  - Reports of low suggested prices on valuable items (search summary of the valueaddedresource complaints article).
  - Only works on eBay.
- **Gap we exploit:** eBay's tool writes *for* you. Our kit makes the seller the editor: prompts that refuse to invent details, force a condition and flaws section, check the draft against what is actually in the photos and notes, and strip hype phrases. It also works for the same item on Vinted, Depop, Etsy and the rest, and it covers buyer messages and pricing method, not just the listing form.

## 2. Depop "Generate Description" (built into Depop)

- **Link:** https://news.depop.com/company-news/depop-launches-ai-powered-listing-from-one-photo/ (also reported at https://www.retaildive.com/news/depop-generative-artificial-intelligence-product-descriptions-photo/727860/)
- **Price:** Free, built into the Depop app.
- **Positioning:** Upload a photo, tap "Generate Description", and Depop suggests a description with colour, category, brand and hashtags in a Depop-style tone. Reported as available in English-speaking countries including the UK.
- **What they do well:** Native, one tap, tone tuned to Depop's audience, hashtags included.
- **What they do badly:** Depop only. Description only (it does not help with buyer messages, pricing logic or returns). No control over tone beyond editing by hand. It can only see what is in the photo, so measurements and flaws still need adding.
- **Gap we exploit:** A cross-platform "item facts sheet" template the seller fills once, then prompts that turn it into a Depop description, a Vinted description and an eBay title, each with the right conventions.

## 3. Amazon generative AI listing tools (built into Seller Central)

- **Link:** https://www.aboutamazon.co.uk/news/small-businesses/amazon-announces-expansion-of-generative-ai-listing-tools-to-sellers-in-the-uk and https://sell.amazon.com/blog/amazon-listing-ai
- **Price:** Free for Amazon sellers (selling plan fees apply separately).
- **Positioning:** UK sellers can enter a few keywords or upload a product image and Amazon generates a title, bullet points and description to review and edit. Amazon itself recommends reviewing generated content for accuracy before publishing.
- **What they do well:** Built to Amazon's own format, so structure is right first time.
- **What they do badly:** Amazon only. Sellers still carry the risk if generated claims are wrong. UK Seller Forum threads show confusion about the title rules that came in on 21 January 2025 (200 characters including spaces, limits on special characters and repeated words, with some categories stricter), and sellers reporting suppressed titles (https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/533f9cf7-3b5e-4974-b523-02e4a1a42c5f, https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/dc1ead8a-56ee-4731-8fc4-e69df434de40). Check the current rule in Seller Central help before quoting.
- **Gap we exploit:** A plain-English compliance checklist and "check my title" prompt that flags banned characters, repeated words and hype claims before the seller submits, for small UK brands who also sell on Etsy or TikTok Shop and want one set of product facts.

## 4. Vinting (UK AI listing app)

- **Link:** https://vinting.app/ (generator page: https://vinting.app/vinted-listing-generator)
- **Price:** Free tier advertised. Pro £4.99 a month (50 listings), Max £9.99 a month (250 listings), per a search summary of Vinting's own comparison page (https://vinting.app/blog/ai-listing-generator-comparison). Unverified on the live page.
- **Positioning:** UK-focused. Upload photos, get a title, description, hashtags and a suggested price, with bulk upload. Covers Vinted, eBay and Gumtree.
- **What they do well:** Genuinely UK-aware, very cheap, fast photo-to-draft, bulk mode.
- **What they do badly:** Another monthly subscription. Output is shaped by the app, not the seller. Photo-based identification has the same weakness as eBay's (it cannot see flaws, fabric feel or measurements). Does not cover Etsy, Amazon or TikTok Shop copy, and does not appear to focus on buyer message handling or returns.
- **Gap we exploit:** One-off £12 purchase, no subscription, works in free AI tools the seller already has, and the kit teaches a method (item facts first, then draft, then check) that works on any platform, including ones the apps do not support.

## 6. Vendoo (cross-listing tool with AI)

- **Link:** https://www.vendoo.co/ (pricing comparisons: https://nifty.ai/post/vendoo-pricing , https://crosslist.com/blog/vendoo-pricing)
- **Price:** Sources disagree. One summary lists Starter $14.99, Growth $29.99, Pro $59.99 a month (about £11 to £48 a month, approx). Another mentions plans from $8.99 a month for 25 listings with AI titles and descriptions. Check the live page.

## Gaps we exploit (summary)

1. **Cross-platform in one place.** Built-in tools are locked to one marketplace (eBay, Depop, Amazon). Apps focus mostly on Vinted and eBay. Nobody offers one method for eBay, Vinted, Amazon, Etsy, Depop and TikTok Shop together.
2. **Accuracy over "magic".** The loudest complaint about built-in AI is invented details, missing size and condition, and hype phrases, which lead to "item not as described" claims. Our angle: prompts that only use the facts you give, force a flaws section, and a checker prompt that flags anything not in your notes.
3. **Buyer messages and disputes.** Listing tools barely touch the evening job of answering lowball offers, "is this still available?", late parcels, return requests and feedback worries. This is a clear, emotional gap.
4. **UK rules and UK English.** Etsy prompt packs and many tools are American. UK sellers need UK sizes, postage words, HMRC reporting context (reassuring, not advisory), and consumer rights on faulty items. Compliance checking (no keyword stuffing, no banned title characters, no references to other platforms on TikTok Shop) is almost absent elsewhere.
5. **No subscription, owner in control.** Apps and cross-listers are £5 to £80 a month. Our kit is a one-off £12 at launch (£19 full price), uses free AI chat tools, connects to nothing, and the seller edits and approves every word.

Also worth noting: **pricing method.** Built-in and app price suggestions are a single number, and some sellers say they run low. A simple worksheet (check sold listings, note condition, set a floor, decide an offer rule) teaches judgement rather than handing over a guess.
