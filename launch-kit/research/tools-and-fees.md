# Tools and fees for running Well Listed

Last checked: 30 September 2026 (fact-check session). First research session: 29 September 2026.

## Important: read before using any number here

The first session (29 September) could not open the providers' pages and ran out of search budget, so most figures were NOT VERIFIED. **On 30 September 2026 a fact-check session confirmed most of them from the official pages' own text as returned in web search results** (the pages themselves still could not be opened directly, because the network blocks them). Those rows now say "VERIFIED (official, 30 September 2026)" with the source. Anything still marked **NOT VERIFIED** is background knowledge for planning only: open the linked official page before using it in a budget, a price calculation or customer copy. Prices and limits change, so recheck before relying on any figure months from now.

Labels: **VERIFIED (official)** = confirmed from the provider's own page text. **SECONDARY** = consistent third-party reports. **NOT VERIFIED** = not confirmed this session.

## Website and email

| Tool | Free plan as we understand it | Confidence | Check here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Netlify (plan type) | Every Netlify account created on or after 4 September 2025 is on the credit-based plans (older accounts stay on "Legacy" plans unless they switch). A new Well Listed account will therefore be on the credit-based Free plan. | VERIFIED (official, 30 September 2026) | https://docs.netlify.com/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/billing-for-credit-based-plans/credit-based-pricing-plans/ ; https://www.netlify.com/changelog/netlify-pricing-update-introducing-credit-based-plans/ |

Netlify emails at 50%, 75%, 90% and 100% of the credits. What uses credits: each successful production deploy 15 credits (failed deploys and rolling back to an earlier deploy use none), bandwidth 20 credits per GB, web requests 2 credits per 10,000, compute 10 credits per GB-hour. So 300 credits is at most 20 production deploys a month, or about 15 GB of bandwidth, or a mix. Our whole site is about 0.5 MB, so for us the thing to watch is the number of deploys: batch changes into one deploy. | VERIFIED (official, 30 September 2026) | https://docs.netlify.com/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/billing-for-credit-based-plans/how-credits-work/ ; https://www.netlify.com/pricing/ |

Because submissions are unlimited, Netlify suggests a honeypot field or reCAPTCHA against spam. A custom success page is set with the form's `action` attribute, a path from the site root starting with `/` (our form uses `action="/free/thanks.html"`, which fits this rule). | VERIFIED (official, 30 September 2026) | https://docs.netlify.com/manage/forms/usage-and-billing/ ; https://www.netlify.com/changelog/2026-04-14-pricing-updates-april-2026/ ; https://docs.netlify.com/manage/forms/setup/ |
| MailerLite | Free plan cut from 1 July 2026 for existing free accounts (reported as 16 June 2026 for new sign-ups): **250 active subscribers** (was 500) and **2,500 emails a month** (was 12,000). ## Advertising minimums

| Channel | Minimum as we understand it | Confidence | Check here |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok Ads Manager | Campaign daily or total budgets must be more than $50; ad group daily budgets must be more than $20 (TikTok quotes these in US dollars; the exact GBP figures were not found). So **a £5 a day test is not possible in TikTok Ads Manager**. | VERIFIED (official, 30 September 2026); GBP figures NOT VERIFIED | https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/budget?lang=en ; https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-daily-budgets |

Each ad set needs a minimum daily amount that Meta works out from the currency, optimisation, bid strategy, budget type and schedule, and Ads Manager shows it when you set the budget. The "about £1 a day" figure quoted earlier is not confirmed, so ignore it. | VERIFIED (official, 30 September 2026) | https://www.facebook.com/business/help/203183363050448 ; https://www.facebook.com/business/help/190490051321426 |

## Company set-up and compliance costs

**Not needed now:** the owner trades as a sole trader with a £0 budget, so the Companies House row below is kept only as a research record.

| Item | What we believe applies | Confidence | Check here |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK domain (.co.uk or .uk) | Not needed: the £0 plan uses the free `.netlify.app` address. Typically about £5 to £15 a year at retail registrars if ever wanted. | NOT VERIFIED (not needed for launch) | Registrar sites; Nominet: https://www.nominet.uk |
| Companies House incorporation | The brief's "£50 online" is probably wrong. Companies House raised fees on 1 May 2024, with online incorporation going from £12 to £100 (and confirmation statement from £13 to £34). A further fee change was proposed for 2026; the current figure must be checked. A sole trader needs no Companies House registration (for anything to do with tax, see gov.uk or an accountant). | NOT VERIFIED (not needed) | https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/companies-house-fees/companies-house-fees |
| ICO data protection fee | Organisations and sole traders that use personal information must pay unless exempt. **Exempt:** processing personal data only for staff administration, for advertising, marketing and public relations for your own business, goods or services, and for accounts and records. A website that only advertises your own products does not on its own mean you must pay; promoting someone else's goods or services does. Well Listed only collects sign-up emails and customer records to market and sell its own Kit, so it looks exempt, but other processing (for example CCTV) would change that: confirm with the ICO's free self-assessment at https://ico.org.uk/fee-checker . If a fee were due: tier 1 (turnover up to £632,000 or no more than 10 staff) £52 a year, tier 2 £78, tier 3 £3,763. | VERIFIED (official, 30 September 2026) | https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/ ; https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/data-protection-fee/exemptions/ ; https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/data-protection-fee/ |
