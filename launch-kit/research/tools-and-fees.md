# Tools and fees for running Well Listed

Last checked: September 2026 (research session dated 29 September 2026).

## Important: read before using any number here

This session could not open any of these providers' pricing pages directly (the network blocks direct page fetches) and the shared web search budget ran out after the marketplace research. Only the Etsy figures below were confirmed from official text. Every other figure is marked **NOT VERIFIED** and is background knowledge for planning only. Before any number is used in a budget, a price calculation or customer copy, open the linked official page and replace "NOT VERIFIED" with the date checked.

Labels: **VERIFIED (official)** = confirmed from the provider's own page text. **SECONDARY** = consistent third-party reports. **NOT VERIFIED** = not confirmed this session.

## Selling platforms for the Kit

| Tool | What we believe it costs | Confidence | Check here |
|---|---|---|---|

## Company set-up and compliance costs

**Not needed now:** the owner trades as a sole trader with a £0 budget, so the Companies House row below is kept only as a research record.

| Item | What we believe applies | Confidence | Check here |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK domain (.co.uk or .uk) | Typically about £5 to £15 a year at retail registrars; Nominet raised wholesale prices in recent years. A .com is usually about £10 to £15. | NOT VERIFIED | Registrar sites; Nominet: https://www.nominet.uk |
| Companies House incorporation | The brief's "£50 online" is probably wrong. Companies House raised fees on 1 May 2024, with online incorporation going from £12 to £100 (and confirmation statement from £13 to £34). A further fee change was proposed for 2026; the current figure must be checked. A sole trader needs no Companies House registration at all, only HMRC Self Assessment registration. | NOT VERIFIED | https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/companies-house-fees/companies-house-fees |
| ICO data protection fee | Tiers based on size and turnover: tier 1 (micro organisations) about £52 a year (slightly less by direct debit), tier 2 about £78, tier 3 about £3,763 (fees rose in February 2025). Exemptions exist for organisations that only process personal data for staff administration, advertising, marketing and public relations for their own business, and accounts and records. An email list for our own marketing may fall within the exemption, but using other processing (for example CCTV, or processing for other purposes) removes it. Use the ICO self-assessment tool. | NOT VERIFIED | https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/ and https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/self-assessment/ |
