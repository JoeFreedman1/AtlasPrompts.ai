# Keywords and hashtags: Well Listed

Research date: 29 September 2026. Written for The Well Listed Kit (launch £12, full £19).

## How this list was built (read first)

- **No paid keyword tools were used.** We had no access to Ahrefs, Semrush, Google Keyword Planner volumes, TikTok Creative Center exports or Pinterest Trends data in this session. **No search volumes are quoted anywhere in this file, because we do not have real ones.**
- Choices are based on:
  1. **TikTok Discover pages.** TikTok auto-generates public "Discover" pages for phrases people actually search on TikTok (for example `tiktok.com/discover/how-to-write-vinted-description`). Their existence is a reasonable autocomplete-style signal that the phrase is searched. Every TikTok Discover page cited below was returned by a live web search.
  2. **What ranks on Google** for each phrase (checked by running the query and noting who ranks: eBay Seller Centre, Vinted Help, small tool blogs, Medium posts). Where the top results are thin tool-vendor blogs, that is an opening for a practical, UK-specific article.
  3. **Hashtags seen in real posts** returned by search (for example a Vinted tips post tagged `#vintedtips #reseller #resellercommunity #resellercommunityuk #vintedseller`).
- Before scheduling, re-check each hashtag on the platform itself (tap it, look at the top posts, make sure it is relevant and not flooded with spam). Platforms change what is restricted without notice.
- Brand rule reminder: we can use marketplace names as descriptive hashtags (#ebayseller) because they describe what the content is about. We never use them in our handle, logo or in a way that suggests we are official.

## 7. Hashtags and phrases to avoid

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| #passiveincome, #makemoneyonline, #sidehustleincome, #financialfreedom, #quityourjob, #6figures, #getrichquick, #moneymaking | Break our own no-income-claims rule. They also sit close to what TikTok's Community Guidelines class as "get-rich-quick" schemes, which TikTok says it does not allow, and the ASA has ruled that "typical earnings" and lifestyle claims in UK ads for money-making courses were misleading (CAP Code rule 3.1). |
| #sidehustle (on its own) | Heavily used by income-claim content. We sit next to it rather than in it. Use #resellinguk or #vintedtips instead. |
| #followforfollow, #f4f, #like4like, #l4l, #follow4follow | Engagement-bait tags. Widely reported as flagged or restricted, and they attract bots. |
| #fyp, #foryou, #foryoupage, #viral | Not banned, but carry no topic signal. They waste one of the limited hashtag slots. |
| #ebayofficial, #vintedofficial, #etsyofficial or anything suggesting we are the platform | Implies affiliation. Breaks our trademark rule. |
| #dropshipping, #dropshippinguk | Off-topic for us and associated with get-rich-quick content. |
| #replica, #dupe, #rep, #fake | Associated with counterfeits. Never use. |
| Random trending tags unrelated to the post | Misleading. Can be treated as spam. |
| "Everyday" words that happen to be restricted on Instagram (third-party checkers have flagged tags such as #adulting, #beautyblogger, #besties) | Instagram does not publish a list and it changes. Check any tag by tapping it before use: if recent posts are hidden or a notice appears, drop it. |
