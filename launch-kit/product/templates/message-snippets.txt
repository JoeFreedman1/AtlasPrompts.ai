THE WELL LISTED KIT: 22 READY REPLIES FOR BUYER MESSAGES

How to use: copy, change the words in [SQUARE BRACKETS], check every fact is true for your item, and send through the platform's own messages. Keep every conversation and payment on the platform: it protects you and the buyer. Never paste buyers' names, addresses or order details into an AI tool.

If you are a business seller, buyers have legal rights on returns and faulty items. Do not use any reply that refuses those rights. Check the current rules on GOV.UK and in your platform's help pages.

------------------------------------------------------------

1. IS THIS STILL AVAILABLE?
Hi, yes, it's still available. Let me know if you have any questions.

2. IS THIS STILL AVAILABLE? (ASKING FOR MORE DETAIL)
Hi, yes it is. Is there anything you'd like to know, like measurements or more photos?

3. LOW OFFER, POLITE COUNTER
Hi, thanks for the offer. That's a bit lower than I can go, but I could do £[PRICE]. Let me know and I'll send it through as an offer.

4. LOW OFFER, NO MOVEMENT
Hi, thanks for your offer. I've priced this one fairly for its condition, so I'll keep it at £[PRICE] for now. Thanks for looking.

5. OFFER ACCEPTED
Hi, happy to accept £[PRICE]. I'll send the offer through now, and I'll post it within [NUMBER] working days of payment.

6. BUNDLE REQUEST
Hi, happy to do a bundle. If you add the items you'd like to a bundle, I can offer £[PRICE] for them together, and they'll go in one parcel.

7. ASKING FOR MEASUREMENTS
Hi, of course. Laid flat, it measures [MEASUREMENT] cm pit to pit and [MEASUREMENT] cm long. I'm happy to measure anything else.

8. WILL IT FIT ME?
Hi, it's hard for me to say how it will fit you, but it measures [MEASUREMENT] cm pit to pit, laid flat. If you measure something similar that fits you well, you can compare.

9. ASKING FOR MORE PHOTOS
Hi, no problem. I've added [NUMBER] more photos to the listing, including [DETAIL]. Let me know if you'd like to see anything else.

10. IS IT GENUINE?
Hi, good question. [HOW YOU KNOW, E.G. "I BOUGHT IT FROM [SHOP] IN [YEAR] AND HAVE THE RECEIPT"]. I've included photos of the labels and [DETAIL] in the listing so you can see for yourself.
(Only send this if you have real proof. If you cannot be sure it is genuine, do not list it as that brand.)

11. CAN YOU POST TODAY?
Hi, I post within [NUMBER] working days. If it's paid before [TIME], I'll do my best to get it out [DAY], but I can't promise a same-day collection.

12. CAN I PAY BY BANK TRANSFER, CASH OR ANOTHER APP?
Hi, thanks for asking. I only take payment through [PLATFORM]'s checkout, as it keeps us both covered. The item is ready to buy whenever you are.

13. POSTED, WITH TRACKING
Hi, your parcel is on its way. It went out with [SERVICE] today, and the tracking is on your order page. Thanks again.

14. PARCEL LATE
Hi, sorry it hasn't arrived yet. I posted it on [DATE] with [SERVICE], and the tracking shows [STATUS]. I'll keep an eye on it and let you know if anything changes. If it doesn't turn up, we'll sort it through [PLATFORM].

15. ARRIVED DAMAGED
Hi, I'm really sorry it arrived damaged. Could you send a couple of photos of the item and the packaging through the messages here? Then we can sort it out through [PLATFORM]'s process.

16. NOT AS DESCRIBED
Hi, thanks for letting me know, and sorry it's not what you expected. Could you tell me what's different and send a photo? I want to put it right, and if it isn't as described you're covered through [PLATFORM].

17. CHANGE OF MIND (PRIVATE SELLER)
Hi, sorry it isn't quite right. As a private seller I don't accept returns for change of mind, as stated in the listing. If there's a problem with the item itself, please let me know and I'll help.

18. RETURN REQUEST (BUSINESS SELLER)
Hi, thanks for letting me know. You're welcome to return it. Please start the return through [PLATFORM] and I'll refund you as soon as it's back with me, in line with our returns policy and your rights.

19. THANK YOU AFTER DELIVERY
Hi, glad it arrived safely. Thanks for buying, and enjoy it.

20. PUSHY OR RUDE MESSAGE
Hi, thanks for your message. The price and details are in the listing, and I'm happy to answer any questions about the item. If there is a problem with it, please open a request through [PLATFORM] and it will be looked at properly.

21. LATE DISPATCH (BEFORE POSTING)
Hi, I'm sorry, I can't post until [DAY] because [SHORT TRUE REASON]. It will go out then and I'll update you here.

22. SOLD ON ANOTHER APP (CROSS-LISTED ITEM)
Hi, I'm really sorry, this has just sold elsewhere and I've removed it. I've cancelled the order through [PLATFORM] so you will be refunded in full.
(Only for an order that has already come in. Cancel through the platform's own process and check its rules, as some platforms count seller cancellations against you.)

Please do not: ask for positive feedback in return for anything, offer a discount for a review, agree to payment or delivery outside the platform, or refuse a buyer's legal rights.
