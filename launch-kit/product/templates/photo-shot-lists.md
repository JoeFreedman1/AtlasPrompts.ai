# Photo shot lists

Tick through the list for your category before you pack the camera (well, the phone) away. Most lists have 8 to 12 shots. Check each platform's current photo limit in the cheatsheet (module 12) and in the platform's help pages, and put the most useful shots first.

## Rules for every category

- [ ] Photograph the **actual item** you are selling, not a stock photo or one from the internet.
- [ ] Use **daylight** near a window, or one steady light. No colour filters. Colours on screen should match the item.
- [ ] Use a **plain background**: a white sheet, a clean wall, a wooden floor or a plain board.
- [ ] **First photo shows the whole item**, clearly, straight on.
- [ ] **Show every flaw** in the Listing Brief, close up, with the rest of the item nearby for context.
- [ ] No faces, no reflections of you in mirrors or screens, no post with your address, no house numbers, no family photos in the background.
- [ ] Do not edit out marks, change the shape or colour, or use AI to "improve" the item. Straightening and cropping are fine.
- [ ] Do not include props that could look like they are part of the sale. If you use one for scale, say it is not included.

## Clothing (tops, jumpers, dresses, trousers, coats)

- [ ] Front, laid flat or on a plain hanger
- [ ] Back
- [ ] Brand label
- [ ] Size label
- [ ] Care and fabric content label
- [ ] Close-up of fabric or texture (knit, print, weave)
- [ ] Details: buttons, zips, pockets, collar, cuffs
- [ ] Measurement shots: tape measure pit to pit and length (and waist, inside leg for trousers)
- [ ] Every flaw: bobbling, marks, pulls, fading, holes, missing buttons
- [ ] Optional: on a mannequin or dress form (never a face)

## Shoes and boots

- [ ] Both shoes side by side, from the side
- [ ] Front (toes) and back (heels)
- [ ] Soles, showing wear
- [ ] Inside, showing the insole and size label
- [ ] Brand logo or mark
- [ ] Close-up of any scuffs, creasing or worn areas
- [ ] Box, dust bag or spare laces if included

## Bags and accessories

- [ ] Front, back and both sides
- [ ] Base (corners wear first)
- [ ] Inside, open, showing lining and pockets
- [ ] Brand stamp, logo or label, and any serial or date code (crop the full code out of the photo if you prefer, but keep proof for yourself)
- [ ] Hardware: zips, clasps, buckles
- [ ] Straps and handles, with any wear
- [ ] Next to an everyday object for size
- [ ] Every flaw: marks, scratches, corner wear, lining stains

## Electronics and games consoles

- [ ] Front, powered on showing it works (a menu or home screen, no personal account details)
- [ ] Back and all ports
- [ ] Top, bottom and sides, including any scratches
- [ ] Model and serial label (blur or cover the serial number in the photo; keep a record for yourself)
- [ ] Everything included, laid out in a row: cables, controllers, chargers, remote
- [ ] Screen close-up in good light, to show scratches or dead pixels (or the lack of them)
- [ ] Box and manuals if included
- [ ] Every flaw: scratches, dents, worn buttons, missing covers

Before you photograph: factory reset the device and sign out of all accounts.

## Books, DVDs, vinyl and games

- [ ] Front cover
- [ ] Back cover or back of case
- [ ] Spine
- [ ] Copyright or edition page for books (for first editions or specific printings)
- [ ] Disc or record surface in angled light, to show scratches
- [ ] Inside inserts, booklets or sleeves
- [ ] Any damage: creased spines, foxing, writing, water marks, cracked cases
- [ ] For a bundle: all items together, then small groups

## Homeware (ceramics, glass, kitchenware, decor)

- [ ] Whole item, straight on
- [ ] From above and from the side
- [ ] Base, showing any maker's mark or stamp
- [ ] Close-up of glaze, pattern or finish
- [ ] Next to an everyday object (mug, book) for scale, or with a tape measure
- [ ] Rims and edges in angled light, to show chips or cracks
- [ ] Every flaw: chips, crazing, scratches, fading
- [ ] Set items together, then one on its own

## Toys and collectables

- [ ] Whole item, front
- [ ] Back and sides
- [ ] Box, all sides, if boxed (collectors care about box condition)
- [ ] All included parts and accessories laid out
- [ ] Any maker's marks, codes or labels
- [ ] Close-ups of paint wear, marks, loose parts
- [ ] Working features, if battery-operated (a short video helps where the platform allows it)
- [ ] Missing parts: show the gap and say so in the listing

Note: check safety rules for children's toys, and the platform's rules on selling used toys, before listing.

## Handmade items

- [ ] Hero shot: the item alone on a clean background
- [ ] Every angle: front, back, sides, top, base
- [ ] Close-up of the handmade detail (stitching, glaze, grain, brushwork)
- [ ] Scale: next to a common object, in a hand, or with a tape measure
- [ ] In use, styled simply (for example a candle holder with a tealight, clearly noting the tealight is not included)
- [ ] Colour variations, if you offer them, each labelled
- [ ] Packaging, if it is part of the product
- [ ] A shot that shows natural handmade variation, so buyers know what to expect

## New products (Amazon UK, TikTok Shop UK and similar)

- [ ] Main image: the product on a plain white background, front view, filling most of the frame, no added text (check each platform's main image rules)
- [ ] All angles
- [ ] Key features close up (one feature per shot)
- [ ] Size: dimensions shown with a tape measure or next to an everyday object
- [ ] What's in the box, laid out
- [ ] In use, hands only
- [ ] Packaging, if relevant
- [ ] Any variants (colours, sizes), each shown clearly and labelled correctly

TikTok Shop UK images: up to 9 square images, at least 800 x 800 pixels, colour only, no text or watermarks other than product branding (checked September 2026; check the current rules in Seller Center).
