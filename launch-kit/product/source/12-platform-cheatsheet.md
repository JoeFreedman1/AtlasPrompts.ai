# Module 12: Platform cheatsheet

Limits, fees and the rules that matter most when you write a listing, one table per platform. **Last checked: September 2026.**

**Please check the current rules yourself.** Platforms change limits, fees and policies often. If this sheet and the platform disagree, the platform is right. A figure on its own was confirmed from the platform's own pages. "Commonly reported" means other sellers and tools quote it but we could not confirm it officially. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these platforms, and this is not legal or tax advice.

| Platform | Title limit | Tags or hashtags | Photos | Seller selling fee (summary) |

## eBay UK

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Title | 80 characters. Most important words first (brand, item type, model, size, colour) |
| Description | Up to 500,000 characters (HTML counts). No active content such as scripts. Phones show a short summary, so put the key facts and flaws first |
| Item specifics | Fill in all that apply: they power filtered search. Values commonly reported as up to 65 characters |
| Photos | Must show the actual item. No stock photos for used items. No added text, borders or watermarks (eBay's Picture policy) |
| Private seller fees | UK private sellers pay no final value fee and no regulatory operating fee in all categories except Motors. 300 free listings a month. Fees apply to optional upgrades and overseas delivery |
| Buyer Protection fee | Paid by the buyer on private sellers' items, not taken from your payout. Current tiers: check the help page |
| Business seller fees | Final value fee by category, a per-order fee and a regulatory operating fee, plus VAT on fees. Rates changed in early 2026 |
| Key rules | Describe the item accurately. Every word must be accurate and about the item: no unrelated keywords and no other brands' names (search manipulation policy). No counterfeits. Keep communication and payment on eBay |

- Private seller fees: https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/fees-private-sellers-activated-managed-payments?id=4822
- Business seller fees: https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/fees-business-sellers-activated-managed-payments?id=4809
- Buyer Protection fee: https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/buying/paying-items/buyer-protection-fee?id=5594

## Vinted UK

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Title and description | No official limits found (title commonly reported as 100 characters). Include measurements, material and every flaw |
| Fields | Category, brand, size, condition, colour and material instead of free tags. Fill in every one that applies |
| Photos | Up to 20. Whole item first. Your own photos of its current condition: no stock, found-online or watermarked images, no collages. Show labels on branded items and every defect |
| Seller fees | Private sellers pay no selling fee (commonly reported). Optional paid promotions cost extra |
| Buyer Protection fee | Paid by the buyer on every order: a percentage of the price plus a fixed amount, including VAT. Check the price list |
| Business sellers | Vinted is for private second-hand selling. Traders must use Vinted Pro. Pro sellers must accept returns within 14 days of receipt, and their business details show on their profile |
| Key rules | Only items allowed by the Catalogue Rules. No counterfeits. Keep payment and messages on Vinted |

- Price list: https://www.vinted.co.uk/pricelist
- What photos to upload: https://www.vinted.com/help/48-what-photos-you-should-upload
- Catalogue Rules: https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules
- Vinted Pro: https://www.vinted.co.uk/pro

## Depop UK

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Description | One combined field of up to 1,000 characters. No email addresses. Depop advises short, clear descriptions with measurements and signs of wear |
| Hashtags | Up to 5. Only relevant words, brands and tags |
| Photos | Up to 8. A short video can be added (check the current video rules in the app) |
| Fees | No selling fee for UK sellers on GBP sales (for listings created from 20 March 2024). Payment processing is still charged to the seller. UK buyers pay a marketplace fee of up to 5% plus up to £1 |
| Key rules | Describe condition honestly. No lists of unrelated brands. No counterfeits. Keep payment on Depop |

- Seller fees: https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges
- Tips for describing your item: https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360020435158-Tips-for-describing-your-item

## Etsy (UK sellers)

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Title and tags | Title 140 characters, clear and readable. Up to 13 tags, each up to 20 characters |
| Photos and video | Up to 20 photos. Video commonly reported as 1 clip of 5 to 15 seconds |
| Fees | Listing $0.20 (about £0.15) per listing or renewal, lasting 4 months or until sold. Transaction fee 6.5% including postage. UK payment processing 4% + £0.20. A UK Regulatory Operating Fee applies (check the current rate). Offsite Ads fees apply to orders from Etsy's adverts |
| Key rules | Handmade, vintage (at least 20 years old; check the current rule) and craft supplies only. If AI was used in creating an item or its design, Etsy's Creativity Standards (commonly reported) ask you to say so in the description and to label the item "Designed by". We found no Etsy rule about using AI to help write listing text: check the Creativity Standards. Selling lists of AI prompts is not allowed. No keyword stuffing, counterfeits or health claims |

- Fees and payments policy: https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/fees/
- Payment processing fees: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy
- Creativity Standards: https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity

## Amazon UK

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Title | 75 characters or fewer including spaces in all categories except media, from 27 July 2026. Amazon may shorten over-length titles itself |
| Item Highlights | New field of up to 125 characters below the title. Only works if the title is 75 characters or fewer |
| Title rules | No ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ unless part of the brand name. The same word no more than twice (small words excepted). No promotional wording |
| Bullets and search terms | Up to 5 bullets, no prices or delivery details. Backend search terms have a byte limit (commonly reported: 250 bytes); going over can mean the field is ignored. No title words or other brands |
| Fees | Individual plan £0.75 per item sold, or Professional £25 a month excluding VAT. Plus a referral fee by category, and fulfilment fees if you use FBA |
| Key rules | Accurate, verifiable product information. No health claims unless allowed for the category. Reference ("was") prices must be genuine |

- Selling fees and plans: https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing
- Title change announcement (July 2026): https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/33f0a42a-17f1-46ef-b110-ba7512a3c881
- Product title requirements: https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/help/hub/reference/external/GYTR6SYGFA5E3EQC

## TikTok Shop UK

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Title | At least 15 characters (maximum: check Seller Center). Accurate and concise. No promotions, shop URL or seller name, no mention of TikTok or other platforms, no symbols |
| Description | At least 30 words. Claims must be genuine, accurate and verifiable |
| Images | Up to 9 square images, at least 800 x 800 pixels, at least 3. Colour, main image shows the front, no text or watermarks other than product branding |
| Commission | Standard 9% (inclusive of VAT). Lower rates can apply to new sellers and some categories. Affiliate commission is extra |
| Banned claims | No medicines or medical devices. No claims to cure, treat, prevent or relieve any condition, anywhere. No weight loss claims without diet and exercise, no before and after weight images |
| AI content | Realistic AI images or video must be labelled and must not make the product look different |
| Key rules | Check the prohibited and restricted products lists. Some categories need approval. Keep orders and payment in TikTok Shop. Discounts must be real |

## Beyond the platforms: UK rules worth knowing

These are pointers, not legal advice. Please read the official pages yourself.

- **Selling as a business?** Read GOV.UK on accepting returns and giving refunds: https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds
- **Tax:** tax rules for online selling are explained on gov.uk. Search "selling online tax" on gov.uk, or ask an accountant. This kit does not cover tax.
- **Making and selling your own products?** Product safety and labelling rules depend on the product (candles, toys, cosmetics, electricals, children's clothing and items that touch food all have their own). If you post to Northern Ireland or the EU, check whether the EU General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR) applies to you. Search GOV.UK for "product safety for businesses", and use C11 to gather your details.
