# 12 Platform cheatsheet

One quick reference per platform: title and description limits, tags, photos, the main seller fees and the rules that matter most when you write a listing.

**Last checked: September 2026.**

**Please check the current rules yourself.** Platforms change limits, fees and policies often, sometimes with little notice. Before you rely on any figure here, check the current rule in the platform's help pages (links under each table). If this sheet and the platform disagree, the platform is right.

How to read this sheet:

- A figure on its own was confirmed from the platform's own pages when we checked.
- **"Check in the platform's help pages"** means we could not confirm it from an official source. Where a figure is commonly reported by other sellers and tools, we show it in brackets as "(commonly reported: ...)" so you know roughly what to expect, but please confirm it before relying on it.
- We are not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these platforms. This is a summary for sellers, not legal or tax advice.

## eBay UK

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Title | 80 characters. Put the most important words first (brand, item type, model, size, colour). |
| Subtitle | Optional paid upgrade. Length: check in the platform's help pages. |
| Description | Check in the platform's help pages (very long descriptions are allowed; active content such as scripts is not). |
| Item specifics | Fill in as many as apply: they help your item appear in filtered searches. Value length: check in the platform's help pages. |
| Photos | Check in the platform's help pages (commonly reported: up to 24 free). Photos must show the actual item. Picture rules on size, borders, text and watermarks: check eBay's Picture policy. |
| Private seller fees | UK-based private sellers pay no final value fee and no regulatory operating fee when an item sells, in all categories except Motors (cars, motorcycles and vehicles). 300 free listings a month, then a listing fee per listing. Fees apply for optional upgrades and for delivery to an overseas address. |
| Buyer Protection fee | On items from private sellers, the buyer pays a Buyer Protection fee (a small flat amount plus a tiered percentage of the item price). It does not come out of the seller's payout. Current tiers: check in the platform's help pages. |
| Business seller fees | Business sellers pay a final value fee by category, a per-order fee and a regulatory operating fee, plus VAT on fees. Rates changed in early 2026: check in the platform's help pages. |
| Key rules | Describe the actual item and its condition accurately. No unrelated brand names or keyword spam in titles (search and browse manipulation policy). No counterfeits or replicas. Keep all communication and payment on eBay. |

## Vinted UK

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Title | Check in the platform's help pages (commonly reported: 100 characters). Short and plain works well: brand, item, size, colour. |
| Description | Check in the platform's help pages. Include measurements, material and every flaw. |
| Tags and keywords | Vinted uses structured fields (category, brand, size, condition, colour, material) rather than free tags. Fill in every field that applies. |
| Photos | Up to 20 photos per listing. Show the whole item in the first photo. Use your own photos showing its current condition: no stock images, no images found online, no watermarks, no collages. Show labels and logos for branded items, and show any defects. |
| Seller fees | Private sellers pay no selling fee (commonly reported; check the price list). Optional paid promotions such as bumps have a cost. |
| Buyer Protection fee | Paid by the buyer on every order, added at checkout. Made up of a percentage of the item price plus a fixed amount, and includes VAT. Current amounts: check the price list. |
| Business sellers | Vinted is for private second-hand selling. Traders must use Vinted Pro. Pro sellers must accept returns within 14 days of receipt, and their business details are shown on their profile. |
| Key rules | Only list items allowed under Vinted's Catalogue Rules. No counterfeits. Keep payment and messages on Vinted. |

## Depop UK

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Title and description | One combined description field. Length: check in the platform's help pages (commonly reported: 1,000 characters). Depop advises short, clear descriptions with measurements and any signs of wear. |
| Hashtags | Up to 5. Use only relevant words, brands and tags. |
| Photos | Up to 8 photos. A short video can also be added (check the current video rules in the app). |
| Selling fee | No selling fee for UK sellers on sales in GBP (removed for listings created from 20 March 2024). |
| Payment processing | Still charged to the seller on each sale. Current rate: check in the platform's help pages. |
| Boosting | Optional paid boosted listings. Current fee: check in the platform's help pages. |
| Buyer fee | UK buyers pay a marketplace fee of up to 5% of the item price plus a fixed amount of up to £1 (excluding taxes and postage). |
| Key rules | Describe condition honestly. Only relevant hashtags (no lists of unrelated brands). No counterfeits. Keep payment on Depop. Check Depop's prohibited items list. |

## Etsy (UK sellers)

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Title | 140 characters. Etsy's own guidance favours clear, readable titles with the most important words first. |
| Description | Check in the platform's help pages. |
| Tags | Up to 13 tags, each up to 20 characters. |
| Photos and video | Up to 20 photos. Video: check in the platform's help pages (commonly reported: 1 short video of 5 to 15 seconds). |
| Listing fee | $0.20 USD per listing or renewal (shown to UK sellers as about £0.15 and billed at the exchange rate). A listing lasts 4 months or until it sells. |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% of the sale price, including the postage you charge. |
| Payment processing (UK) | 4% + £0.20 per order. |
| Regulatory Operating Fee (UK) | Charged on UK orders. Current rate: check in the platform's help pages. |
| Offsite Ads | A fee on orders that come from Etsy's offsite adverts (15% or 12% depending on shop sales, capped per order; commonly reported). Check in the platform's help pages. |
| Key rules | Etsy is for handmade items, vintage items and craft supplies. Vintage means at least 20 years old (check the current rule). If AI was used in creating an item, Etsy's Creativity Standards ask you to say so in the description. No keyword stuffing, no counterfeits, no health or medical claims. |

## Amazon UK

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Title | 75 characters or fewer, including spaces, in all categories except media (rule from 27 July 2026). Amazon may shorten over-length titles itself. |
| Item Highlights | New field of up to 125 characters, shown below the title. Your title must be 75 characters or fewer for it to work. |
| Title rules | No special characters ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ unless part of the brand name. The same word no more than twice (prepositions, articles and conjunctions excepted). No promotional wording. |
| Bullet points | Up to 5. Length per bullet: check in the platform's help pages. No prices, delivery or company information in bullets. |
| Description | Check in the platform's help pages (commonly reported: 2,000 characters). |
| Search terms (backend keywords) | A byte limit applies, and going over it can mean the whole field is ignored. Check in the platform's help pages (commonly reported: 250 bytes). Do not repeat title words or use other brands' names. |
| Photos | Main image rules (white background, no added text or logos): check in the platform's help pages. Image count: check in the platform's help pages. |
| Selling plans | Individual: £0.75 per item sold. Professional: £25 a month excluding VAT. |
| Referral fees | Charged on each sale, varying by category. Check the current rate for your category on Amazon's pricing page. Fulfilment fees apply if you use FBA. |
| Key rules | Accurate, verifiable product information only. No health or medical claims unless allowed for the category. No other brands' names as keywords. Reference ("was") prices must be genuine. |

## TikTok Shop UK

| What | Rule |
|---|---|
| Product title | At least 15 characters. Maximum: check in the platform's help pages. Must be accurate and concise (product type, material, key features, quantity, size). No promotional wording (for example "x% off"), no shop URL or seller name, no mention of TikTok or other platforms, no symbols or special characters. |
| Description | At least 30 words. Keep it concise and readable. Maximum: check in the platform's help pages. Claims must be genuine, accurate and verifiable. |
| Images | Up to 9 square images, at least 800 x 800 pixels. Upload at least 3. No duplicates. No text or watermarks other than product branding. Colour images only. The main image shows the front of the product. |
| Commission | Standard platform commission of 9% (inclusive of VAT). Lower rates can apply to new sellers and some categories. Affiliate creator commission is extra and set by you. |
| Banned claims | No medicines or medical devices. No claims that a product cures, treats, prevents or relieves any disease or condition, anywhere in the listing or video. No weight loss claims without diet and exercise, no before and after weight images, no claims that weight loss is easy, guaranteed or permanent. |
| AI content | Realistic AI-generated images or video must be labelled, and must not make the product look different from reality. Check the AIGC policy for detail. |
| Key rules | Check the prohibited and restricted products lists before listing. Some categories need extra approval or documents. Keep orders and payment in TikTok Shop. Any discount must be real. |

## Beyond the platforms: UK rules worth knowing

These are not platform rules, and we have not verified the detail here. Please read the official pages yourself.

- **Selling as a business?** Consumer rights on returns and faulty goods apply to traders. Read GOV.UK's guidance on accepting returns and giving refunds: https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds
- **Platform reporting to HMRC.** Online platforms report some sellers' details to HMRC. Being reported does not by itself mean tax is due. Read: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/reporting-rules-for-digital-platforms
- **Trading allowance and tax.** Read HMRC's guidance on tax-free allowances on trading income: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/tax-free-allowances-on-property-and-trading-income
