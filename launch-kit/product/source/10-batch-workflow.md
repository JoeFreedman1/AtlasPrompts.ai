# 10 Batch workflow

Listing one item with AI is quick. Listing ten items one at a time, starting a new chat for each, is not. This module shows you how to set up one chat session that knows your shop, feed it a stack of items, and get back tidy listings you can copy straight in, or paste into a spreadsheet for a bulk upload or cross-listing tool.

The order we suggest:

1. **W1** Write your shop style instruction once. Save it in your phone's notes.
2. **W3** Turn your rough notes for the whole pile into Listing Briefs in one go.
3. **W2** Start a batch session, paste your style instruction, then feed it up to 10 briefs.
4. **W4** Get the batch back as a table or CSV.
5. **W5** Run a quick accuracy check over the whole batch.
6. **W6 to W8** Plan the week, repurpose across platforms, and refresh listings that have gone quiet.

A tip for phones: free AI chats can lose track in very long sessions. If answers start drifting (American spelling, invented details, forgetting your rules), start a fresh chat and paste your shop style instruction again. Batches of 5 to 10 items work best.

Privacy: never paste buyers' names, addresses or order details into an AI tool.

### W1 Build your reusable "shop style" instruction

**Use it when:** you are fed up of re-explaining your shop to the AI every time. Do this once, save the result, and paste it at the start of every session.

**Paste this:**
```text
Help me write a short, reusable "shop style instruction" I can paste at the start of any AI chat before I list items. Write in UK English.

Here are my answers:
- Platforms I sell on: [E.G. VINTED AND EBAY UK]
- What I mostly sell: [E.G. WOMEN'S CLOTHING SIZES 8 TO 18, SOME HOMEWARE]
- Am I a private seller or a business? [PRIVATE / BUSINESS]
- My tone: [E.G. FRIENDLY AND PLAIN, NO EMOJIS / A FEW EMOJIS OK ON DEPOP]
- How I describe condition: [E.G. NEW WITH TAGS / NEW WITHOUT TAGS / VERY GOOD / GOOD / SATISFACTORY]
- How I measure: [E.G. PIT TO PIT AND LENGTH IN CM, LAID FLAT]
- My postage: [E.G. POSTED WITHIN 2 WORKING DAYS, SMOKE-FREE HOME, PET-FREE HOME, ONLY IF TRUE]
- My returns line: [E.G. "PRIVATE SELLER: I DON'T ACCEPT RETURNS FOR CHANGE OF MIND, BUT YOU'RE COVERED IF THE ITEM ISN'T AS DESCRIBED" OR THE BUSINESS RETURNS POLICY]
- Words or things I never want: [E.G. "STUNNING", "RARE" UNLESS IT IS, EMOJIS IN TITLES]
- Anything else: [E.G. ALWAYS LIST FLAWS FIRST IN THE CONDITION SECTION]

Turn this into a numbered instruction of no more than 15 lines that starts with "You are my listing assistant for a UK shop." It must include these fixed rules:
- Use UK English spelling, UK sizes and £.
- Only use facts I give you in each Listing Brief. Never invent brand, size, material, measurements, age, condition or included items.
- Where something is missing, write [CHECK: what is missing] instead of guessing.
- Respect each platform's title and description limits. For eBay UK, keep titles to 80 characters or fewer.
- No hype words, no keyword stuffing, no misleading claims, no health or medical claims, no fake urgency, no mentions of other platforms in a listing, and never suggest payment outside the platform.
- Always state flaws clearly.
Keep it short enough to fit in a phone note.
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example for a private seller on Vinted and eBay UK.

> You are my listing assistant for a UK shop.
> 1. I am a private seller on Vinted and eBay UK. I mainly sell women's clothing (UK sizes 8 to 18) and some homeware.
> 2. Use UK English, UK sizes and £.
> 3. Only use facts from the Listing Brief I give you. Never invent details.
> 4. If something is missing, write [CHECK: what is missing].
> 5. eBay UK titles: 80 characters or fewer, most important words first. Vinted titles: short and plain.
> 6. Tone: friendly and plain. No emojis. Never use "stunning", "gorgeous" or "rare" unless I say so.
> 7. Condition words: New with tags, New without tags, Very good, Good, Satisfactory.
> 8. Measurements: pit to pit and length in cm, laid flat.
> 9. List flaws first in the condition section, clearly and calmly.
> 10. Postage line: "Posted within 2 working days from a smoke-free home."
> 11. Returns line: "Private seller, so no returns for change of mind. You're covered if the item isn't as described."
> 12. No keyword stuffing, no misleading or health claims, no fake urgency, no other platform names, never suggest paying outside the platform.

**Check before you post:**
- Only include "smoke-free" or "pet-free" if it is true for every item.
- If you are a business seller, your returns line must reflect UK consumer rights (for most online sales, buyers have a right to cancel within 14 days). Check the platform's business seller rules.
- Re-read it every few months, and update it if your platforms or policies change.

### W2 Start a batch session (list up to 10 items in one chat)

**Use it when:** you have a pile of items photographed and noted, and want to list them in one sitting.

**Paste this:**
```text
[PASTE YOUR SHOP STYLE INSTRUCTION FROM W1]

We are going to list [NUMBER, UP TO 10] items in this chat. Platform for this batch: [EBAY UK / VINTED / DEPOP / ETSY].

How this works:
- I will paste one Listing Brief at a time, each starting with "ITEM [NUMBER]".
- For each item, reply with: Title, Description, Condition line, [ITEM SPECIFICS FOR EBAY / TAGS FOR ETSY / HASHTAGS FOR DEPOP / NOTHING EXTRA FOR VINTED], and a list of any [CHECK: ...] items.
- Keep the same layout for every item so I can copy quickly on my phone.
- Do not carry facts from one item to another. Each item uses only its own brief.
- Do not add a price unless I include one in the brief.
- When I type "DONE", stop and wait for my next instruction.

Reply with "Ready for ITEM 1" and nothing else.
```

Then paste each brief, for example:

```text
ITEM 1
[PASTE LISTING BRIEF]
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example, platform eBay UK, ITEM 3 brief: "Joules women's quilted gilet, navy, size 12, zip front, 2 zip pockets, light pilling on inside collar, pre-owned."

> **ITEM 3**
> **Title:** Joules Womens Navy Quilted Gilet Size 12 Zip Front Pockets Pre-Owned (68 characters)
> **Description:** Navy quilted gilet from Joules in women's size 12. Full zip front and two zip pockets. Pre-owned in good condition with light pilling on the inside collar (see photos). Measurements: pit to pit [CHECK: cm], length [CHECK: cm]. Posted within 2 working days from a smoke-free home.
> **Condition line:** Pre-owned, good. Light pilling on the inside collar.
> **Item specifics:** Brand: Joules. Size: 12. Colour: Blue [CHECK: eBay's colour list may use Blue for navy]. Type: Gilet. Department: Women. Closure: Zip. Outer shell material: [CHECK].
> **To check:** measurements, outer shell material.

**Check before you post:**
- Scan each item for facts that "leaked" from an earlier item (a size or brand that belongs to something else).
- Count eBay titles (80 characters maximum). The AI's count can be wrong, so trust eBay's counter.
- Answer every [CHECK: ...] before you list.

### W3 Turn messy notes for 10 items into Listing Briefs in one go

**Use it when:** you have scribbled quick notes for a stack of items and want them turned into proper briefs before you start W2.

**Paste this:**
```text
Write in UK English.

Below are my rough notes for [NUMBER] items, one line or short paragraph each. Turn each one into a Listing Brief using exactly these headings:
Item number | What it is | Brand | Size | Measurements | Colour | Material | Condition and flaws | What's included | Platform | Postage | Price floor

Rules:
- Only use what is in my notes. Do not guess brand, size, material, age or value.
- Where a heading has no information, write [CHECK: ...] with a short prompt of what I need to find (for example [CHECK: look at care label for material]).
- If a note mentions a flaw, it must appear in "Condition and flaws".
- Keep each brief to one short block so I can check it on my phone.
- At the end, give me one combined "to find" list grouped by item, so I can check everything in one trip to the pile.

My notes:
[PASTE YOUR NOTES]
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented notes: "1. m&s cardi cream 14 bobbly cuffs. 2. hunter wellies green uk5 scuffed toe. 3. le creuset mug blue chip base"

> **Item 1** | Cardigan | M&S | 14 | [CHECK: pit to pit and length in cm] | Cream | [CHECK: care label] | Bobbling on cuffs | Cardigan only | [CHECK: platform] | [CHECK: postage] | [CHECK: price floor]
> **Item 2** | Wellington boots | Hunter | UK 5 | [CHECK: insole length] | Green | [CHECK: material, look inside the boot] | Scuffs on toe | Boots only | [CHECK] | [CHECK] | [CHECK]
> **Item 3** | Mug | Le Creuset | [CHECK: capacity] | [CHECK: height] | Blue | [CHECK: material, look on the base] | Chip on base | Mug only | [CHECK] | [CHECK] | [CHECK]
>
> **To find:** Item 1: measurements, material. Item 2: insole length, material. Item 3: capacity, height, material. All: platform, postage, price floor.

**Check before you post:**
- Check the brand label on every item before you trust the brief. Never list an item as a brand you cannot confirm.
- Measure while the pile is out, then fill in all the [CHECK: ...] gaps in one go.

### W4 Batch output as a table or CSV for bulk upload tools

**Use it when:** you have finished a batch and want it in a spreadsheet, or in a CSV to import into a bulk listing or cross-listing tool.

**Paste this:**
```text
Take all the items we have written in this chat and put them into one table.

Columns (in this order): [PASTE THE COLUMN HEADERS YOUR TOOL OR SPREADSHEET USES, OR USE: SKU, Title, Description, Condition, Brand, Size, Colour, Material, Category, Price, Quantity, Postage]

Rules:
- One row per item. Use the exact facts already agreed in this chat. Do not add or change anything.
- Leave a cell as [CHECK] if the fact is missing. Do not guess.
- If I ask for CSV: output plain CSV in a single code block, comma-separated, with every cell wrapped in double quotes. Replace any line breaks inside descriptions with a space. Replace any double quote inside a cell with two double quotes.
- Use UK English and £ with no currency symbol in the Price column (numbers only, for example 12.50).
- After the table or CSV, list any rows that still contain [CHECK].

Output format: [TABLE / CSV]
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example, CSV of two items:

```text
"SKU","Title","Description","Condition","Brand","Size","Colour","Material","Category","Price","Quantity","Postage"
"WL-014","Joules Womens Navy Quilted Gilet Size 12 Zip Front Pockets Pre-Owned","Navy quilted gilet from Joules in women's size 12. Full zip front and two zip pockets. Light pilling on the inside collar (see photos).","Pre-owned, good","Joules","12","Blue","[CHECK]","Coats and jackets","18.00","1","Royal Mail 2nd Class"
"WL-015","Hunter Wellington Boots Green UK 5","Green Hunter wellington boots in UK 5. Scuffs on the toes (see photos).","Pre-owned, good","Hunter","5","Green","[CHECK]","Boots","25.00","1","[CHECK]"
```

Rows with [CHECK]: WL-014 (Material), WL-015 (Material, Postage).

**Check before you post:**
- Every bulk tool has its own template. Download your tool's template and use its exact column headers, then paste those into the prompt.
- Open the CSV in a spreadsheet app and look at it before you upload. Check that long descriptions have not split across columns.
- Do a test upload of one or two rows first.
- Remember the price in the table is your decision. The AI only copies what you told it.

### W5 Batch accuracy check before you post

**Use it when:** you have a batch of listings ready and want a second pair of eyes to spot invented details, missing flaws and rule problems.

**Paste this:**
```text
Act as a careful UK listings checker. Write in UK English.

Below are my Listing Briefs (the facts) and then the listings written from them. For each item, compare the listing with its brief and report:
1. Any detail in the listing that is NOT in the brief (possible invention).
2. Any flaw in the brief that is missing or softened in the listing.
3. Title length problems (eBay UK: over 80 characters; other platforms: [CHECK: the platform's current limit]).
4. Hype words, keyword stuffing (the same word repeated, lists of unrelated brands), misleading claims, health claims, fake urgency or other platform names.
5. American spellings or US sizes where a UK one is needed.

Give one short row per item: Item number | Problems found | Suggested fix. If an item is fine, write "No problems found".
Do not rewrite whole listings unless I ask.

BRIEFS:
[PASTE BRIEFS]

LISTINGS:
[PASTE LISTINGS]
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example:

| Item | Problems found | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Listing says "100% wool" but brief says "wool blend" | Change to "wool blend" |
| 2 | Brief lists scuffed toes, listing does not mention them | Add "Scuffs on the toes (see photos)" to condition |
| 3 | Title is 86 characters | Remove "Great" and "Look" to get under 80 |
| 4 | "Color" (US spelling) | Change to "colour" |
| 5 | No problems found | None |

**Check before you post:**
- The checker can miss things too. Always read the flaws section yourself.
- If the checker flags an invention, go back to the item, not the AI, for the truth.

### W6 Weekly listing routine planner

**Use it when:** you want a realistic weekly plan that fits your evenings and weekends, so listing does not pile up.

**Paste this:**
```text
Help me plan a weekly listing routine. Write in UK English. Be realistic, not motivational.

My situation:
- Time I have: [E.G. MONDAY AND WEDNESDAY EVENINGS 1 HOUR, SATURDAY MORNING 2 HOURS]
- Roughly how many items I want to list each week: [NUMBER]
- Platforms: [LIST]
- Where I get stock: [E.G. OWN WARDROBE, CAR BOOTS, CHARITY SHOPS, MY OWN HANDMADE ITEMS]
- What slows me down most: [E.G. PHOTOS, MEASURING, WRITING, POSTING PARCELS]
- Post office or drop-off times near me: [E.G. LOCKER AVAILABLE 24 HOURS]

Give me:
1. A day-by-day plan in a table: Day, Time, Task, Roughly how many items, Tools or prompts to use (refer to prompt IDs like W2, W4, R prompts for pricing, M prompts for messages).
2. Batch tasks together (all photos in one go, all measuring in one go, all writing in one AI session).
3. A 10-minute daily check for messages and dispatch.
4. A weekly 20-minute review: what sold, what has had no interest for a while, what to refresh or reprice.
Keep it simple enough to follow on a phone. Do not promise any sales results.
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example: two weekday evenings and Saturday morning, aiming for about 15 items a week on Vinted and eBay.

| Day | Time | Task | Items | Prompts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Saturday | 9:00 to 10:00 | Photograph and measure the week's items in daylight | 15 | P prompts, shot lists |
| Saturday | 10:00 to 11:00 | Notes into briefs, then list the first 5 | 5 | W3, W2 |
| Monday | 19:30 to 20:30 | List the next 5, run the batch check | 5 | W2, W5 |
| Wednesday | 19:30 to 20:30 | List the last 5, weekly review | 5 | W2, W8, R prompts |
| Every day | 10 minutes | Reply to messages, pack and post sold items | | M prompts |

Weekly review questions: What sold? Which listings have had few views or likes for 2 weeks or more? Which items need better photos, a clearer title or a price check?

**Check before you post:**
- Dispatch times you promise in listings must match the plan you can actually keep.
- Keep a simple stock and pricing tracker (see templates/stock-and-pricing-tracker.csv) so the review takes minutes, not an hour.
- If you are selling regularly for profit, read HMRC's guidance on trading income and the platforms' reporting rules. Check the current rules on GOV.UK.

### W7 Repurpose one listing across platforms

**Use it when:** you have written a good listing for one platform and want versions for others without starting again.

**Paste this:**
```text
[PASTE YOUR SHOP STYLE INSTRUCTION FROM W1, IF YOU HAVE ONE]

Here is my Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

Here is the listing I already wrote for [PLATFORM]:
[PASTE THE LISTING]

Rewrite it for: [LIST TARGET PLATFORMS, E.G. VINTED, DEPOP, ETSY]

For each platform, give me what that platform needs:
- eBay UK: title of 80 characters or fewer, description, item specifics list.
- Vinted: short plain title, description with measurements and condition.
- Depop: description that starts with the key details, plus up to 5 relevant hashtags.
- Etsy: title (plain and readable, most important words first), description, and up to 13 tags of 20 characters or fewer. Only for handmade, vintage (20 years or older) or craft supplies items.
- Amazon UK: title of 75 characters or fewer for most categories (Amazon's rule from 27 July 2026; media categories differ), an Item Highlights line of 125 characters or fewer, no banned symbols (! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ unless part of the brand), no word more than twice, up to 5 bullet points, description.
- TikTok Shop UK: title with no symbols, promo words or platform names, and a short description of at least 30 words.

Rules:
- Only use facts from the brief. The facts must be the same on every platform. Change the format and tone, not the facts.
- Write [CHECK: ...] for anything a platform needs that is not in the brief.
- Do not mention any other platform inside a listing, and never suggest buying or paying elsewhere.
- No hype, no keyword stuffing, no misleading or health claims.
- Tell me if the item looks unsuitable for a platform (for example, not handmade or vintage for Etsy, or a used item for a new-only listing) instead of forcing it.
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example: vintage 1990s Levi's denim jacket, size M, mid-wash, small fade at the left cuff. Originally written for eBay UK.

> **Vinted**
> Title: Vintage 90s Levi's denim jacket, size M, mid-wash
> Description: Vintage Levi's trucker-style denim jacket in a mid-wash, size M. Small fade at the left cuff (see photos). Pit to pit 56 cm, length 62 cm, laid flat.
>
> **Depop**
> Vintage 90s Levi's denim jacket, size M. Mid-wash, trucker style. Small fade at the left cuff (see photos). Pit to pit 56 cm, length 62 cm.
> #vintagelevis #denimjacket #90svintage #levisjacket #truckerjacket
>
> **Etsy**
> Title: Vintage 1990s Levi's Denim Jacket Size M Mid Wash Trucker Jacket
> Tags: vintage levis, denim jacket, 90s denim, trucker jacket, levis jacket, mid wash denim, mens denim jacket, 1990s jacket, vintage jacket, blue jean jacket, retro denim, size m jacket, levis trucker
> Note: [CHECK: confirm the decade from the care label or tab so the "vintage" claim is accurate. Etsy's vintage category is for items at least 20 years old; check the current rule in Etsy's help pages.]

**Check before you post:**
- Cross-listing the same single item? Remove it from every other platform as soon as it sells, or you may sell it twice.
- Check each platform's current limits in module 12 (the cheatsheet) and in the platform's help pages.
- Only call something "vintage" if you can back up its age.

### W8 Refresh a batch of quiet listings

**Use it when:** some listings have had little interest for a couple of weeks and you want to improve them honestly, without keyword stuffing or fake price tricks.

**Paste this:**
```text
Write in UK English.

Below are [NUMBER] listings that have had little interest. For each one I have added the Listing Brief, the current title and description, how long it has been listed, and any views, likes or watchers I can see.

[FOR EACH ITEM PASTE: BRIEF / CURRENT TITLE / CURRENT DESCRIPTION / DAYS LISTED / VIEWS, LIKES OR WATCHERS]

For each item, suggest:
1. A clearer title (using words a buyer would actually search for, most important first, within the platform's limit).
2. The top 1 or 2 fixes to the description (missing measurements, unclear condition, missing brand or size).
3. Photo fixes to try (for example daylight, plain background, label close-up, flaw close-up).
4. Whether a price check is worth doing, and what sold listings I should look up myself to compare (describe the search, do not invent prices).

Rules: only use facts from the brief; [CHECK: ...] for missing facts; no keyword stuffing, no "rare" or "vintage" unless the brief supports it, no fake discounts, no misleading claims. Do not promise it will sell.
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example: "Blue top size 10", listed 21 days, 14 views, 0 likes. Brief says: Whistles, silk-blend blouse, cornflower blue, UK 10, no flaws.

> **Item 1**
> New title: Whistles Silk Blend Blouse Cornflower Blue UK 10
> Description fixes: add the brand and fabric in the first line. Add pit to pit and length [CHECK: measure].
> Photo fixes: a daylight flat lay on a plain background, plus a close-up of the Whistles label and the fabric content label.
> Price check: search sold listings for "Whistles silk blouse size 10" on the same platform and compare items in similar condition. Note 3 to 5 sold prices in your tracker before deciding.

**Check before you post:**
- Do not relist the same item many times to game search. Check the platform's rules on duplicate listings.
- A price drop should be real. Never raise a price just to "drop" it later.
