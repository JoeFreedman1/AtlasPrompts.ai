# Module 02: Titles

The title is what search matches, and on a phone it is often the only text a buyer reads before tapping. A good title is not clever. It is the words a buyer would type, in the order they would type them, with nothing untrue and nothing wasted.

Every prompt here starts from your Listing Brief (Module 01).

## Title limits at a glance

We checked these in September 2026. Limits change, so **check the current rule in the platform's help pages**. Module 12 has the sources.

| Platform | Title limit | Worth knowing |
|---|---|---|
| eBay UK | 80 characters | Front-load brand and item type. No unrelated brand names |
| Vinted | No confirmed limit. Check the listing form | Brand, size, colour and condition have their own fields, which power the filters |
| Depop | No separate title: the first line of the description acts as one | Description commonly quoted as 1,000 characters |
| Etsy | 140 characters | A readable phrase first, not a keyword list |
| Amazon UK | 75 characters in all categories except media, from 27 July 2026 | No ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ unless in the brand name. No word more than twice. New 125-character Item Highlights field |
| TikTok Shop UK | At least 15 characters. Maximum commonly quoted as 255 | No promotions, shop name, web address, platform names or symbols |

Spaces and punctuation count as characters everywhere.

## Title formulas

Use these as a starting order. Swap it if your buyers search differently (collectors often search set number first).

| Platform and category | Formula |
|---|---|
| eBay: clothing | Brand + Item type + Style + Colour + Material + Size |
| eBay: shoes | Brand + Model + Shoe type + Colour + UK size |
| eBay: electronics | Brand + Model name + Model number + Capacity + Colour + (Unlocked / Boxed) |
| eBay: books | Title + Author + Format + (Edition, only if confirmed) + Year of this copy |
| eBay: homeware and toys | Maker + Range, name or set number + Item type + Colour or size + (Complete / Boxed) |
| Vinted and Depop | Brand + Item type + Colour + Size + (one true style word, e.g. "oversized") |
| Etsy | What it is in plain words + Main material + Key feature + (Personalisation). Vintage: era only if you can support it |
| Amazon and TikTok Shop | Brand + Product type + Key feature or material + Size or quantity + Colour |

**Mistakes that cost you:** hype ("Stunning", "L@@K"), unsupported claims ("Rare", "Vintage", "Brand new" when it is not), other brands ("Zara style", "not Nike"), US words ("pants", "sweater", "sneakers", "purse"), bare shoe sizes ("8" instead of "UK 8"), and repeated words ("Dress Midi Dress Summer Dress"). Flaws belong in the description, not the title.

## The T prompts

### T1 eBay UK title
**Use it when:** You are listing on eBay UK and want a title that uses the 80 characters well without padding.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write 3 eBay UK titles for the item in my Listing Brief below.

- Put the words a UK buyer would search first: brand, item type, then the most useful details (model, style, colour, material, size).
- 80 characters or fewer including spaces. Show the count after each.
- Use the space for true, searchable details from the brief. If you run out of true details, stop. Do not pad.

Rules:
- UK English and UK sizes ("UK 8" for shoes, "trousers" not "pants").
- Use ONLY facts in my brief. If a key search word is missing (e.g. model number), write [CHECK: model number] and tell me.
- No hype words, exclamation marks, emoji or whole words in capitals.
- No rare, vintage, genuine, new or limited edition unless the brief supports it. No other brand names. No repeated words.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a brief that says: Next, women's jumper, crew neck, grey, 60% acrylic and 40% wool, size 12, light bobbling under both arms:

> 1. Next Womens Grey Wool Blend Crew Neck Jumper Size 12 (51)
> 2. Next Grey Crew Neck Jumper Womens Size 12 Acrylic Wool Blend (61)
> 3. Next Womens Jumper Grey Crew Neck Wool Blend UK Size 12 (55)

Every word is in the brief. It does not reach 80 characters because there are no more true details to add, and that is fine. The bobbling goes in the description.

**Check before you post:**
- Paste the title into eBay's title box. AI tools often miscount.
- If a word like "Long Sleeve" is true and buyers search it, add it to the brief first, then to the title.

### T2 Vinted title
**Use it when:** You are listing clothes, shoes, accessories or homeware on Vinted.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write 3 Vinted titles for the item in my Listing Brief below.

- Short and clear: brand, item type, colour, size, plus one style word buyers use if it is true (e.g. oversized, cropped, wide leg).
- 60 characters or fewer, so it reads well in the app. Show the count after each.

Rules:
- UK English and UK sizes.
- Use ONLY facts in my brief. Write [CHECK: ...] if something important is missing.
- No hype words, emoji, exclamation marks, or price words ("bundle deal").
- No rare, vintage, genuine or new unless the brief supports it. No other brand names.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For black Levi's 501 jeans, W30 L32, straight leg, button fly (checked), faded at the knees:

> 1. Levi's 501 Black Straight Leg Jeans W30 L32 (43)
> 2. Levi's 501 Jeans Black W30 L32 Button Fly (41)

**Check before you post:**
- Fill in Vinted's brand, size, colour and condition fields too. They power the filters.

### T3 Depop opening line (acts as the title)
**Use it when:** You are listing on Depop, where the first words of the description do the job of a title.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write 3 options for the opening line of a Depop listing for the item in my Listing Brief below. It works as the title, so it must say what the item is straight away.

- Brand and item type first, then colour, size and one or two style words buyers search (e.g. y2k, 90s, grunge) ONLY if they honestly fit my brief.
- One line each, under about 60 characters. Show the count. No hashtags in this line.

Rules:
- UK English and UK sizes.
- Use ONLY facts in my brief. Write [CHECK: ...] for missing key facts.
- An era word (90s, y2k) must be backed by the brief. If not, use "90s style" or leave it out, and tell me.
- No hype, emoji or exclamation marks. No other brand names.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a brown suede Topshop jacket, size 10, button front, boxy fit, tag style suggests late 1990s (not confirmed):

> 1. Topshop brown suede jacket, size 10, 90s style (46)
> 2. Brown suede Topshop jacket UK 10, button front (46)
> 3. Topshop suede jacket in brown, size 10, boxy fit (48)

"90s style", not "90s", because the era is not confirmed.

**Check before you post:**
- The opening line matches what is in the photos.

### T4 Etsy title
**Use it when:** You are listing a handmade, vintage or craft supply item on Etsy.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write 3 Etsy titles for the item in my Listing Brief below.

- Start with a clear phrase saying what the item is, as a person would say it. Then the key details: main material, key feature, size, colour, personalisation if offered.
- Under 140 characters including spaces, ideally under 15 words. Show the character and word count.
- Each phrase adds a new detail. Do not repeat the same idea in different words.

Rules:
- UK English (personalised, colour, jewellery).
- Use ONLY facts in my brief. Write [CHECK: ...] for anything missing.
- No hype, no "best", no "perfect gift" unless the brief says who it is for.
- "Vintage" only if the brief says at least 20 years old (check Etsy's current rule).
- No health or wellbeing claims. No other brands or trademarked characters.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a handmade sterling silver stacking ring, hammered finish, thin band, UK sizes J to Q:

> 1. Hammered Sterling Silver Stacking Ring, Handmade Thin Band, UK Sizes J to Q (75 characters, 13 words)
> 2. Sterling Silver Stacking Ring with Hammered Finish, Handmade Thin Band (70 characters, 10 words)

**Check before you post:**
- "Sterling silver" is a legal description. Use it only for 925 silver, and check whether your item needs hallmarking in the UK.
- Put extra search words in the 13 tags (K2), not the title.

### T5 Amazon UK title and Item Highlights
**Use it when:** You are a brand owner or seller of new stock creating an Amazon UK product listing. Skip this if you only sell second-hand.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write an Amazon UK product title and an Item Highlights line for the product in my Listing Brief below.

Title:
- Brand, product type, key feature, size or quantity, colour (unless my category style guide says otherwise).
- 75 characters or fewer including spaces (the rule for all categories except media from 27 July 2026). Show the count.
- Capitalise each word except short ones (and, or, for, the, with). Numbers as digits.

Item Highlights: up to 125 characters of useful details that did not fit in the title, no repeats. Show the count.

Rules:
- UK English. Use ONLY facts in my brief. Write [CHECK: ...] for anything missing.
- No ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ unless in the brand name. No emoji. No word more than twice (small words excepted).
- No promotional or subjective words (best, top quality, free delivery, sale).
- No health, medical or eco claims unless the brief gives evidence. No other brands.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For an invented "Hartwell Home" set of 4 linen napkins, 45 cm square, sage green, 100% linen, machine washable at 40 degrees, hemmed edges:

> **Title:** Hartwell Home Linen Napkins Set of 4, 45 x 45 cm, Sage Green (60)
> **Item Highlights:** 100% linen, machine washable at 40 degrees, hemmed edges (54)

**Check before you post:**
- Check your category's style guide in Seller Central. Title rules differ by category.

### T6 TikTok Shop UK product title
**Use it when:** You are listing a product in TikTok Shop UK. Skip this if you only sell second-hand on the resale apps.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write 3 TikTok Shop UK product titles for the product in my Listing Brief below.

- Brand, product type, material, the one or two features that matter most, then size, colour or quantity.
- Clear at a glance on a phone. At least 15 characters, aim for 60 to 100. Show the count. I will check the current maximum in Seller Center.

Rules:
- UK English. Use ONLY facts in my brief. Write [CHECK: ...] for anything missing.
- No hype or trend words (viral, best seller, must have), emoji, symbols, exclamation marks or whole words in capitals.
- Do not mention TikTok or any other platform, my shop name or a web address.
- No price, discount or urgency words (sale, 20% off, only 2 left).
- No claims that it cures, treats, prevents or relieves anything, and no weight loss claims.
- Use the brand only if it is mine or I am authorised to sell it. No other brands.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For an invented "Brookfield" stainless steel insulated water bottle, 750 ml, double wall, matte black:

> 1. Brookfield 750ml Insulated Water Bottle, Double Wall Stainless Steel, Black (75)
> 2. Brookfield Water Bottle 750ml, Stainless Steel, Insulated, Matte Black Finish (77)

**Check before you post:**
- Claims like "leakproof" or "keeps drinks cold for 24 hours" must be in your product spec and tested. Leave them out if not.

### T7 Title tester: which version is clearest?
**Use it when:** You have 2 to 5 title ideas and want an honest, buyer's-eye comparison.
**Paste this:**
```text
I am choosing a title for a [PLATFORM] UK listing. Here are my options and my Listing Brief.

For each title:
1. Count the characters including spaces. Say if it is over [LIMIT].
2. Give 3 short searches a UK buyer would likely type for this item, and say which ones each title matches.
3. Flag anything not in the brief, any hype, repeated words, or US spellings or sizes.
4. Say whether the first 40 characters (roughly what shows on a phone) make it clear what the item is.

Then recommend one in two lines. If you suggest a better version, use only facts from the brief. UK English.

Titles:
[PASTE TITLE 1]
[PASTE TITLE 2]
[PASTE TITLE 3]

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For three eBay titles for a Nintendo Switch Lite:

> | Title | Count | Matches "switch lite grey" | First 40 clear? | Issues |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | 1 | 78 | Yes | Yes | None |
> | 2 | 84 | Yes | Yes | Over 80 |
> | 3 | 61 | Yes | No, starts "Amazing condition" | Hype, wasted space |
>
> **Recommendation:** Title 1. Brand and model first, within the limit.

**Check before you post:**
- The platform's own search suggestions (start typing in the search bar) are a better guide than the AI's guesses. After posting, search as a buyer would and change one thing at a time.

### T8 Title fixer: too long, too vague or too spammy
**Use it when:** You already have a title (from an old listing or a generic AI tool) and it needs fixing.
**Paste this:**
```text
Fix this [PLATFORM] UK listing title. The limit is [LIMIT] characters.

My current title:
[PASTE TITLE]

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]

1. List what is wrong: over the limit, hype, repeated words, US spellings or sizes, unsupported claims, other brand names, wasted words.
2. Write 2 fixed versions within the limit, with character counts.
3. Use only facts from the brief. If the old title claims something the brief does not support, remove it and tell me what and why.

UK English. No hype, emoji or exclamation marks.
```
**What good output looks like:**

For "STUNNING!!! Vintage RARE Mulberry Style Leather Purse Bag Bag Brown L@@K" on an unbranded brown leather shoulder bag with a top zip and two handles:

> **Wrong:** capitals and exclamation marks, "Stunning" and "L@@K" are hype, "Vintage" and "Rare" are not in the brief, "Mulberry Style" names another brand, "Bag" is repeated, "Purse" is a US word here.
> **Fixed:** Brown Leather Shoulder Handbag, Top Zip, Two Handles, Unbranded (64)

**Check before you post:**
- If the item has no brand label, do not guess one. "Unbranded" is honest.
- Update the description so it tells the same story as the new title.
