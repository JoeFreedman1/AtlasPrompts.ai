# Module 00: Start here

## Quick start: your first listing in 5 minutes

You do not need to read this guide first. Pick one item, like a jumper, and do this:

1. **Minute 1.** Open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot on your phone (free versions are fine). Start a new chat.
2. **Minutes 2 and 3.** Copy the all-in-one prompt for your platform (A1 Vinted is below, A2 to A6 follow). Fill in the [BRACKETS] with the item in front of you: read the labels, measure it flat, and write each flaw as where, what and how big. If you do not know something, write "not sure".
3. **Minute 4.** Send it. Anything missing comes back as [CHECK: ...]. Find the fact or delete the line.
4. **Minute 5.** Read the result next to the item. If every word is true, paste it into the app and post.

That is the whole method: **facts in, no guessing, you check before you post.** The rest of the kit goes deeper when you want more control.

### A1 All-in-one Vinted listing
**Use it when:** You want a Vinted title, description and field values in one go.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write a Vinted listing in UK English using ONLY these facts.

What it is: [E.G. JUMPER]
Brand: [BRAND OR "UNBRANDED"]
Size on label: [SIZE]
Measurements, laid flat (cm): [E.G. PIT TO PIT 51, LENGTH 63]
Colour: [COLOUR]
Material (from the label): [MATERIAL OR "NOT SURE"]
Condition: [NEW WITH TAGS / NEW WITHOUT TAGS / VERY GOOD / GOOD / SATISFACTORY]
Flaws (where, what, how big): [FLAWS OR "NONE FOUND"]
Anything else true: [E.G. "SMOKE-FREE HOME" OR "NONE"]

Give me:
1. A title of 60 characters or fewer: brand, item, colour, size. No padding.
2. A description of 50 to 120 words: what it is, size and measurements, condition with every flaw, material. End with one line inviting questions.
3. Values for Vinted's fields: category, brand, size, condition, colour, material.

Rules:
- If a fact is missing, write [CHECK: what is missing]. Never guess.
- Keep every flaw exactly as serious as I wrote it.
- No hype words, emoji or exclamation marks.
- Do not say rare, vintage, genuine or new unless I did. No other brand names.
- No payment, returns or contact details.
```
**What good output looks like:**

> **Title:** Next grey wool-blend jumper, size 12 (36)
> **Description:** Grey wool-blend jumper from Next, size 12. Measured flat: pit to pit 51 cm, length 63 cm, sleeve 58 cm. Good condition: light bobbling under both arms, no holes or marks that I can see. 60% acrylic, 40% wool. Happy to answer questions or send more photos.
> **Fields:** Category: [CHECK: women's or men's? Then pick the closest jumper category you see]. Brand: Next. Size: 12. Condition: Good. Colour: Grey. Material: Acrylic, Wool.

**Check before you post:**
- Tap every field in the app yourself. Filters use the fields, not the text.
- AI tools miscount characters. Trust the counter in the app.

Included / not included: [LIST]

Give me:
1. Two titles, 80 characters or fewer including spaces, with the count. Brand and item type first, then the details a buyer would search. Only true words, no padding.
2. Item specifics as a two-column table. Leave any I have not given as [CHECK].
3. A description under 200 words: what it is, measurements, condition with every flaw on its own line, what is included, postage.

Rules:
- Missing facts become [CHECK: what is missing]. Never guess.
- Keep every flaw. Do not soften any.

**Check before you post:**
- Paste the title into eBay's title box and read its counter.
- Choose eBay's condition option that matches your flaws, reading eBay's own definition.

### A4 All-in-one Etsy listing
**Use it when:** You make or sell handmade, vintage or craft supply items and want a title, tags and description in one go.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write an Etsy listing in UK English using ONLY these facts.

What it is: [ITEM]
Handmade, vintage or craft supply: [TYPE]
Materials (all of them): [MATERIALS]
Size and weight: [DIMENSIONS]
Colours or options: [OPTIONS]
Personalisation (if any): [WHAT CAN CHANGE, CHARACTER LIMITS, OR "NONE"]
Made to order or ready to post, and dispatch time: [DETAILS]
Care instructions: [CARE]
Safety notes: [E.G. "NOT A TOY" OR "NONE"]
Condition and flaws (vintage only): [DETAILS OR "NOT APPLICABLE"]

Give me:
1. Two titles under 140 characters, with the count. A clear, readable phrase first, not a keyword list.
2. 13 tags, each 20 characters or fewer including spaces, with counts, then all 13 on one comma-separated line.
3. A description: two clear opening lines, then details, personalisation, dispatch, care and safety.

Rules:
- Missing facts become [CHECK: what is missing]. Never guess.
- Materials exactly as I wrote them. Do not upgrade "silver-plated" to "silver".
- No health or wellbeing claims (calming, healing, hypoallergenic). No hype words or exclamation marks.
- No other brands or characters. "Vintage" only if I gave an age of 20 years or more.
```
**Check before you post:**
- Count any tag close to 20 characters. Etsy will reject a longer one.
- If you used AI in making the item or its design, check Etsy's current rules on saying so.

**Use it when:** You are a brand owner or seller of new stock creating an Amazon UK product listing.
**Paste this:**
```text

Give me:

2. An Item Highlights line of 125 characters or fewer, with the count, adding details not in the title.
3. Up to 5 bullet points, each a short label and one or two plain sentences.
4. A product description of 100 to 200 words.
5. Backend search terms: lower case, spaces only, no words already used above, under 240 bytes.

Rules:
- Missing facts become [CHECK: what is missing]. Never guess.
- Title: no ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ unless in the brand name, and no word more than twice (small words excepted).

```
**Check before you post:**

## How every prompt works

Generic AI output goes wrong because it is given too little, so it fills the gaps with guesses: invented materials, wrong sizes, American spellings and "stunning vintage piece". Every prompt here fixes that the same way:

1. **Facts first.** You give it the facts, either in the A prompts' fill-in lines or in a **Listing Brief** (Module 01), which you fill in once and reuse for every platform.
2. **Only those facts.** The AI is told not to add, assume or improve anything.
3. **Gaps are marked [CHECK: ...]** instead of guessed, so you know exactly what to look up.
4. **UK English, UK sizes, the platform's limits, no hype, no misleading claims.**

Each prompt has an ID and name (the letter is the module: A all-in-one, B brief, T titles, D descriptions, K keywords, PH photos, R pricing, M messages, V video, C checks, W workflow), a "Use it when" line, the prompt to paste, usually an example of good output for an invented item, and a short "Check before you post" list. Replace everything in [SQUARE BRACKETS] before you send.

## Choosing a free AI tool

Every prompt works in the free versions of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot. No plugins, no paid features, no browsing needed. Free limits change often, so use the one you already have.

- **One chat per item, or per batch.** A new chat stops details leaking between items.
- **Paste the whole prompt.** The rules at the bottom keep the AI honest.
- **If it gets something wrong, say so:** "The size is 14, not 12. Rewrite using only my facts."
- **AI tools miscount characters.** Always check title length in the listing form.

**The platforms' own AI helpers** (eBay's app can draft a listing from a photo, Depop can generate a description from a photo, Amazon offers generative listing tools to UK sellers) can be useful. The same rule applies: check every word against the item. You can paste their draft into D10 (Module 03) to tidy it into UK English and remove anything unsupported.

## Privacy: items yes, people no

Treat AI chat tools like a public notice board.

- **Never paste:** buyers' names, usernames, addresses, phone numbers, emails, order or tracking numbers, screenshots of order pages, or your own bank details, passwords or home address.
- **Safe to paste:** facts about the item, sold prices you copied by hand, and a buyer's question with their details replaced by [BUYER].
- Check your AI tool's data settings. Many let you choose whether your chats are used to improve their models.

## The golden rules

1. **Only true words.** If you cannot back it up, leave it out. That includes "rare", "vintage", "genuine", "new" and "fully working".
2. **Every flaw: where, what, how big, which photo.** Between two condition grades, pick the lower one.
3. **Measurements over sizes** for clothing.
4. **UK English and UK sizes.** Jumper, trousers, trainers, handbag, postage.
5. **Only the item's own brand.** No "Zara style", no "not Nike".
6. **Keep it on the platform.** No off-platform payments, no contact details.
7. **Respect buyers' rights.** Never write anything that takes away a legal right or the platform's buyer protection.
8. **Rules change.** Check the current rule in the platform's help pages. Module 12 has the links.
9. **You are the editor.** The AI drafts. You check every word against the item before you post.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Reply to the AI |
|---|---|
| It invented a detail | "Remove anything not in my facts. Use [CHECK: ...] for gaps." |
| American spellings crept in | "Rewrite in UK English." |
| Title too long | "Cut to [LIMIT] characters, keeping brand and item type first." Then check in the listing form |
| Too gushing | "Plain and factual. No hype words, no exclamation marks." |
| It forgot the rules in a long chat | Start a new chat and paste the full prompt again |
