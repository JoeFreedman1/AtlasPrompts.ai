# Module 00: Start here

## Quick start: your first listing in 5 minutes

You do not need to read this guide first. Pick one item, like a jumper, and do this:

1. **Minute 1.** Open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot on your phone (free versions are fine). Start a new chat.
2. **Minutes 2 and 3.** Copy the all-in-one prompt for your platform (A01 Vinted is below, A02 to A06 follow). Fill in the [BRACKETS] with the item in front of you: read the labels, measure it flat, and write each flaw as where, what and how big. If you do not know something, write "not sure".
3. **Minute 4.** Send it. Anything missing comes back as [CHECK: ...]. Find the fact or delete the line.
4. **Minute 5.** Read the result next to the item. If every word is true, paste it into the app and post.

That is the whole method: **facts in, no guessing, you check before you post.** The rest of the kit goes deeper when you want more control.

### A01 All-in-one Vinted listing
**Use it when:** You want a Vinted title, description and field values in one go.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write a Vinted listing in UK English using ONLY these facts.

What it is: [E.G. JUMPER]
Brand: [BRAND OR "UNBRANDED"]
Size on label: [E.G. 12, OR KIDS: AGE 5 TO 6 YEARS, 116 CM]
Measurements, laid flat (cm): [E.G. PIT TO PIT 51, LENGTH 63]
Colour: [COLOUR]
Material (from the label): [MATERIAL OR "NOT SURE"]
Condition: [NEW WITH TAGS / NEW WITHOUT TAGS / VERY GOOD / GOOD / SATISFACTORY]
Flaws (where, what, how big): [FLAWS OR "NONE FOUND"]
What I checked and found fine: [E.G. "NO HOLES, ZIP WORKS" OR "NOTHING CHECKED"]
Anything else true: [E.G. "SMOKE-FREE HOME" OR "NONE"]

Give me:
1. A title of 60 characters or fewer: brand, item, colour, size. No padding.
2. A description of 50 to 120 words: what it is, size and measurements, condition with every flaw, material. End with one line inviting questions.
3. Values for Vinted's fields: category, brand, size, condition, colour, material.

Rules:
- If a fact is missing, write [CHECK: what is missing]. Never guess.
- Keep every flaw exactly as serious as I wrote it.
- No hype words, emoji or exclamation marks.
- Do not say rare, vintage, genuine or new unless I did. No other brand names.
- No payment, returns or contact details.
```
**What good output looks like** for a grey Next jumper, size 12, measured 51, 63 and 58 cm, 60% acrylic and 40% wool, Good, light bobbling under both arms, checked: no holes or marks:

> - **Title:** Next grey wool-blend jumper, size 12 (36)
> - **Description:** Grey wool-blend jumper from Next, size 12. Measured flat: pit to pit 51 cm, length 63 cm, sleeve 58 cm. Good condition: light bobbling under both arms. I checked it over and found no holes or marks. 60% acrylic, 40% wool. Happy to answer any questions or send more photos if useful.
> - **Fields:** Category: [CHECK: women's or men's? Then pick the closest jumper category you see]. Brand: Next. Size: 12. Condition: Good. Colour: Grey. Material: Acrylic, Wool.

**Check before you post:**
- Tap every field in the app yourself. Filters use the fields, not the text.
- Kids' clothes: crop out name tapes, names written on labels and school logos. For a bundle, use D09 instead.

### A04 All-in-one Etsy listing
**Use it when:** You make or sell handmade, vintage or craft supply items and want a title, tags and description in one go.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write an Etsy listing in UK English using ONLY these facts.

What it is: [ITEM]
Handmade, vintage or craft supply: [TYPE]
Who made it and how: [E.G. "I THROW AND GLAZE EACH ONE"]
AI used in the design or photos? [NO / YES, HOW]
Vintage only, age, proof and flaws: [E.G. "1970S, DATED BACKSTAMP, 2 MM RIM CHIP"]
Materials, all of them: [MATERIALS]
Size and weight: [DIMENSIONS]
Options and personalisation: [OPTIONS OR "NONE"]
Made to order or ready, dispatch time: [DETAILS]
Care: [CARE]
Safety text, copied exactly (see C11): [TEXT OR "NONE"]

Give me:
1. Two titles under 140 characters, with the count: readable phrases, not keyword lists.
2. 13 tags of 20 characters or fewer, with counts, then all 13 on one comma-separated line.
3. A description: two opening lines, then details, who made it, personalisation, dispatch, care, safety.

Rules:
- Missing facts become [CHECK: what is missing]. Never guess.
- Materials and safety text exactly as I wrote them (never "silver" for silver-plated).
- Hand words (handmade, hand-poured) only for steps I do by hand.
- If I used AI, one plain sentence saying how.
- No health claims (calming, healing, hypoallergenic), hype or exclamation marks.
- No other brands or characters. "Vintage" only for 20 years or more.
- No returns, refund or guarantee wording.
```
**Check before you post:**
- Count any tag close to 20 characters. Etsy will reject a longer one.
- Selling what you make usually makes you a trader. Never add "no returns" wording: check your policies with C10.

## How every prompt works

Generic AI output goes wrong because it is given too little, so it guesses: invented materials, wrong sizes, American spellings, "stunning vintage piece". Every prompt here works the same way:

1. **Facts first,** either in the A prompts' fill-in lines or in a **Listing Brief** (Module 01) that you fill in once and reuse everywhere.
2. **Only those facts.** Gaps come back as **[CHECK: ...]** instead of guesses.
3. **UK English, UK sizes, the platform's limits, no hype, no misleading claims.**

Each prompt has a two-digit ID, the same in this guide and in the library search (the letter is the module: A all-in-one, B brief, T titles, D descriptions, K keywords, PH photos, R pricing, M messages, V video, C checks, W workflow), a "Use it when" line, the prompt, usually an example for an invented item, and a short check list. Replace everything in [SQUARE BRACKETS] before you send.

**Which AI tool?** Every prompt works in the free versions of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot, with no plugins or browsing. Use the one you have. Start a new chat per item or batch, paste the whole prompt, and if it gets something wrong, say so: "The size is 14, not 12. Rewrite using only my facts." The platforms' own AI helpers (eBay and Depop can draft from a photo, Amazon offers listing tools to UK sellers) follow the same rule: check every word, or tidy their draft with D10.

**Privacy: items yes, people no.** Never paste buyers' names, usernames, addresses, phone numbers, emails, order numbers or screenshots of order pages, or your own bank details or home address. Replace a buyer's details with [BUYER]. Check your AI tool's data settings too.

The download also has **prompt-library.html** (every prompt, searchable, with copy buttons, works offline on your phone), **all-prompts.txt** and a **templates** folder (Listing Brief, stock and pricing tracker, buyer replies, photo shot lists).

## The golden rules

1. **Only true words.** If you cannot back it up, leave it out. That includes "rare", "vintage", "genuine", "new" and "fully working".
2. **Every flaw: where, what, how big, which photo.** Between two condition grades, pick the lower one.
3. **Measurements over sizes** for clothing.
4. **UK English and UK sizes.** Jumper, trousers, trainers, handbag, postage.
5. **Only the item's own brand.** No "Zara style", no "not Nike".
6. **Keep it on the platform.** No off-platform payments, no contact details.
7. **Respect buyers' rights.** Never write anything that takes away a legal right or the platform's buyer protection. Our summaries of UK law are practical guidance, not legal advice.
8. **Rules change.** Check the current rule in the platform's help pages. Module 12 has the links.
9. **You are the editor.** The AI drafts. You check every word against the item before you post.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Reply to the AI |
|---|---|
| It invented a detail | "Remove anything not in my facts. Use [CHECK: ...] for gaps." |
| American spellings crept in | "Rewrite in UK English." |
| Title too long | "Cut to [LIMIT] characters, keeping brand and item type first." Then check in the listing form |
| Too gushing | "Plain and factual. No hype words, no exclamation marks." |
| It forgot the rules in a long chat | Start a new chat and paste the full prompt again |
| The free version hit its limit | Try later, or switch to another free tool |
