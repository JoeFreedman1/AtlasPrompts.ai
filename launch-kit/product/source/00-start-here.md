# Start here

Welcome to **The Well Listed Kit**.

This kit helps UK sellers write clear, accurate listings, price items from real sold listings, and reply to buyers politely, using the free AI chat tools you probably already have on your phone.

It is not software and there is nothing to install. It is a set of tested prompts (instructions you copy and paste into an AI tool), fill-in templates and checklists. You bring the item and the facts. The prompts make the AI do the tidy, time-consuming writing, in UK English, without inventing anything.

We cannot promise you more sales, and nobody honestly can. What the kit does is help you write listings faster that are clear, complete and honest, which is exactly what buyers look for.

## Your first listing in 5 minutes

Pick one item you want to sell today. Something simple, like a jumper or a book.

**Minute 1: fill in a brief.** Open Module 01, copy the brief for your category and fill in what you know. Measure it if it is clothing. Write flaws plainly: where, what, how big. Leave blanks if you do not know.

**Minute 2: open your AI tool.** Start a new chat in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot (free versions are fine).

**Minute 3: paste the starter prompt below** with your brief.

**Minute 4: fix the [CHECK] notes.** The AI will mark anything missing with [CHECK: ...]. Find the fact (look at the label, measure, test it) or delete the line.

**Minute 5: read it next to the item and post.** Does every word match the item and the photos? If yes, copy it into the listing form.

### The 5-minute starter prompt

```text
You are helping a UK seller write one listing for [PLATFORM]. Using ONLY the facts in my Listing Brief below, write:

1. A title that fits [PLATFORM]'s current title limit (eBay UK: 80 characters; if unsure, keep it under 60) with the character count.
2. A short description: what it is, measurements or dimensions, condition with every flaw (where, what, how big), what is included, postage.
3. The values for the main fields the platform asks for (brand, size, colour, condition, material).

Rules:
- UK English (colour, jumper, trousers, postage, trainers).
- Use only facts in my brief. If something important is missing, write [CHECK: what is missing]. Never invent measurements, materials, dates or test results.
- Keep every flaw. Do not soften any.
- No hype words (stunning, amazing, must have), no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- No misleading claims: do not say rare, vintage, genuine, new or fully working unless my brief says so.
- No other brand names.
- Do not reveal my lowest price.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```

When you have done one, move on to the platform-specific prompts in Modules 02 to 04. They go further than the starter prompt.

## Choosing a free AI tool

Every prompt in this kit works in the free versions of the main AI chat tools. No plugins, no paid features, no internet browsing needed.

| Tool | Where to get it | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | App or website | Widely used. Free version has usage limits |
| Claude | App or website | Good at following detailed rules. Free version has usage limits |
| Gemini | App or website, and built into many Android phones | Handy if you already use Google apps |
| Copilot | App or website, and built into Windows and Edge | Handy if you use a Windows laptop |

Free versions and their limits change often, so do not worry too much about which one. Pick the one you already have, or try two and keep the one whose writing you prefer.

**Tips that apply to all of them:**
- **One chat per item, or per batch.** Starting a new chat stops details from one item leaking into another.
- **Paste the whole prompt.** The rules at the bottom are what keep the AI honest and in UK English.
- **If the answer is wrong, say so.** "The size is 14, not 12. Rewrite using only the brief." works well.
- **AI tools miscount characters.** Always check the title length in the listing form itself.
- **You are the editor.** The AI drafts. You check every fact before you post.

**What about the AI tools built into the platforms?** Some marketplaces now have their own AI listing helpers (eBay's app can draft a listing from a photo, Depop can generate a description from a photo, and Amazon offers generative listing tools to UK sellers). They can be useful. The same rule applies: check every word against the item. You can also paste their draft into our prompts (for example D10 in Module 03) to tidy it into UK English and remove anything unsupported.

## Privacy: what never goes into an AI tool

AI chat tools are not the place for other people's personal data. Treat them like a public notice board.

**Never paste:**
- Buyers' names, addresses, phone numbers or email addresses
- Order numbers, tracking numbers linked to a person, or screenshots of order pages
- A buyer's messages with their username showing (replace it with [BUYER])
- Your own bank details, passwords, account login codes or home address

**Safe to paste:**
- Facts about the item (your Listing Brief)
- Sold listing titles and prices you have looked up and copied by hand
- The text of a buyer's question, with their name and details removed

**Also worth doing:** check your AI tool's privacy or data settings. Many let you choose whether your chats are used to improve their models. Settings change, so look in the app's settings menu.

## The Listing Brief method

Generic AI output goes wrong for one reason: it is given too little to work with, so it fills the gaps with guesses. That is where invented materials, wrong sizes, American spellings and "stunning vintage piece" come from.

The Listing Brief fixes that. It is a short fill-in block of facts about one item: what it is, brand, size, measurements, colour, material, condition and flaws, what is included, platform, postage and your price.

Every prompt in this kit:
1. **Starts from your brief.** You fill it in once and reuse it for every platform and every prompt.
2. **Uses only facts from the brief.** The AI is told not to add, assume or improve anything.
3. **Marks gaps with [CHECK: ...].** Instead of guessing, the AI tells you exactly what to find out.
4. **Writes in UK English**, with UK sizes and UK words.
5. **Stays within the platform's limits**, and reminds you to check the current rules.
6. **Avoids hype and misleading claims.**

The brief takes about two minutes. It saves more than that on every listing, and it is also what protects you if a buyer ever says the item was not as described: your listing says exactly what it was.

## The golden rules

1. **Brief first.** No brief, no listing. Two minutes of facts saves twenty minutes of fixing.
2. **Only true words.** If you cannot back it up, it does not go in. That includes "rare", "vintage", "genuine", "new" and "fully working".
3. **Every flaw, with a size and a photo.** Where, what, how big, which photo. If you are between two condition grades, pick the lower one.
4. **Measurements over sizes** for clothing, especially vintage and pre-loved.
5. **UK English, UK sizes, UK words.** Jumper, trousers, trainers, handbag, postage.
6. **No hype.** No "stunning", no "must have", no capitals and exclamation marks.
7. **Only the item's own brand.** No "Zara style", no "not Nike", no tagging brands it is not.
8. **Respect the limits, and check them.** Platform rules change. Check the current rule in the platform's help pages, and see Module 12.
9. **Keep it on the platform.** No off-platform payments, no contact details in listings or messages.
10. **Respect buyers' rights.** Never write anything that tries to take away a buyer's legal rights or the platform's buyer protection.
11. **No personal data in AI tools.** Items, yes. People, no.
12. **You are the editor.** The AI drafts. You check every word against the item before you post.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The AI invented a detail | Reply: "Remove anything not in my brief. Use [CHECK: ...] for gaps." |
| American spellings crept in | Reply: "Rewrite in UK English." Or use D10 in Module 03 |
| Title is too long | Use T8 in Module 02. Then check the count in the listing form |
| The tone is too gushing | Reply: "Plain, factual, no hype words, no exclamation marks." |
| The AI forgot the rules halfway through a long chat | Start a new chat and paste the full prompt again |
| The free version has hit its limit | Try again later, or switch to another free tool. The prompts work in all of them |
