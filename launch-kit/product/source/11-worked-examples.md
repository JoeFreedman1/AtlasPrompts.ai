# Module 11: Worked examples

Six complete examples, start to finish: the messy notes most of us actually write, the Listing Brief, the title, the description, the tags or fields, the price reasoning and one buyer reply.

**Please read this first:**

- **Every item, shop and brand-owner here is invented.** Real brand names appear only to show how a genuine branded item would be described.
- **Every comp (comparable sold price) is invented.** They show the method, not real prices. Look up real sold listings yourself.
- **Fee figures** are the ones we checked in September 2026 (Module 12). Fees change: check the current rule in the platform's help pages.
- Nothing here predicts what you will sell or earn. The aim is a clear, accurate listing.

Examples 1, 2 and 4 are second-hand resale. Examples 3, 5 and 6 are for makers and brand owners.

## What all six have in common

1. **Facts first.** Every detail in the listing came from the brief. Guesses in the notes ("14??", "i think", "works fine", "RRP 17.99??") were checked or dropped.
2. **Flaws up front,** stated plainly with photo numbers.
3. **No claims we cannot prove:** no "best", "rare", "fully working", health wording or invented reference prices.
4. **Prices from like-for-like comps and real costs,** not a hunch.
5. **Replies stay polite, honest and on the platform.**
