# 11 Worked examples

Six complete examples, start to finish. Each one starts with the kind of messy notes most of us actually write, then shows the Listing Brief, the title, the description, the tags or item specifics, the price reasoning and one buyer reply.

**Please read this first:**

- **Every item, shop and brand-owner in this module is invented** for illustration. Real brand names appear only to show how a genuine branded item would be described.
- **Every "comp" (comparable sold price) is an invented example.** They show the method, not real market prices. Always look up real sold listings yourself on the platform you are using, for items in the same condition.
- **Fee figures** are the ones we checked in September 2026 (see module 12). Fees change. Check the current rule in the platform's help pages before you do your own sums.
- Nothing here predicts what you will sell or earn. The aim is a clear, accurate listing.

The prompts used are shown in brackets, so you can follow along: B (brief), T (titles), D (descriptions), K (keywords and specifics), R (pricing), M (messages).

Note: the notes said "14??" so we checked the label before writing the brief. It says 14. The notes guessed "8 quid", but the price came later, from comps.

### Title (T prompts)

> White Stuff oatmeal cable knit jumper, size 14, cotton wool

### Description (D prompts)

> Oatmeal cable-knit jumper from White Stuff, UK size 14. Crew neck, 60% cotton and 40% wool.
>
> Condition: very good. Light bobbling under both arms (see photo 5). No holes, marks or stretching.
>

>

### Tags and fields (K prompts)

Vinted uses fields rather than tags: Category: Women > Jumpers and sweaters. Brand: White Stuff. Size: UK 14. Condition: Very good. Colour: Beige. Material: Cotton, Wool.

### Price reasoning (R prompts)

**Invented example comps (for illustration only, not real prices):**

| Comp | Condition | Sold price |
|---|---|---|
| White Stuff cable jumper, size 12, cream | Very good | £14 |
| White Stuff cable jumper, size 14, grey, bobbling | Good | £11 |
| White Stuff jumper, size 16, oatmeal | Very good | £15 |
| White Stuff cable jumper, size 14, no flaws | Excellent | £18 |

Reasoning: the closest matches (same size or neighbouring sizes, similar condition) sold between £11 and £15. Ours has light bobbling, so it sits in the middle, not at the top. Vinted does not charge private sellers a selling fee; the buyer pays the Buyer Protection fee at checkout. List at **£14**, accept offers down to the **£10** floor. Our original "8 quid" guess would have left money on the table without being any quicker to sell.

### Buyer reply (M prompts)

### Price reasoning

This is our own product, so we price from costs first, then sense-check against the market.

**Costs (invented example figures):** clay and glaze £3.20, kiln share £2.00, packaging £1.40, time (1 hour at the rate we choose) £12.00. Total before fees: £18.60.

**Etsy fees (checked September 2026; check the current rules):** listing fee $0.20 (about £0.15), 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus postage, and a UK payment processing fee of 4% + £0.20. A UK Regulatory Operating Fee also applies [CHECK: current rate in Etsy's fee policy].

At £24 plus £4.50 postage (order total £28.50): transaction fee about £1.85, processing about £1.34, listing about £0.15. That is about £3.34 before the Regulatory Operating Fee. After the £4.50 postage covers the real postage cost, we keep about £20.66 against £18.60 of costs.

**Invented example comps (for illustration only, not real prices):** three similar handmade stoneware tealight pairs listed at £22, £26 and £28.

Reasoning: £24 covers our costs and fees with a small margin and sits within the range of similar handmade pieces. List at **£24 plus postage**.

### Buyer reply

>
> **Reply:** Hi, thanks for asking. They're made for standard tealights, so a pillar candle won't fit inside. The opening is about [CHECK: inner width] cm across. If you'd like something for pillar candles, let me know and I can tell you when I have some in the shop.

## What all six have in common

1. **Facts first.** Every detail in the listing came from the brief. Guesses in the notes ("14??", "i think", "RRP 17.99??") were checked or dropped.
2. **Flaws up front.** Bobbling, scratches, a loose stick and a mark on the back are all stated plainly, with photo numbers.
3. **No claims we cannot prove.** No "best", no "rare", no health wording, no invented reference prices.
4. **Price from like-for-like comps and real costs**, not from a hunch. Your own comps should come from sold listings you look up yourself.
5. **Replies stay polite, honest and on the platform.**
