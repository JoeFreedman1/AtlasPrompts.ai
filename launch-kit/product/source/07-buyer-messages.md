# Module 07: Buyer messages

Messages eat evenings: "Is this still available?", the £3 offer on a £20 coat, the parcel stuck in a depot. These prompts reply quickly and correctly on three rules: **polite, brief and factual** (the platform's support team may read the thread later), **stay on the platform**, and **respect the buyer's rights**, with no pressure about feedback.

For everyday replies you can copy without an AI tool, use **templates/message-snippets.txt** (22 ready replies). This is practical guidance, not legal advice: for a serious dispute, check the platform's help pages, gov.uk or Citizens Advice.

**Privacy:** before pasting a buyer's message into an AI tool, remove their name, username, address, phone, email and order number, and replace them with [BUYER] or [ORDER]. Add the name back in the app if you want to.

**Never move payment off the platform.** Bank transfer, PayPal "friends and family" or "message me on WhatsApp": the answer is no. It removes both sides' protection and it is how many scams start.

## Returns: private sellers and traders are different

**Which are you?** Clearing out your own things: usually a private seller. Buying items to resell for profit, or making items to sell: likely to be a trader in law, even on a personal account. Platform reporting to HMRC is a separate question. If unsure, read the guidance on gov.uk.

| | Private seller | Trader (business seller) |
|---|---|---|
| Item must match your description | Yes | Yes |
| Satisfactory quality and fit for purpose | Not in the same way, so describe faults honestly | Yes (Consumer Rights Act 2015) |
| Faulty item rejected for a refund | Only if it was not as described | Generally within 30 days (the short-term right to reject) |
| Online cancellation for any reason | No legal right | Generally 14 days from the day after delivery, then 14 days to send it back. Some exceptions, such as personalised items |
| Does "no returns" stop this? | It covers change of mind only | No. A trader cannot remove these rights |

**Platform protection sits on top of the law.** Even with "no returns", a buyer can usually ask the platform for help if an item does not arrive, arrives damaged or does not match the listing (eBay Money Back Guarantee, Vinted Buyer Protection and the equivalents on Depop, Etsy, Amazon and TikTok Shop). Time limits differ by platform and change: check the current rule in the platform's help pages. Vinted says traders should use Vinted Pro, whose sellers must accept returns within 14 days of receipt.

The practical upshot: **an honest listing with every flaw described and photographed is your best protection.**

### M1 Reply to any buyer message
**Use it when:** You have a message that does not fit the other prompts, or just want a quick, polite reply.
**Paste this:**
```text
Help me reply to a buyer on [PLATFORM]. I have removed their personal details.

I sell as a: [PRIVATE SELLER / BUSINESS]
Buyer's message:
[PASTE MESSAGE, WITH NAMES REPLACED BY [BUYER]]
What I want to say, rough is fine:
[E.G. "YES I CAN POST TOMORROW BUT NOT BEFORE"]

Write one reply.

Rules:
- UK English, friendly, under 60 words. No emoji unless I ask.
- Only facts from my brief or notes. If they ask something I have not answered, write [CHECK: ...].
- Keep everything on the platform. Do not promise delivery dates I have not given.
- If it is about returns, refunds or a problem with the item, say so and suggest the specific prompt.
- Do not mention feedback.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like,** for "Hi, is it from a smoke free home? Can you post Saturday?" with notes "yes smoke free, I post Mondays and Thursdays":

> "Hi, thanks for asking. Yes, it comes from a smoke-free home. I post on Mondays and Thursdays, so if you buy before Monday it will go out then."

### M2 "Is this still available?"
**Use it when:** You get the classic question and want a reply that answers it and moves things along.
**Paste this:**
```text
A buyer on [PLATFORM] asked if my item is still available. Write a short, friendly reply.

Still available? [YES / NO, SOLD / ON HOLD]
When I can post: [E.G. "NEXT WORKING DAY"]
Anything useful to add: [E.G. "HAPPY TO SEND MORE PHOTOS" OR "NONE"]

Rules: UK English, under 40 words, answer the question in the first sentence. If available, add one useful fact and an easy next step using the platform's tools (buy, make an offer). No fake urgency ("lots of interest") unless I say it is true. Only facts from my brief.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

> "Hi, yes it is still available. It is a UK 6 and I can post the next working day after purchase. Happy to send extra photos if useful."

**Check before you post:**
- It really is still available everywhere you cross-list.

### M3 Lowball offer
**Use it when:** Someone offers far below your price and you want to stay polite without giving the item away.
**Paste this:**
```text
A buyer on [PLATFORM] offered much less than my price. Help me reply.

List price: £[AMOUNT]
Their offer: £[AMOUNT]
What I want to do: [COUNTER AT £... / DECLINE POLITELY / ASK WHAT THEY HAD IN MIND]
A true reason for my price (optional): [E.G. "SOLD COMPS AROUND £25", "HEAVY TO POST"]

Write a friendly counter and a polite decline, each under 50 words.

Rules: UK English, warm and calm, never sarcastic or lecturing. Never below my floor. Only reasons I gave, no invented buyers or deadlines. Any counter goes through the platform's offer button.

My Listing Brief (includes my floor):
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like,** for a Le Creuset casserole, list £65, floor £48, offer £20:

> - **Counter:** "Thanks for the offer. £20 is a bit too low for me, as similar ones have sold for more. I could do £55, and I will send it through the offer button."
> - **Decline:** "Thanks for your interest, but I cannot go that low on this one. You are welcome to make another offer."

**Check before you post:**
- Your stated reason is true (you really looked at comps).

### M4 Bundle request
**Use it when:** A buyer wants several of your items and asks for a deal.
**Paste this:**
```text
A buyer on [PLATFORM] wants several of my items together. Help me reply with a bundle price.

The items (title, list price and floor for each):
[PASTE ITEMS HERE]
Postage as one parcel: £[AMOUNT OR "NOT SURE"]
Bundle price I am happy with (or work it out with R4 first): £[AMOUNT]
How the platform handles bundles: [E.G. "BUYER ADDS ITEMS TO A BUNDLE AND I SEND AN OFFER" / "NOT SURE"]

Write a reply under 60 words that confirms the items, gives the price and whether postage is included, and says how to buy with the platform's bundle or offer tool.

Rules: UK English, friendly, on the platform. Never below the total of my floors. If the process is "NOT SURE", write [CHECK: how bundles work on this app].
```
**What good output looks like:**

> "Hi, happy to do all three Frugi tops (stripe, dinosaur and navy) for £19 plus postage, all in one parcel. If you add them to a bundle, I will send you an offer for that price."

**Check before you post:**
- The combined parcel fits the postage size you quoted.

### M5 Postage questions
**Use it when:** A buyer asks about postage cost, speed, tracking, collection or sending abroad.
**Paste this:**
```text
A buyer on [PLATFORM] asked about postage. Help me reply in under 60 words.

Their question (personal details removed):
[PASTE QUESTION]

My postage facts:
- Service: [E.G. "TRACKED 48"]
- Cost to buyer: £[AMOUNT OR "SET BY THE APP"]
- How soon I post: [E.G. "NEXT WORKING DAY"]
- Post outside the UK? [YES / NO]
- Collection? [YES, AND HOW THE PLATFORM HANDLES IT / NO]

Rules: UK English. Only these facts; anything else becomes [CHECK: ...]. Never promise a delivery date: say when I post and the service's usual time ("usually", not "guaranteed"). Collection only if I said yes, with payment on the platform. Never suggest undervaluing or marking anything as a gift on customs forms.
```
**What good output looks like,** for "Could you send it first class? I need it for Friday.":

> "Hi, I post with Tracked 48 as standard, the next working day after purchase. I cannot promise Friday delivery, sorry. [CHECK: can you offer a faster service, and at what cost?]"

**Check before you post:**
- Any upgrade cost is added through the platform, not paid separately.

### M6 Delay: late dispatch or parcel stuck in transit
**Use it when:** You are going to post late, or tracking has stalled and the buyer is asking where their parcel is.
**Paste this:**
```text
Help me update a buyer on [PLATFORM] about a delay.

Type of delay: [I WILL POST LATE / STUCK IN TRANSIT / TRACKING SAYS DELIVERED BUT BUYER HAS NOT GOT IT]
Date of sale: [DATE]
Date posted: [DATE OR "NOT YET"]
Service, tracked or not: [E.G. "TRACKED 48, YES"]
What tracking shows now: [STATUS, NO TRACKING NUMBER NEEDED]
What I will do next: [E.G. "POST TOMORROW MORNING" / "OPEN A CARRIER ENQUIRY"]
Buyer's message (details removed): [PASTE OR "NONE, I AM MESSAGING FIRST"]

Write a reply under 80 words: sorry once, plainly; the facts and what I am doing next; and that their purchase is still covered by the platform's buyer protection (no specific time limits).

UK English. Do not blame the buyer. Do not promise a delivery date. No cancelling or refunding outside the platform. Missing facts become [CHECK: ...].
```
**What good output looks like:**

> "Hi, sorry for the wait. I posted it on Monday with Tracked 48 and the tracking has shown it at a depot since Tuesday. I have asked the carrier to look into it and will update you as soon as I hear. Your purchase is still covered by the app's buyer protection."

**Check before you post:**
- If you promised to follow up, set a reminder on your phone.

### M7 Return request
**Use it when:** A buyer asks to return an item, for any reason.
**Paste this:**
```text
A buyer on [PLATFORM] asked to return an item. Help me work out my position and reply.

I sell as a: [PRIVATE SELLER / TRADER]
My listing's return policy: [E.G. "NO RETURNS" / "30 DAY RETURNS"]
Platform rules I checked today (paste, or "NOT CHECKED"): [PASTE RULE TEXT]
Date delivered: [DATE]
Buyer's reason (details removed): [PASTE MESSAGE]

Part 1, my position, in short bullets:
- Is this change of mind, or a claim that it is faulty, damaged or not as described?
- If I am a trader: remind me of the 14-day right to cancel most online orders and the short-term right to reject faulty goods, which "no returns" cannot remove. Exceptions only as [CHECK: ...].
- If I am a private seller: change-of-mind returns are up to me, but "not as described", faulty or damaged claims may be covered by platform protection whatever my policy says.
- Platform rules only from the text I pasted. If I pasted nothing, tell me to check the help pages first.
- One line saying this is not legal advice.

Part 2: a polite reply under 80 words, using the platform's own return or refund process. Never tell the buyer they have no rights, argue about their reason, or refund off the platform.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like,** for a private seller on Vinted, no returns, and "It's lovely but doesn't fit, can I send it back?":

> **Part 1:** change of mind, not a fault, so accepting it is your choice. Not legal advice: check Vinted's current help pages.
>
> **Part 2:** "Hi, sorry it did not fit. As I sell privately I do not accept returns for fit, which is why I listed the measurements. If there is anything wrong with the item itself, please let me know and I will help."

**Check before you post:**
- You are clear whether you are a private seller or a trader. If the claim is about a fault or the description, use M8.

### M8 "Item not as described"
**Use it when:** A buyer says the item is different from the listing, faulty, or has a flaw you did not mention.
**Paste this:**
```text
A buyer on [PLATFORM] says my item is not as described. Help me respond fairly.

I sell as a: [PRIVATE SELLER / TRADER]
My original listing (title and description):
[PASTE LISTING TEXT]
What my photos showed: [E.G. "FLAW ON LEFT CUFF IN PHOTO 4"]
Buyer's complaint (details removed), and what their photos show:
[PASTE MESSAGE]

1. Compare the complaint with my listing, point by point: covered, not covered or unclear. Do not take my side automatically.
2. Suggest fair options (ask for photos, return for a full refund through the platform, partial refund through the platform, or let the platform decide) and say which fits best.
3. Write a calm reply under 90 words.

UK English. If the listing did not mention the problem, say so plainly. Never accuse the buyer of lying; if I suspect a swapped or damaged return, tell me to keep records and use the platform's process. Never refund off the platform or link a refund to feedback. Not legal advice.
```
**What good output looks like,** for the Next jumper: listing said "light bobbling under both arms", buyer says "there is a hole on the back":

> 1. **Bobbling:** covered. **Hole on the back:** not mentioned anywhere. Likely a fair claim.
> 3. "Hi, I am sorry about that. I did not spot a hole when I listed it, so thank you for telling me. I am happy to accept a return for a full refund through the app, or offer a partial refund if you would prefer to keep it. Just let me know which suits."

**Check before you post:**
- Any refund goes through the platform so both of you stay covered.

### M9 Damaged in the post
**Use it when:** The buyer says the item arrived broken, crushed or wet.
**Paste this:**
```text
A buyer on [PLATFORM] says the item arrived damaged. Help me handle it.

Item and how I packed it: [E.G. "GLASS VASE, BUBBLE WRAP, DOUBLE-WALLED BOX"]
Postage: [E.G. "PLATFORM LABEL" / "MY OWN TRACKED SERVICE"]
Buyer's message and what their photos show (details removed):
[PASTE MESSAGE]

Give me:
1. What to ask the buyer for (photos of item, packaging and label) and what to keep myself (proof of postage, packing photos).
2. Who likely handles the claim: the platform (e.g. if I used its label) or me with the carrier. If unsure, write [CHECK: ...] and tell me to check the help pages.
3. A calm reply under 80 words asking for photos and explaining the next step through the platform.

UK English. Do not blame the buyer or the carrier, refuse a refund, or settle off the platform. Not legal advice.
```
**What good output looks like,** for a Denby mug set in bubble wrap and a single-wall box, sent with the seller's own tracked service:

> 2. **Claim:** your own service, so you would usually claim from the carrier yourself while the platform handles the buyer's request. [CHECK: the carrier's claim rules and time limit.]
> 3. "Hi, I am really sorry it arrived damaged. Could you send photos of the mugs and of the box and packaging, including the outside? That helps me sort it out with the carrier. I will make sure it is put right through the app."

**Check before you post:**
- Check the carrier's claim time limit today, not next week.

### M10 Asking for feedback, without pressure
**Use it when:** A sale has gone smoothly and you want to send a thank-you that mentions feedback, fairly.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write a short thank-you message to a buyer on [PLATFORM] after delivery.

Item: [SHORT NAME]
Anything nice and true to mention: [E.G. "HOPE IT FITS WELL" OR "NONE"]
Mention feedback? [YES, ONE NEUTRAL LINE / NO]

Rules:
- UK English, under 40 words, no emoji unless I ask.
- Feedback, if mentioned, in one neutral line. Never ask for "positive" or "5 star".
- Never offer or hint at a discount, refund or reward for feedback or a review. That is against platform rules and UK law on fake and incentivised reviews.
- Nothing that discourages honest feedback.
```
**Check before you post:**
- It is sent once, and nothing is linked to feedback.

### M11 Difficult or abusive buyer
**Use it when:** A buyer is rude, threatening, demanding things not in the listing, or threatening bad feedback to get a discount.
**Paste this:**
```text
A buyer on [PLATFORM] is being difficult. Help me respond calmly and protect myself.

What happened (details removed):
[DESCRIBE OR PASTE MESSAGES]
What the listing said on this point: [PASTE OR "NOT RELEVANT"]
Has a sale happened? [YES / NO]

Give me:
1. A one-line read: a genuine problem said rudely, a demand for something not in the listing, a threat linked to feedback, or abuse?
2. What to do: reply, reply once then stop, or do not reply; use the platform's report and block tools; keep all messages on the platform as a record.
3. If a reply makes sense, one calm reply under 60 words.

Rules:
- UK English. Calm and neutral. Never insult or threaten back.
- A genuine complaint still gets the platform's proper return or refund route, however rude the buyer.
- Threatening negative feedback to get something not in the listing is generally not allowed by platforms: tell me to report it, not give in.
- Threats to my safety: stop replying, report to the platform, and contact the police if I feel at risk.
```
**What good output looks like,** for "Refund me £10 or I'll leave negative feedback", after delivery, item as described:

> 1. **Read:** a threat linked to feedback. **Do:** reply once, then report it through the platform.
> 3. "Hi, I am sorry you are not happy. The item was sent as described and photographed. If there is a problem with it, please open a request through the app and it will be looked at properly."

**Check before you post:**
- You have not written anything you would mind the platform's support team reading.

### M12 Suspicious request or off-platform payment
**Use it when:** A buyer asks to pay another way, wants your email or number, asks you to post to a different address, or something just feels off.
**Paste this:**
```text
A buyer on [PLATFORM] sent a request that may be risky. Help me spot the risk and reply.

The request (details removed):
[PASTE MESSAGE]

Give me:
1. The warning signs (e.g. paying outside the app, a link to click, "my courier will collect", asking for my email or phone, a different delivery address, overpaying and asking for money back).
2. What to do: reply, decline, report or ignore.
3. A short, polite reply that keeps everything on the platform.

UK English. Do not help arrange payment, contact or delivery outside the platform. Do not tell me to click any link. Remind me that platform protection generally only covers sales paid through the platform.
```
**What good output looks like,** for "Can I pay you by bank transfer so we avoid fees? Send me your number.":

> 1. **Warning signs:** paying outside the app, asking for your number. **Do:** decline politely, report if they push.
> 3. "Hi, thanks, but I only sell through the app so we are both covered. You are welcome to buy or make an offer here."

**Check before you post:**
- You have not shared contact details or clicked any link from the buyer.
