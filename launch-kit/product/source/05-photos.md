# Module 05: Photos

Buyers cannot pick your item up, so your photos do most of the describing. AI cannot take them for you, but it is good at three jobs: planning the shots so you do not forget the label, the flaw or the back; checking your photo set against your Listing Brief; and writing captions and alt text.

For a ready tick-list of shots by category, use **templates/photo-shot-lists.md**. PH1 tailors it to one item.

## The one rule about edited and AI photos

**Your photos must show the actual item you are sending, in its actual condition.**

- Cropping, straightening and a little brightness are fine.
- Removing a stain, scratch or bobbling, changing the colour, adding a background that suggests extras are included, or an AI "model" image that changes how the item looks is not fine.
- Stock photos and photos copied from other listings are not fine for second-hand items.

| Platform | Their rules, in short |
|---|---|
| eBay UK | Photos must show the actual item. No stock photos for used items. No added borders, text or watermarks |
| Vinted | Your own photos of the item's current condition. Whole item first, labels for branded items, every defect. No stock, watermarked or found-online images, no collages |
| Etsy | Listings must accurately show what the buyer will receive. Specific rules on disclosing AI use |
| TikTok Shop UK | Realistic AI images or video must be labelled, and must not make the product look different (texture, features, size) |
| Depop, Amazon UK | Images must accurately show the product. Amazon has strict main image rules |

Rules change. **Check the current rule in the platform's help pages before using any AI editing or background tool.** A misleading photo is also a fast route to a "not as described" claim.

### PH1 Shot list for my item
**Use it when:** You have written the Listing Brief and want a numbered shot list before you pick up your phone.
**Paste this:**
```text
Help me plan listing photos for this exact item on [PLATFORM]. Most photos allowed (check the app): [NUMBER OR "NOT SURE"]

Write a numbered shot list. For each shot: what to photograph, the angle or distance, and why a buyer needs it (one short line).

Rules:
- UK English, short enough to read on a phone.
- Order: cover photo (whole item), back, labels, then one shot for every flaw in my brief, then details.
- Base it only on my brief. If a shot depends on something the brief does not say (e.g. whether there is a box), write [CHECK: ...].
- If there are more shots than the platform allows, say which to combine or drop, but never drop a flaw shot.
- No filters or edits that change colour or hide wear.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like,** for Clarks black leather Chelsea boots, UK 6, light creasing across the toes, small scuff on the left heel:

> 1. **Cover:** pair side on, plain floor, phone at boot height.
> 2. **Front:** both toes to camera. Shows the creasing honestly.
> 3. **Soles:** side by side, top down. Buyers judge wear from soles.
> 4. **Inside:** size and brand stamp on the insole.
> 5. **Flaw:** left heel scuff, close up, coin for scale.
> [CHECK: is there a box? If yes, add one shot.]

**Check before you post:**
- The cover photo shows the whole item, not a close-up.

### PH2 Flaw photo checklist
**Use it when:** Your item has any wear, marks or faults and you want to show them properly. This protects you as much as the buyer.
**Paste this:**
```text
Help me photograph the flaws on my item honestly and clearly.

1. List every flaw in my brief (and any extras below) as a checklist.
2. For each, tell me how to photograph it: distance, angle, light, and what to put next to it for scale (coin, ruler, tape measure).
3. Suggest a short caption for each flaw photo using the same words as my brief.
4. List places flaws often hide on this type of item that my brief does not mention, as [CHECK: ...] questions. Do not assume they are flawed.

UK English. Plain, factual wording. Never describe a flaw as smaller than my brief says.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]

Extra flaws I have noticed since: [EXTRA FLAWS OR "NONE"]
```
**What good output looks like,** for the Next jumper with light bobbling under both arms:

> - **How to shoot it:** lay it flat, lift one arm out, shoot from about 20 cm in window light from the side (side light shows bobbling better). Ruler along the patch.
> - **Caption:** "Light bobbling under both arms, shown close up."
> - **Also check:** [CHECK: bobbling where a bag strap rubs?] [CHECK: any small holes? Hold it up to a window.] [CHECK: any smell, such as smoke or perfume?]

**Check before you post:**
- The flaw is visible at phone screen size, not only when zoomed in.

### PH3 Phone lighting and background plan
**Use it when:** You photograph at home with just a phone and want truer colour without buying kit.
**Paste this:**
```text
I photograph listings on my phone at home. Help me set up for the item in my brief.

- When I usually shoot: [E.G. WEEKDAY EVENINGS]
- Space and window: [E.G. SMALL BEDROOM, WINDOW FACING NORTH]
- Possible backgrounds I own: [E.G. WHITE DUVET COVER, GREY WALL, WOODEN FLOOR]

Give me:
1. The best place and time to shoot with my window light.
2. Which background suits this item's colour, and why.
3. Five phone tips (focus, exposure, clean lens, no zoom, no flash glare).
4. How to check the colour on screen matches the real item.
5. What to do if I must shoot after dark, with ordinary lamps.

UK English, no jargon, short steps. Do not recommend buying anything or any edit that changes colour or hides wear.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**Check before you post:**
- Nothing in the background could be mistaken as included.

### PH4 Photo set audit
**Use it when:** You have taken your photos and want a second pair of eyes to spot gaps before you post.
**Paste this:**
```text
Check my photo set against my Listing Brief before I post.

My photos, in order (a plain description of each):
1. [DESCRIBE PHOTO 1, E.G. "FRONT OF JUMPER, FLAT ON BED"]
2. [DESCRIBE PHOTO 2]
3. [ADD MORE LINES AS NEEDED]

(If your AI tool accepts images you can upload the photos instead, but only photos of the item, never addresses, faces or documents.)

Tell me:
1. Missing shots a buyer would expect.
2. Mismatches with the brief (a flaw shown but not in the brief, or something in shot that is not included).
3. The best order. The cover should show the whole item in true colour on a plain background, filling the frame and clear as a small square thumbnail. Major flaws go early in the set.
4. Privacy: anything to crop out (post, family photos, house numbers, reflections).

UK English. Only comment on what I describe or show. If you cannot tell, write [CHECK: ...].

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like,** for a Tefal kettle with limescale inside and a small chip on the lid:

> - **Missing:** the base plate and plug, and the inside showing the limescale (it is in your brief, so show it).
> - **Mismatch:** photo 3 has two mugs beside the kettle. Retake without them.
> - **Privacy:** photo 2 shows a letter on the worktop. Crop it out.

**Check before you post:**
- Nothing in any photo suggests something is included when it is not.

### PH5 Photo captions
**Use it when:** Your platform lets you add a caption to each photo, or you want matching lines for the description.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write one short caption for each of my listing photos.

My photos, in order:
1. [DESCRIBE PHOTO 1]
2. [DESCRIBE PHOTO 2]
3. [ADD MORE AS NEEDED]

Rules:
- UK English, 12 words or fewer each. Say what the photo shows, plainly.
- Flaw captions use the same words as my brief and never play the flaw down.
- Only facts from the brief. If a caption needs a fact I have not given, write [CHECK: ...].
- No hype words, emoji or exclamation marks.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**Check before you post:**
- Captions match the description word for word on flaws.

### PH6 Alt text for listing photos
**Use it when:** Your platform or web shop has an alt text box (a short description of an image for people using screen readers). Etsy and most web shops have one.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write alt text for each of my product photos.

Photos, in order:
1. [DESCRIBE PHOTO 1]
2. [DESCRIBE PHOTO 2]
3. [ADD MORE AS NEEDED]

Rules:
- UK English. Describe plainly, for someone who cannot see it: item, colour, angle, any flaw shown.
- Do not start with "Image of" or "Photo of".
- No keyword stuffing or repeating the title. Alt text is for people, not search.
- Only facts from my brief. Missing details become [CHECK: ...].
- Under [CHARACTER LIMIT, OR 125] characters each.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**Check before you post:**
- Each line describes the photo, not the sales pitch.

### PH7 Is this edit OK?
**Use it when:** You are tempted to use a background remover, a "magic eraser", an AI model or any AI photo tool, and want to check it will not misrepresent the item.
**Paste this:**
```text
I want to edit a listing photo. Tell me whether the edit risks misrepresenting my item.

Platform: [PLATFORM]
The original photo shows: [DESCRIBE THE ORIGINAL]
The edit I want: [E.G. PLAIN WHITE BACKGROUND / REMOVE A HAIR / PUT THE JUMPER ON AN AI MODEL]
The tool: [TOOL NAME OR TYPE]
Rule text I copied from the platform's help pages (optional): [PASTE OR "NONE"]

Answer in three parts:
1. Risk: low, medium or high that the edit changes how the item looks (colour, condition, size, shape, what is included), in one or two lines.
2. A safer way to get a similar result without editing the item (e.g. reshoot on a plain sheet).
3. A reminder to check the platform's current photo and AI rules. Do not state rules as fact unless I pasted them.

UK English. Be honest, not reassuring. If the edit would hide or reduce any flaw in my brief, say plainly not to do it.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like,** for a cream Zara linen shirt with a faint mark on the left cuff, edited onto an AI model:

> 1. **Risk: high.** An AI model image generates a new version of the shirt. Fit, colour and creases may change and the cuff mark may disappear.
> 2. **Safer:** hang it on a door against a plain wall in daylight, add the cuff close-up, and give chest and length measurements for fit.

**Check before you post:**
- At least one unedited photo of the real item is in the set, and every flaw is still visible.
