# Module 03: Descriptions and condition

The title gets the tap. The description closes the gap between "that looks nice" and "I know exactly what I am getting". It is also your best protection against returns and "not as described" claims.

A good second-hand description is not long. It is complete. It answers the questions a buyer would ask, in the order they would ask them, and it tells the truth about flaws in plain words.

Every prompt here uses your Listing Brief from Module 01.

---

## The shape of a good description

For most items, this order works on every platform:

1. **What it is.** One line: brand, item, key detail.
2. **Size and measurements.** For clothes, measurements in cm. For everything else, dimensions.
3. **Condition, with flaws.** The grade, then each flaw: where, what, how big, which photo.
4. **Details.** Material, features, what is included, what is not.
5. **Postage.** How it will be sent, if the platform does not already show it.

Short paragraphs or short lines. Most of your buyers are reading on a phone.

---

## The honest condition grading scale

Use this scale in your brief, then match it to the nearest option in the platform's own condition dropdown. Platforms use different names, and they change them, so always **read the platform's own definitions when you pick** and check the current options in its help pages.

| Our grade | What it means | Typical flaws allowed | eBay UK (pre-owned clothing options, from February 2025) | Vinted options |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| New with tags | Unworn, unused, original tags attached | None | New with tags | New with tags |
| New without tags | Unworn, unused, tags removed or no tags | None, apart from possible shop marks | New without tags | New without tags |
| Excellent | Used, but hard to tell | Very faint signs of use, described | Pre-owned: Excellent | Very good |
| Very good | Light, normal signs of use | Light bobbling, faint fading, tiny marks, all described | Pre-owned: Excellent or Pre-owned: Good (read the definitions) | Very good |
| Good | Clearly used, fully usable | Visible bobbling, small marks, light wear at cuffs or corners, all described and photographed | Pre-owned: Good | Good |
| Fair | Well used, flaws are obvious | Stains, holes, heavy wear, faults, all described and photographed | Pre-owned: Fair | Satisfactory |

Other eBay categories (electronics, books, homeware) have their own condition lists. eBay publishes an "item conditions by category" help page. Check it for your category.

**Two rules for grading:**
- **If you are between two grades, pick the lower one.** A buyer who gets better than expected is happy. A buyer who gets worse opens a case.
- **The grade never replaces the flaw list.** "Good" is an opinion. "Two small pulls on the back, about 3 mm each, see photo 6" is a fact.

## Flaw disclosure wording that protects you

Most "not as described" disputes come from flaws the buyer did not expect. The fix is boring and effective: describe every flaw with **location, type, size and photo**.

| Weak (invites disputes) | Strong (hard to dispute) |
|---|---|
| Minor wear | Light rubbing on both heels, about 1 cm, see photo 5 |
| Small mark | Pale grey mark on the front, below the left pocket, about 5 mm. May wash out, not tested |
| Some bobbling | Light bobbling under both arms and along the side seams. None on the front |
| A few scratches | Three fine scratches on the back casing, only visible at an angle. Screen has no scratches |
| Used condition | Pages lightly yellowed. Previous owner's name in pen on the first page. Spine uncreased |
| Tiny chip | Chip on the rim next to the handle, about 2 mm, cannot be felt when drinking. See photo 4 |

**Wording habits that help:**
- Say what you checked: "No holes or stains that we can see" is honest. "Perfect" is a promise.
- Say what you did not check: "Not tested with a charger" or "Not checked for odour".
- Point to photos: "See photo 5" helps buyers find the flaw and shows you disclosed it.
- Do not soften: "barely noticeable" is your opinion. Give the size and let the buyer decide.

**A note on "sold as seen":** it does not cover a description that is wrong. Even private sellers must describe items accurately, and if you sell as a business, buyers keep their legal rights under UK consumer law whatever your listing says. Platforms also run their own buyer protection schemes. An accurate description is the protection, not a disclaimer.

## Measurements over sizes

For clothing, especially vintage and pre-loved, measurements are more useful than the size label. Sizes vary by brand, by decade and after washing.

| Item | Measurements buyers want |
|---|---|
| Tops, jumpers, shirts, jackets | Pit to pit, length (shoulder by collar to hem), sleeve (shoulder seam to cuff), shoulder width |
| Dresses | Pit to pit, waist, length, and hips for fitted styles |
| Trousers, jeans, shorts | Waist (flat, and doubled), inside leg, rise, leg opening, hips |
| Skirts | Waist, hips, length |
| Shoes | UK size as labelled, insole length in cm if you can measure it |
| Bags | Height, width, depth, strap drop (top of strap to top of bag) |

**How to measure:** lay it flat on a table, do up buttons and zips, smooth it without stretching, and measure in cm. Say "measured flat" in the listing, and say if a figure has been doubled.

## The D prompts

### D1 eBay UK description
**Use it when:** you have a title and want a clear, complete eBay UK description.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write an eBay UK item description for the item in my Listing Brief below.

Structure:
1. One opening line: what it is (brand, item, key detail).
2. Size and measurements (in cm, say "measured flat" for clothing).
3. Condition: the grade, then every flaw as a separate line with location, size and the photo number if I gave one.
4. Details: material, features, what is included, what is not included.
5. Postage: only what is in my brief.

Rules:
1. UK English (colour, jumper, trousers, postage, parcel).
2. Use ONLY facts from my Listing Brief. For anything important that is missing, write [CHECK: ...]. Never invent measurements, materials, dates or test results.
3. Include every flaw from the brief. Do not soften, shorten or leave any out.
4. No hype words (stunning, amazing, gorgeous, perfect, must have), no exclamation marks, no emoji.
5. No misleading claims: do not say rare, vintage, genuine, fully working, new, or smoke-free unless the brief says so.
6. Do not reveal my lowest acceptable price.
7. Do not write anything about returns, refunds or buyers' rights. I will set those in eBay's own settings.
8. Short lines, easy to read on a phone. Under 200 words.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a grey Next wool-blend jumper, size 12:

> Next women's grey wool-blend crew neck jumper, size 12.
>
> **Measurements (laid flat):**
> Pit to pit: 51 cm
> Length: 63 cm
> Sleeve: 58 cm
>
> **Condition:** Good, pre-owned.
> Light bobbling under both arms and along the side seams (photo 5). No holes, stains or pulls that we can see.
>
> **Details:** 60% polyester, 30% acrylic, 10% wool (from the care label). Ribbed cuffs and hem. Long sleeves.
>
> **Postage:** Folded and sent in a postal bag.

**Check before you post:**
- Read it next to your photos. Every flaw in the text should be visible in a photo, and every flaw in a photo should be in the text.
- Make sure the eBay condition dropdown matches the grade in the description.
- Replace any [CHECK] before you post. eBay buyers notice gaps.

```text

4. No hype words, no exclamation marks, no emoji strings.
5. Do not mention payment, postage outside Vinted, or contact details. Everything stays in the app.

[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For black Levi's 501 jeans, W30 L32, faded at the knees:

> Black Levi's 501 straight leg jeans. Label size W30 L32.
>
> Measured flat: waist 39 cm (78 cm doubled), inside leg 81 cm, rise 28 cm, leg opening 20 cm.
>

**Check before you post:**
- Fill in Vinted's brand, size, colour, condition and material fields too. They matter as much as the text.
- Match your Vinted condition choice to the description (see the grading table above).
- Never move a sale or payment off the app, whatever a buyer suggests.

### D4 Etsy description (handmade or vintage)
**Use it when:** you sell handmade or vintage items on Etsy and need a description that is warm but factual.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write an Etsy listing description for the item in my Listing Brief below.

Structure:
1. Opening two lines: what it is and what makes it distinctive, from facts in the brief only. These lines show first, so make them clear.
2. Details: materials (all of them), size and dimensions, colours, weight if relevant.
3. Personalisation: what can be personalised, how to request it, any character limits. (Skip if not offered.)
4. Making and dispatch: made to order or ready to post, and making time, from my brief only.
5. Care instructions.
6. Safety notes from my brief (e.g. "not a toy", candle safety).
7. For vintage items: era (only as supported by the brief) and full condition with every flaw.

Rules:
1. UK English (personalised, colour, jewellery, organise).
2. Use ONLY facts in my brief. Write [CHECK: ...] for missing facts.
3. Warm, plain tone. No hype words, no "perfect gift for everyone", no exclamation marks.
4. No health, wellbeing or medical claims (e.g. calming, healing, detox, relieves anxiety, hypoallergenic) unless the brief includes supplier evidence and I confirm it.
5. Materials must be exactly as the brief says. Do not upgrade "silver-plated" to "silver" or "cotton blend" to "cotton".
6. Short paragraphs, readable on a phone.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a handmade soy wax candle in a 200 ml amber glass jar, fig scent:

> A hand-poured soy wax candle in an amber glass jar, scented with fig. Made in small batches in our workshop.
>
> **Details**
> Jar: amber glass, 200 ml, 8 cm tall
> Wax: soy wax blend [CHECK: exact wax type from supplier]
> Wick: cotton
> Burn time: [CHECK: your tested burn time, or leave out]
>
> **Made to order.** Dispatched within 3 working days.
>
> **Care:** trim the wick to 5 mm before each burn. Burn for no more than 4 hours at a time.
>
> **Safety:** never leave a burning candle unattended. Keep away from children, pets and anything flammable. [CHECK: include the safety and allergen information your fragrance supplier provides for scented candles]

**Check before you post:**
- Scented products sold in the UK may need specific safety and allergen labelling. Check the current requirements with your supplier and official guidance.
- Leave out burn times and scent strength claims you have not tested.
- Make sure dispatch times match your Etsy shipping settings.

### D6 Condition grader
**Use it when:** you are not sure which condition grade to choose, or which platform option matches.
**Paste this:**
```text
Help me choose an honest condition grade for an item I am selling on [PLATFORM] in the UK.

Use this scale: New with tags, New without tags, Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair.
Rules for grading:
- If the item sits between two grades, choose the lower one.
- Base the grade only on the flaws and details I list. Do not assume flaws I have not mentioned, and do not ignore any I have.

Then:
1. Give the grade and a one-line reason.
2. Tell me which option in [PLATFORM]'s condition list is the closest match, but remind me to read that platform's own definition before choosing, as the options change.
3. Write a one or two line condition summary for the description, listing each flaw with location and size.
4. List anything I should check or photograph to be sure of the grade.

UK English. No softening words (barely, hardly, tiny) unless I used them, and never without a size.

Item: [WHAT IT IS]
Flaws and wear: [LIST EVERY FLAW]
Tags attached? [YES / NO]
Used or worn? [HOW OFTEN, IF KNOWN]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a Nike hoodie with light pilling on the cuffs, a faded logo print, and one small bleach spot on the back:

> **Grade:** Good. The bleach spot is a visible permanent mark, so this cannot be Very good.
> **Closest Vinted option:** Good (read Vinted's description of each option before choosing).
> **Condition summary:** Good pre-owned condition. Light pilling on both cuffs. Logo print on the chest has faded and has fine cracks. One small bleach spot on the upper back, about 4 mm, see photo 6.
> **Check or photograph:** the bleach spot next to a coin for scale; the logo print close up; inside the hood for marks.

**Check before you post:**
- The grade and the description must agree. A mismatch is a common cause of disputes.
- Photograph every flaw mentioned in the summary.
- If in doubt, go one grade lower.

### D7 Flaw disclosure rewriter
**Use it when:** you have written your flaws in a hurry and want them clear, specific and protective.
**Paste this:**
```text
Rewrite the flaws below so they are clear and specific for a UK [PLATFORM] listing. The goal is that a buyer knows exactly what to expect and there are no surprises.

For each flaw, write one line with:
- What it is (mark, pull, chip, scratch, fading, stain, fault)
- Where it is (use left and right as the wearer or user sees it, and say so)
- How big (in mm or cm)
- How visible (e.g. "visible from arm's length", "only visible close up", only if I said so)
- Which photo shows it, if I gave photo numbers

Rules:
1. UK English.
2. Do not make any flaw sound smaller or less serious than I described it. Do not remove any flaw.
3. If size, location or visibility is missing, write [CHECK: ...].
4. Add one honest line of what I checked and found no problems with, only if I list it below.
5. No hype or reassurance words (perfect otherwise, amazing condition apart from). Just facts.

My flaws, as I wrote them:
[PASTE FLAWS]

Things I checked and found fine:
[E.G. NO HOLES, ZIP WORKS, NO ODOUR]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For the notes "mark on the front, bit of fraying at the hem, zip a bit stiff":

> - Pale mark on the front, [CHECK: which side, and size in mm]
> - Light fraying along the bottom hem at the back, [CHECK: length in cm]
> - Zip works but is stiff to pull, especially at the top
> - Checked: no holes, all buttons present, no odour noticed

**Check before you post:**
- Fill every [CHECK] with a real measurement.
- "Left" and "right" cause confusion. Say "as worn" and point to the photo.
- Keep the same flaw list across every platform you list on.

### D8 Measurements block
**Use it when:** you want a neat, consistent measurements section for clothing, bags or shoes, plus a reminder of what to measure.
**Paste this:**
```text
I am listing [ITEM TYPE] on [PLATFORM] in the UK.

Step 1: Tell me which measurements buyers most want for this type of item, and in one short line each, how to measure them flat with a tape measure. Keep it to the essentials (4 to 6 measurements).

Step 2: When I reply with my numbers, format them as a neat measurements block:
- Heading: "Measurements (laid flat, in cm)"
- One measurement per line
- If I give inches, convert to cm and round to the nearest 0.5 cm, showing inches in brackets
- If I give a doubled figure, say "doubled"
- Add the label size at the top

Rules: UK English. Never estimate or invent a measurement. If one is missing, write [CHECK: measure X]. Do not add fit advice unless I give it (e.g. "fits like a UK 10 on me").
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a wool coat, after the seller replies with numbers:

> **Label size:** UK 14
> **Measurements (laid flat, in cm)**
> Pit to pit: 58 cm
> Shoulder to shoulder: 44 cm
> Length (shoulder by collar to hem): 96 cm
> Sleeve (shoulder seam to cuff): 61 cm
> Waist (flat, buttoned): 55 cm (110 cm doubled)

**Check before you post:**
- Measure with the item buttoned and smoothed, not stretched.
- Use the same method every time so repeat buyers can compare.
- If the item has stretch, say so. It affects fit more than the numbers do.

### D9 Bundle and job lot description

> 2. [Title], [Author], paperback, 2017 printing. Good. Light reading creases on spine.
> 3. [Title], [Author], paperback. Good. Previous owner's name on first page. [CHECK: printing year]

**Check before you post:**

### D10 De-hype and UK-ify an AI description
**Use it when:** you have a description from a generic AI tool or an old listing that sounds American, gushing or over-promising.
**Paste this:**
```text
Rewrite this listing description for a UK [PLATFORM] buyer.

Fix:
1. US spelling and vocabulary to UK (color to colour, pants to trousers, sweater to jumper, sneakers to trainers, purse to handbag, zipper to zip, closet to wardrobe).
2. US sizes to UK where the brief gives the UK size. If only a US size is given, keep it and label it "US size".
3. Remove hype and filler (stunning, gorgeous, must have, elevate your wardrobe, timeless piece, you will love it, exclamation marks, emoji).
4. Remove or flag any claim not supported by my Listing Brief (write [CHECK: ...] after it, or remove it and tell me).
5. Keep every flaw, with location and size.
6. Keep it short and readable on a phone.

After the rewrite, list what you removed or flagged and why, in short bullet points.

Old description:
[PASTE OLD DESCRIPTION]

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

Old: "OMG this gorgeous vintage sweater is a MUST HAVE!! Timeless piece in amazing condition, perfect color for fall. Fits true to size!"

> **Rewrite:** Cream cable knit jumper, size M on label. Measured flat: pit to pit 55 cm, length 66 cm. Very good condition, light bobbling under the arms. [CHECK: fabric composition from the care label]
>
> **Removed or flagged:**
> - "Vintage": not supported by the brief (no era given).
> - "Amazing condition": replaced with the grade and the actual flaw.
> - "Fits true to size": not in the brief. Measurements given instead.
> - "Fall": US word, and seasonal wording was not needed.
> - Hype words and exclamation marks removed.

**Check before you post:**
- Read the rewrite against the item one last time.
- If the AI removed a claim you know is true, add it back with the evidence (e.g. "receipt available").
- Run Module 09's rule checker if the item is in a category with extra rules.
