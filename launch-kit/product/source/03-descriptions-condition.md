# Module 03: Descriptions and condition

The title gets the tap. The description turns "that looks nice" into "I know exactly what I am getting", and it is your best protection against "not as described" claims. A good second-hand description is not long. It is complete.

## The shape of a good description

1. **What it is:** brand, item, key detail.
2. **Size and measurements:** in cm for clothes, dimensions for everything else.
3. **Condition, with flaws:** the grade, then each flaw: where, what, how big, which photo.
4. **Details:** material, features, what is included and not included.
5. **Postage,** if the platform does not already show it.

Short lines. Most buyers read on a phone.

---

## The honest condition grading scale

Use this scale in your brief, then pick the nearest option in the platform's own condition list. Platforms name and define their options differently, and change them, so **read the platform's own definitions when you choose** and check the current options in its help pages.

| Our grade | What it means | Typical flaws, all described | Nearest Vinted option |
|---|---|---|---|
| New with tags | Unworn, unused, tags attached | None | New with tags |
| New without tags | Unworn, unused, no tags | None, apart from possible shop marks | New without tags |
| Excellent | Used, but hard to tell | Very faint signs of use | Very good |
| Very good | Light, normal signs of use | Light bobbling, faint fading, tiny marks | Very good |
| Good | Clearly used, fully usable | Visible bobbling, small marks, light wear at cuffs or corners, photographed | Good |
| Fair | Well used, flaws are obvious | Stains, holes, heavy wear, faults, photographed | Satisfactory |

eBay's pre-owned options and their definitions vary by category. Read the definitions shown in the listing form.

**Two rules:**
- **Between two grades, pick the lower one.** A buyer who gets better than expected is happy. One who gets worse opens a case.
- **The grade never replaces the flaw list.** "Good" is an opinion. "Two small pulls on the back, about 3 mm each, see photo 6" is a fact.

## Flaw wording that protects you

Describe every flaw with **location, type, size and photo**.

| Weak (invites disputes) | Strong (hard to dispute) |
|---|---|
| Minor wear | Light rubbing on both heels, about 1 cm, see photo 5 |
| Small mark | Pale grey mark on the front, below the left pocket, about 5 mm. Not tested whether it washes out |
| A few scratches | Three fine scratches on the back casing, only visible at an angle. Screen has no scratches |

- Say what you checked: "No holes or stains that I can see" is honest. "Perfect" is a promise.
- Say what you did not check: "Not tested with a charger".
- Do not soften: "barely noticeable" is your opinion. Give the size and let the buyer decide.

**"Sold as seen"** does not cover a wrong description. Every seller must describe items accurately, business sellers cannot remove buyers' legal rights, and platform buyer protection applies on top (Module 07 explains). An accurate description is the protection, not a disclaimer.

## The D prompts

### D1 eBay UK description
**Use it when:** You have a title and want a clear, complete eBay UK description.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write an eBay UK item description for the item in my Listing Brief below.

Order:
1. One opening line: brand, item, key detail.
2. Size and measurements in cm ("measured flat" for clothing).
3. Condition: the grade, then every flaw on its own line with location, size and photo number if given.
4. Details: material, features, what is included and not included.
5. Postage: only what is in my brief.

Rules:
- UK English. Short lines. Under 200 words.
- Use ONLY facts in my brief. Missing facts become [CHECK: ...]. Never invent measurements, materials, dates or test results.
- Include every flaw. Do not soften or leave any out.
- No hype words, exclamation marks or emoji.
- No rare, vintage, genuine, fully working, new or smoke-free unless the brief says so.
- Do not reveal my lowest price. Nothing about returns (I set those in eBay).

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For the Next jumper (grey, women's, crew neck, size 12, 60% acrylic and 40% wool, measured, light bobbling under both arms, sent in a postal bag):

> Next women's grey wool-blend jumper, size 12.
>
> **Measured flat:** pit to pit 51 cm, length 63 cm, sleeve 58 cm.
>
> **Condition:** Good, pre-owned. Light bobbling under both arms (photo 5). No holes, stains or pulls that I can see.
>
> **Details:** 60% acrylic, 40% wool (care label).
>
> **Postage:** Folded and sent in a postal bag.

**Check before you post:**
- Every flaw in the text is in a photo, and every flaw in a photo is in the text.
- eBay's condition option matches the grade in the description.

### D2 Vinted description

**Paste this:**
```text
Write a Vinted description for the item in my Listing Brief below.

Order:
1. What it is, in one line.
2. Label size and measurements in cm (measured flat).
3. Condition, then every flaw with location and size.
4. Material and useful details (fit, pockets, lining).
5. One line inviting questions.

Rules:
- UK English. Use ONLY facts in my brief. Missing facts become [CHECK: ...].
- Every flaw included, at the same level of seriousness.
- No hype words, exclamation marks or emoji strings.
- No payment, contact details or "no returns" lines. No other brand names.
- Do not reveal my lowest price.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For black Levi's 501 jeans, W30 L32:

> Black Levi's 501 straight leg jeans, label size W30 L32. Measured flat: waist 39 cm (78 cm doubled), inside leg 81 cm. Good condition: faded to a washed charcoal on both knees (photo 4). No rips or holes. 100% cotton, button fly. Happy to answer questions or send more photos.

**Check before you post:**
- Fill in Vinted's brand, size, colour, condition and material fields. They matter as much as the text.

```text
- No payment or contact details.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```

For the brown suede Topshop jacket, size 10:

> Topshop brown suede jacket, size 10, 90s style
>
> Boxy, cropped fit, four-button front. Measured flat: pit to pit 52 cm, length 58 cm. Good condition: light darkening on both cuffs, small scuff on the back hem, about 1 cm (photo 6).
>

**Check before you post:**
- Fill in Depop's category, size, brand and condition fields as well.

### D4 Etsy description (handmade or vintage)
**Use it when:** You sell handmade or vintage items on Etsy and need a description that is warm but factual.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write an Etsy listing description for the item in my Listing Brief below.

Order:
1. Two opening lines: what it is and what makes it distinctive, from the brief only.
2. Details: every material, size and dimensions, colours, weight if relevant.
3. Personalisation: what can change and how to request it (skip if not offered).
4. Made to order or ready to post, and making time.
5. Care instructions, then safety notes from my brief.
6. Vintage items only: era (as supported by the brief) and every flaw.

Rules:
- UK English. Warm, plain tone. Short paragraphs.
- Use ONLY facts in my brief. Missing facts become [CHECK: ...].
- Materials exactly as written. Do not upgrade "silver-plated" to "silver" or "cotton blend" to "cotton".
- No health, wellbeing or medical claims (calming, healing, detox, hypoallergenic) unless I give supplier evidence.
- No hype, no "perfect gift for everyone", no exclamation marks.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a handmade soy wax candle in a 200 ml amber glass jar, fig scent:

> A hand-poured soy wax candle in a 200 ml amber glass jar, scented with fig. **Wax:** soy wax blend [CHECK: exact wax from supplier]. **Burn time:** [CHECK: your tested burn time, or leave out]. Made to order, dispatched within 3 working days. **Safety:** never leave a burning candle unattended. [CHECK: the safety and allergen information your fragrance supplier provides]

**Check before you post:**
- Scented products sold in the UK may need specific safety and allergen labelling. Check with your supplier and official guidance.
- Dispatch times match your Etsy postage settings.

### D5 Amazon UK bullet points and description

```text

Rules:
- UK English. Use ONLY facts in my brief. Missing facts become [CHECK: ...].

- No special characters, emoji or other brands. Do not repeat the title word for word.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```

> - IN THE BOX: 4 napkins.

**Check before you post:**
- Every fact matches your product specification and packaging exactly.

### D6 Condition grader
**Use it when:** You are not sure which condition grade to choose, or which platform option matches.
**Paste this:**
```text
Help me choose an honest condition grade for an item I am selling on [PLATFORM] in the UK.

Scale: New with tags, New without tags, Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair.
- If the item sits between two grades, choose the lower one.
- Base the grade only on what I list. Do not assume or ignore any flaw.

Then:
1. Give the grade and a one-line reason.
2. Name the closest option in [PLATFORM]'s condition list, and remind me to read the platform's own definition first.
3. Write a one or two line condition summary listing each flaw with location and size.
4. List anything I should check or photograph to be sure.

UK English. No softening words (barely, hardly, tiny) unless I used them, and never without a size.

Item: [WHAT IT IS]
Flaws and wear: [LIST EVERY FLAW]
Tags attached? [YES / NO]
Used or worn? [HOW OFTEN, IF KNOWN]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a Nike hoodie with light pilling on the cuffs, a faded and cracked logo print, and a bleach spot on the back, about 4 mm:

> **Grade:** Good. The bleach spot is a permanent visible mark, so not Very good.
> **Closest Vinted option:** Good (read Vinted's description first).
> **Summary:** Light pilling on both cuffs. Logo print faded with fine cracks. One bleach spot on the upper back, about 4 mm, see photo 6.

**Check before you post:**
- The grade and the description agree. A mismatch is a common cause of disputes.

### D7 Flaw disclosure rewriter
**Use it when:** You wrote your flaws in a hurry and want them clear, specific and protective.
**Paste this:**
```text
Rewrite the flaws below so they are clear and specific for a UK [PLATFORM] listing. A buyer should know exactly what to expect.

For each flaw, one line with: what it is, where it is (left and right as worn or used, and say so), how big (mm or cm), how visible (only if I said), and the photo number if I gave one.

Rules:
- UK English.
- Do not make any flaw sound smaller than I described it. Do not remove any.
- Missing size, location or visibility becomes [CHECK: ...].
- Add one line of what I checked and found fine, only from my list below.
- No reassurance words ("perfect otherwise"). Just facts.

My flaws, as I wrote them:
[PASTE FLAWS]

Things I checked and found fine:
[E.G. NO HOLES, ZIP WORKS, NO ODOUR]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For "mark on the front, bit of fraying at the hem, zip a bit stiff":

> - Pale mark on the front [CHECK: which side, and size in mm]
> - Light fraying along the bottom hem [CHECK: length in cm]
> - Zip works but is stiff to pull

**Check before you post:**
- Use the same flaw list on every platform you list on.

### D8 Measurements block
**Use it when:** You want a neat measurements section for clothing, bags or shoes, and a reminder of what to measure.
**Paste this:**
```text
I am listing [ITEM TYPE] on [PLATFORM] in the UK.

Step 1: Tell me the 4 to 6 measurements buyers most want for this item, with one short line each on how to measure it flat.

Step 2: When I reply with my numbers, format them as:
- Heading: "Measurements (laid flat, in cm)", with the label size at the top
- One measurement per line
- Inches converted to cm, rounded to the nearest 0.5 cm, inches in brackets
- "Doubled" next to any doubled figure

UK English. Never estimate or invent a measurement. If one is missing, write [CHECK: measure X]. No fit advice unless I give it.
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a wool coat, after the seller replies:

> **Label size:** UK 14. **Measurements (laid flat, in cm):** pit to pit 58, shoulder to shoulder 44, length (shoulder by collar to hem) 96, sleeve (shoulder seam to cuff) 61.

**Check before you post:**
- Measure buttoned and smoothed, not stretched, the same way every time. Say if the fabric stretches.

### D9 Bundle and job lot description
**Use it when:** You are selling several items together (kids' clothes, a box of books, a job lot of mugs).
**Paste this:**
```text
Write a [PLATFORM] UK description for a bundle or job lot from my list below.

1. Opening line: what the bundle is, how many items, key shared details (e.g. "Bundle of 8 girls' tops, age 7 to 8 years").
2. A numbered list: one line per item with brand, item, size, condition and any flaw with location and size.
3. What the bundle does NOT include (only if I say).
4. Parcel weight or size if I give it.

Rules:
- UK English. Use ONLY facts in my list. A line missing brand, size or condition gets [CHECK: ...].
- Flaws listed against the right item, never merged into "some wear".
- If condition varies, say so: "Condition varies from Very good to Fair, see each item".
- No hype, exclamation marks, "bargain" or price talk. Do not reveal my lowest price.

My list:
[ITEM 1: BRAND, ITEM, SIZE, CONDITION, FLAWS]
[ITEM 2: BRAND, ITEM, SIZE, CONDITION, FLAWS]
[ITEM 3: BRAND, ITEM, SIZE, CONDITION, FLAWS]
Parcel weight or size: [IF KNOWN]
```
**What good output looks like:**

> Bundle of 5 paperback crime novels. Condition varies from Very good to Good, see each book.
>
> 1. [TITLE], [AUTHOR], paperback, 2019 printing. Very good. Spine uncreased.
> 2. [TITLE], [AUTHOR], paperback. Good. Previous owner's name on first page. [CHECK: printing year]
> ...
>
> Parcel: about 1.4 kg.

**Check before you post:**
- Count the items again before you post, and photograph them all together. A missing item is a common dispute.

### D10 De-hype and UK-ify an AI description
**Use it when:** You have a description from a generic AI tool, a platform's AI helper or an old listing that sounds American, gushing or over-promising.
**Paste this:**
```text
Rewrite this listing description for a UK [PLATFORM] buyer.

Fix:
1. US spelling and words to UK (color to colour, pants to trousers, sweater to jumper, sneakers to trainers, zipper to zip).
2. US sizes to UK where my brief gives the UK size. Otherwise keep it and label it "US size".
3. Remove hype and filler (stunning, gorgeous, must have, timeless piece, exclamation marks, emoji).
4. Remove or flag with [CHECK: ...] any claim not supported by my brief.
5. Keep every flaw, with location and size. Keep it short.

Then list what you removed or flagged and why, in short bullet points.

Old description:
[PASTE OLD DESCRIPTION]

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

Old: "OMG this gorgeous vintage sweater is a MUST HAVE!! Timeless piece in amazing condition, perfect color for fall. Fits true to size!"

> **Rewrite:** Cream cable knit jumper, size M on label. Measured flat: pit to pit 55 cm, length 66 cm. Very good condition, light bobbling under the arms. [CHECK: fabric from the care label]
>
> **Removed:** "Vintage" (no era in the brief), "amazing condition" (replaced with the grade and flaw), "fits true to size" (not in the brief), "fall" (US word), hype and exclamation marks.

**Check before you post:**
- If the AI removed a claim you know is true, add it back with the evidence (e.g. "receipt available").

## Description checklist

- [ ] Opens with what it is, then measurements in cm or dimensions
- [ ] Grade matches the platform's condition field
- [ ] Every flaw: where, what, how big, which photo
- [ ] What is included, and what is not
- [ ] No hype, unsupported claims, other brands, contact details or "no returns" wording that contradicts buyers' rights
