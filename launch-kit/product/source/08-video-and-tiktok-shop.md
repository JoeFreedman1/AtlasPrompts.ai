# 08 Video and TikTok Shop

Short videos sell things. You do not need to show your face, use your voice or film your home to make them. Everything in this module works with hands-only shots, a plain background, on-screen text and a text-to-speech voice.

This module covers two jobs:

1. **Faceless product videos** for items you are selling yourself (on TikTok, Instagram, or as the video slot on a listing where the platform allows it).
2. **TikTok Shop UK copy**: product titles, descriptions, a claims check, and a run-sheet for live selling.

As always, start with your **Listing Brief** (see module 01). The AI is only as accurate as the facts you give it.

## Before you start: three rules for video

- **Show the real item.** Film the exact item (or, for new stock, the exact product) the buyer will receive. No stock footage of a different item, no filters that change the colour, no editing out flaws.
- **No faces, no personal details.** Keep your face, reflections in mirrors, house numbers, post, pets, family photos and number plates out of shot. Hands are fine.
- **Say only what is true.** A video is part of your listing. If you would not write it in the description, do not say it in the video.

## TikTok Shop UK: claims to avoid

TikTok Shop UK's own policies say claims in e-commerce content must be genuine, accurate and verifiable, and that products must not claim or imply they can cure, heal, treat, prevent or relieve any disease or medical condition. This applies to titles, descriptions, images and videos. Product titles must not include promotional wording (for example "x% off"), your shop URL or seller name, references to TikTok or other marketplaces, or symbols and special characters.

In practice, keep these out of your TikTok Shop copy and videos:

- Health or medical wording: "cures", "heals", "relieves pain", "treats", "anti-inflammatory", "detox", "boosts immunity", "clinically proven" (unless you hold the evidence and the claim is allowed in that category).
- Before and after results you cannot prove.
- "Best", "number one", "better than [brand]" comparisons.
- Fake urgency: "only 2 left" when that is not true, countdowns that reset, "price going up tomorrow" when it is not.
- "Guaranteed", "100% safe", "non-toxic", "eco-friendly" or "sustainable" unless you can back it up with specific, checkable facts.
- Other platform names, discount codes for other sites, or asking buyers to pay you elsewhere.

Rules change. Read TikTok Shop UK's current policies in Seller Center (the Academy pages on product listing, content and prohibited or restricted products) before you list, and check the current rule in the platform's help pages when in doubt.

### V1 Faceless product video script (15 to 30 seconds)

**Use it when:** you want a short, honest video of one item without showing your face or using your own voice.

**Paste this:**
```text
You are helping a UK seller make a short faceless product video for an item they are selling. Write in UK English.

Here is my Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

Platform the video is for: [TIKTOK / INSTAGRAM / TIKTOK SHOP / OTHER]
Length: [15 / 20 / 30] seconds
Voice: [TEXT-TO-SPEECH VOICEOVER / ON-SCREEN TEXT ONLY]

Write a video script as a table with these columns: Seconds, What we see (hands-only or item-only shot), On-screen text (maximum 8 words), Voiceover line (if any).

Rules:
- Only use facts from the Listing Brief. If something I would need is missing, write [CHECK: what is missing] instead of guessing.
- No faces, no people, no reflections. Shots must be possible with a phone, a plain background and my hands.
- Show the flaws listed in the brief on screen, clearly, in at least one shot.
- No hype words (amazing, stunning, must-have, jaw-dropping), no "best", no invented comparisons, no health or medical claims, no fake urgency.
- Open with a plain hook in the first 2 seconds that says what the item is.
- End with a calm call to action that fits the platform (for example "Link in the listing" or "Details in the description"). Do not mention other platforms or ask buyers to pay outside the platform.
- After the table, give me a one-line caption and a list of any [CHECK: ...] items.
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example: grey Next wool-blend jumper, size 12, small bobbling under both arms, 20 seconds, text-to-speech.

| Seconds | What we see | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | Jumper laid flat on a cream sheet, top-down | Grey Next jumper, size 12 | "Grey Next wool-blend jumper, size 12." |
| 2 to 6 | Hand slowly turns the cuff to show the ribbing | Wool blend, ribbed cuffs | "Wool blend with ribbed cuffs and hem." |
| 6 to 10 | Tape measure across the chest, pit to pit | Pit to pit: 52 cm | "Fifty-two centimetres pit to pit." |
| 10 to 15 | Close-up under the left arm in daylight | Small bobbling under arms | "Light bobbling under both arms. Shown here." |
| 15 to 20 | Jumper folded, placed in a kraft mailer | Details in the listing | "Full details and photos in the listing." |

Caption: Grey Next wool-blend jumper, size 12, light bobbling under the arms (shown). Details in the listing.

[CHECK: exact wool percentage from the care label]

**Check before you post:**
- Watch it back with the sound off. Does the on-screen text alone tell the truth about the item?
- Is every flaw from your brief visible in at least one shot?
- Scan every frame for faces, reflections, post, house numbers or anything personal.
- Replace every [CHECK: ...] with a real fact or remove that line.

### V2 Hands-only shot list and showcase ideas

**Use it when:** you know what the item is but not how to film it without a face or a model.

**Paste this:**
```text
You are a UK product videographer who only films hands and products, never faces. Write in UK English.

Here is my Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

My kit: [PHONE ONLY / PHONE AND TRIPOD / PHONE, TRIPOD AND RING LIGHT]
Where I can film: [E.G. KITCHEN TABLE BY A WINDOW, WHITE BEDSHEET, BLANK WALL]

Give me 8 short shot ideas I can film in under 20 minutes. For each shot give: shot name, how to set it up (one or two lines), what it proves to the buyer (size, texture, how it works, condition), and roughly how many seconds to film.

Rules:
- No faces, no people wearing the item from the neck up, no mirrors that could show me. Hands, flat lays, item on a surface, or item on a plain hanger or mannequin only.
- Include at least one shot that shows scale (next to a common object or tape measure) and at least one clear flaw shot if the brief lists flaws.
- Only use facts from the brief. Where a shot depends on something not in the brief (for example the item lights up), write [CHECK: ...].
- Keep the colour true: tell me to use daylight or one consistent light and no colour filters.
- No props that could be mistaken as included with the item unless the brief says they are included.
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example: small-brand beech wood phone stand, adjustable angle, fits phones up to 7 inch.

1. **Hero turn.** Stand on a white sheet, slowly rotate it by hand. Proves shape and finish. 4 seconds.
2. **Angle adjust.** Hand tilts the stand through its angles. Proves it adjusts. 4 seconds.
3. **Phone drop-in.** Hand places a phone on the stand, portrait then landscape. Proves fit and use. 5 seconds.
4. **Scale shot.** Stand next to a mug. Proves size. 3 seconds.
5. **Grain close-up.** Slow pan across the wood in window light. Proves material. 3 seconds.
6. **Base grip.** Flip it to show the base pads. Proves it will not slide [CHECK: are there non-slip pads?]. 3 seconds.
7. **Cable gap.** Plug a charging cable through the gap. Proves charging while in use [CHECK: is there a cable gap?]. 4 seconds.
8. **What is in the box.** Items laid out in a row. Proves contents. 3 seconds.

Note: do not show a phone brand logo prominently, and make clear the phone is not included.

**Check before you post:**
- Does any prop (phone, cable, mug) look like it comes with the item? Say clearly what is not included.
- Is the colour on your screen the same as the real item in daylight?
- Have you answered every [CHECK: ...] before you film shots 6 and 7?

**Paste this:**
```text
Write in UK English for a UK audience.

Here is my Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

Here is a short description of my video, shot by shot:
[DESCRIBE YOUR SHOTS, E.G. "1. FLAT LAY 2. CLOSE-UP OF LABEL 3. FLAW UNDER ARM"]

Platform: [TIKTOK / INSTAGRAM / TIKTOK SHOP]

Give me:
1. On-screen text for each shot (maximum 8 words each, plain and readable on a phone).
2. One caption under 150 characters that says what the item is, the size or key spec, and the condition in plain words.
3. 3 to 5 relevant hashtags (item type, brand if genuine, category). No trending hashtags that have nothing to do with the item, no platform names, no hashtags that suggest a brand the item is not.

Rules: only use facts from the brief; write [CHECK: ...] for anything missing; no hype, no "best", no health claims, no fake urgency, no emojis unless I ask for them.
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example: Levi's 501 jeans, W32 L30, mid-blue, small fraying at the back hems.

On-screen text:
1. Levi's 501, W32 L30
2. Mid-blue, button fly
3. Red tab and patch
4. Light fraying at back hems

Caption: Levi's 501 jeans, W32 L30, mid-blue. Light fraying on the back hems, shown in the video.

Hashtags: #levis501 #mensjeans #secondhandjeans #denim

**Check before you post:**
- Is the size on screen the size on the label, not your guess?
- Does the caption mention the flaw as clearly as the video shows it?
- Only use a brand hashtag if the item is genuinely that brand.

### V4 TikTok Shop UK product title

**Use it when:** you are adding a product to TikTok Shop UK and want a clear, compliant title.

**Paste this:**
```text
You are a UK e-commerce copywriter who knows TikTok Shop UK's product listing guidelines. Write in UK English.

Here is my Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

Write 3 product title options for TikTok Shop UK.

Rules:
- Structure: brand (if I own or am authorised to sell it) + product type + key feature or material + size, quantity or capacity + colour or variant.
- Keep each title clear and readable, roughly 60 to 120 characters, and tell me the character count for each. [CHECK: the current maximum title length in TikTok Shop Seller Center]
- Do NOT include: promotional wording (sale, x% off, free postage, limited offer), my shop name or URL, the word TikTok, other marketplace names, symbols or special characters (such as ! * ~ | and similar), emojis, or ALL CAPS words.
- Do NOT include health, medical or results claims, "best", "number one" or comparisons with other brands.
- Only use facts from the brief. If a key fact is missing (size, material, quantity), put [CHECK: ...] in its place.
- After the titles, list any words I should double-check because they could be a claim.
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example: small UK brand "Tidy Nook" bamboo drawer organiser, set of 6 boxes, 3 sizes, natural colour.

1. Tidy Nook Bamboo Drawer Organiser Set of 6 Boxes in 3 Sizes Natural (67 characters)
2. Tidy Nook Bamboo Drawer Dividers 6 Piece Set for Kitchen Utensils Natural (73 characters)
3. Tidy Nook 6 Piece Bamboo Storage Boxes for Drawers 3 Sizes Natural Finish (73 characters)

Words to double-check: none are claims. Only use "for kitchen utensils" if the brief lists that use and the boxes suit it.

**Check before you post:**
- Count the characters yourself in Seller Center (it shows a counter) and trim if needed.
- Is the brand yours, or are you authorised to use it? Never use another brand's name for your own product.
- No symbols, promo words, platform names or claims.

### V5 TikTok Shop UK product description

**Use it when:** you need a TikTok Shop product description that is clear, scannable on a phone and free of risky claims.

**Paste this:**
```text
You are a UK e-commerce copywriter who knows TikTok Shop UK's content rules. Write in UK English.

Here is my Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

Write a TikTok Shop UK product description with:
1. An opening line (one sentence) that says what the product is and who it is for.
2. 4 to 6 short feature lines, each starting with the feature, then the plain benefit. One fact per line.
3. A "What's included" line.
4. A "Size and specs" section (dimensions, weight, capacity, materials) using only facts in the brief.
5. A "Care" line if the brief gives care instructions.

Rules:
- At least 30 words, and keep it concise (aim for under 500 characters for the main text if possible).
- Only use facts from the brief. Write [CHECK: ...] where anything is missing. Never invent materials, certifications, dimensions or safety standards.
- No health or medical claims (cure, heal, treat, prevent, relieve, detox, anti-bacterial unless certified and I say so), no "clinically proven", no "best", no comparisons with named brands, no guarantees, no fake urgency, no mention of other platforms or payment outside TikTok Shop.
- Avoid vague green claims ("eco-friendly", "sustainable") unless the brief gives a specific, checkable fact (for example "made from FSC-certified bamboo" with the certificate details).
- Plain, friendly UK tone. No hype words, no emojis unless I ask.
- After the description, list every phrase you think could count as a claim so I can check it.
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example: Tidy Nook bamboo drawer organiser set.

Six bamboo boxes in three sizes for sorting cutlery, utensils, stationery or make-up in drawers.

- Three sizes: mix and match the boxes to fit your drawer layout.
- Bamboo with a natural finish: a smooth, light wood look.
- Separate boxes: move them around or use them in different drawers.
- Raised edges: help keep small items in their own section.

What's included: 6 boxes (2 small, 2 medium, 2 large).

Size and specs: small 15 x 7.5 x 5 cm, medium 22.5 x 7.5 x 5 cm, large 30 x 7.5 x 5 cm. Material: bamboo [CHECK: is there a lacquer or coating?].

Care: wipe clean with a damp cloth. Do not soak.

Phrases to check: "help keep small items in their own section" is a mild functional statement. Keep it only if it is true in your own testing.

**Check before you post:**
- Measure the product yourself and match the numbers to the listing exactly.
- Remove every [CHECK: ...] by filling in the real fact.
- If you mention a certification (for example FSC), keep the proof handy in case TikTok Shop or a buyer asks.

### V6 TikTok Shop claims check and safe rewrite

**Use it when:** you have written (or been sent) product copy or a video script and want to catch risky claims before TikTok Shop or a buyer does.

**Paste this:**
```text
You are a careful UK compliance reviewer for TikTok Shop UK product listings and short videos. Write in UK English.

Here is the copy or script to check:
[PASTE TITLE, DESCRIPTION, CAPTION OR VIDEO SCRIPT]

Here is my Listing Brief (the facts I can back up):
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

Go through the copy line by line. Make a table with: Original wording, Why it might be a problem, Safer rewrite.

Flag anything that:
- makes or implies a health or medical claim (cure, heal, treat, prevent, relieve, detox, boosts immunity, pain relief, anti-ageing results);
- makes a result or performance claim not supported by the brief;
- uses "best", "number one", "better than", or compares with a named brand;
- uses fake urgency or scarcity, or a discount that is not real;
- uses vague green or safety claims (eco-friendly, non-toxic, 100% safe, chemical-free) without a specific fact in the brief;
- mentions TikTok or another platform in a product title, or points buyers to pay or order elsewhere;
- uses symbols, promo wording or a shop URL in the product title;
- states a fact that is not in the brief (mark these [CHECK: ...]).

Then give me the full cleaned-up version. Only use facts from the brief. Do not add new claims while rewriting. This is a helpful check, not legal advice.
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example: a lavender wheat bag.

| Original wording | Why it might be a problem | Safer rewrite |
|---|---|---|
| "Relieves back pain and helps you sleep" | Health claim: implies it treats a medical condition | "Microwaveable wheat bag with dried lavender. Warm it up for a cosy feel." |
| "100% natural and totally safe" | Vague safety claim, not in the brief | "Filled with wheat and dried lavender. Cotton cover." [CHECK: cover material] |
| "Only 3 left!!!" | Possible fake urgency, symbols | Remove, or state real stock in Seller Center only |
| "Better than the high street ones" | Comparison not supported | Remove |

Cleaned-up version: Microwaveable wheat bag with dried lavender and a cotton cover [CHECK: cover material]. Warm it up for a cosy feel. Heat as shown on the care label. [CHECK: heating time and safety instructions from the label]

**Check before you post:**
- For anything heated, electrical, for children or worn on skin, check the category rules in Seller Center: some need extra documents or are restricted.
- Include the real safety and heating instructions from the product label, word for word.
- If in doubt, leave the claim out.

### V7 Live-selling run-sheet

**Use it when:** you are planning a TikTok Shop LIVE (or any live selling session) and want a calm, honest plan to follow, without showing your face.

**Paste this:**
```text
You are helping a UK seller plan a faceless live selling session (hands and products only, voice or on-screen text). Write in UK English.

Session length: [30 / 45 / 60] minutes
Platform: [TIKTOK SHOP LIVE / OTHER]
Products (paste a short Listing Brief for each, or name, price, key facts and flaws):
[PASTE PRODUCTS]
Any real offer I am running (only if genuine): [E.G. "BUY 2 GET FREE POSTAGE" OR "NONE"]

Create a run-sheet as a table with: Time, Segment, What is on camera (hands-only or product-only), What I say or show on screen, Notes.

Include:
- A 2-minute welcome that says what we are showing today and how to buy (through the platform's own checkout only).
- One segment per product: show it, give the key facts, show flaws or limits honestly, show size or scale, say the price.
- A "questions" slot every 10 to 15 minutes.
- A 2-minute wrap-up.

Also give me:
- 6 short, honest answers to common live questions (size, postage time, returns, "is it genuine", "can you do cheaper", "can I pay another way"). Returns answers must respect UK consumer rights and the platform's return policy. The payment answer must say we only take payment through the platform.
- A list of things NOT to say on the live: health or medical claims, "best", fake countdowns or fake stock levels, invented discounts, other platform names, asking viewers to pay or message off the platform.

Only use facts from what I pasted. Write [CHECK: ...] for anything missing.
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example: 30-minute live, three kitchen products.

| Time | Segment | On camera | What I say or show | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 2:00 | Welcome | Three products on a clean worktop | "Evening. Tonight: a bamboo drawer set, a spice rack and a set of jar labels. Buy through the shop basket below." | Pin the first product |
| 2:00 to 9:00 | Drawer set | Hands lay out the 6 boxes | Sizes, bamboo, what's included, price | Show the tape measure on each size |
| 9:00 to 11:00 | Questions | Product stays in shot | Answer from the Q&A list | Read comments aloud, never usernames with personal details |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| 28:00 to 30:00 | Wrap-up | All three products | "Everything is in the shop basket. Thanks for watching." | |

Sample answer: "Can I pay by bank transfer?" "We only take payment through the TikTok Shop checkout. It keeps you covered."

**Check before you post:**
- Film a 30-second test first and check nothing personal is in shot or audible.
- Any offer you mention must be set up for real in Seller Center before you go live.
- Keep the "do not say" list on a sticky note next to your phone.

### V8 Turn one listing into a week of short videos

**Use it when:** you want a few different short videos for the same item or product without repeating yourself or stretching the truth.

**Paste this:**
```text
Write in UK English.

Here is my Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

Give me 5 different faceless short video ideas for this item, each 10 to 20 seconds. Use these angles:
1. "What you get" (unboxing or laying out contents).
2. "Size check" (next to everyday objects or a tape measure).
3. "Details close-up" (label, stitching, material, texture, ports, fixings).
4. "Honest condition" (shows any flaws in the brief and explains them calmly).
5. "How it is packed" (folded or wrapped, into the parcel).

For each idea give: a hook line (maximum 8 words), 3 to 4 shots (hands or item only), one line of on-screen text per shot, and a caption under 150 characters.

Rules: only facts from the brief; [CHECK: ...] for anything missing; no hype, no "best", no health claims, no fake urgency, no faces, no trending audio suggestions I would need rights for (tell me to use the platform's commercial-use music library if I add music).
```

**What good output looks like:**

Invented example: Nintendo Switch Lite, turquoise, used, light scratches on the back, charger included, no box.

Idea 4, "Honest condition":
- Hook: Switch Lite, turquoise. Here's the honest bit.
- Shot 1: front of the console, screen on. Text: "Screen: no scratches"
- Shot 2: back of the console in daylight. Text: "Light scratches on the back"
- Shot 3: charger next to the console. Text: "Charger included, no box"
- Caption: Used Switch Lite in turquoise. Screen clear, light scratches on the back (shown). Charger included, no box.

[CHECK: has the console been factory reset and signed out of the Nintendo account?]

**Check before you post:**
- Only claim "screen: no scratches" if you have checked it under a bright light.
- Factory reset any electronics and remove accounts before selling, and do not show serial numbers in the video.
- Each video should still match the listing exactly.

## Quick reminders for faceless video

- Film in daylight near a window, on a plain background. Keep the colour true.
- Hands only. Take off rings or anything personal if you would rather not show it.
- Use on-screen text with every voiceover line so it works with the sound off.
- Only use music from the platform's commercial-use library or royalty-free sources.
- Never film a buyer's parcel with their name or address visible.
