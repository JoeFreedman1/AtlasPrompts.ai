# Module 08: Video and TikTok Shop

**Skip this module if** you only list second-hand items on Vinted, eBay or Depop and do not make videos. Nothing else in the kit depends on it.

A short video can show an item's size, texture and flaws better than photos. You do not need to show your face, use your voice or film your home: everything here works with hands-only shots, a plain background, on-screen text and a text-to-speech voice. The module covers faceless product videos (V1 to V3) and TikTok Shop UK copy (V5 to V7). For TikTok Shop titles, use T6 in Module 02.

## Three rules for video

- **Show the real item.** The exact item (or, for new stock, the exact product) the buyer will receive. No stock footage, no colour filters, no editing out flaws.
- **No faces or personal details.** Keep faces, mirror reflections, house numbers, post, family photos and number plates out of shot. Hands are fine.
- **Say only what is true.** A video is part of your listing. If you would not write it, do not say it.

## TikTok Shop UK: claims to avoid

TikTok Shop UK's policies say claims must be genuine, accurate and verifiable, and products must not claim or imply they cure, heal, treat, prevent or relieve any disease or condition, in titles, descriptions, images or videos. In practice, keep out:

- Health wording: "cures", "relieves pain", "detox", "boosts immunity", "clinically proven".
- Before and after results you cannot prove, and weight loss claims.
- "Best", "number one" and "better than [BRAND]".
- Fake urgency: "only 2 left" when untrue, countdowns that reset.
- "Guaranteed", "100% safe", "non-toxic" or "eco-friendly" without specific, checkable facts.
- Other platform names, or asking buyers to pay elsewhere.

Rules change. Read TikTok Shop UK's current policies in Seller Center before you list.

### V1 Faceless product video script (15 to 30 seconds)
**Use it when:** You want a short, honest video of one item without showing your face or using your own voice.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write a short faceless product video script in UK English for the item in my Listing Brief.

Platform: [TIKTOK / INSTAGRAM / TIKTOK SHOP / OTHER]
Length: [15 / 20 / 30] seconds
Voice: [TEXT-TO-SPEECH / ON-SCREEN TEXT ONLY]

Table columns: Seconds, What we see (hands or item only), On-screen text (8 words or fewer), Voiceover line.

Rules:
- Only facts from the brief. Missing facts become [CHECK: ...].
- No faces, people or reflections. Every shot possible with a phone, a plain background and my hands.
- Show every flaw in the brief clearly in at least one shot.
- First 2 seconds say plainly what the item is. End with a calm line such as "Details in the listing".
- No hype, "best", comparisons, health claims, fake urgency or other platform names.
- After the table, a one-line caption and the list of [CHECK] items.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For the Next jumper, 20 seconds, text-to-speech:

> | Seconds | What we see | On-screen text | Voiceover |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 0 to 5 | Jumper flat on a cream sheet, tape across the chest | Grey Next jumper, size 12. Pit to pit 51 cm | "Grey Next wool-blend jumper, size 12. Fifty-one centimetres pit to pit." |
> | 10 to 15 | Close-up under the left arm in daylight | Bobbling under both arms | "Light bobbling under both arms. Shown here." |
> | 15 to 20 | Jumper folded into a mailer | Details in the listing | "Full details and photos in the listing." |

**Check before you post:**
- Watch it with the sound off. Does the on-screen text alone tell the truth about the item?
- Scan every frame for faces, reflections, post or house numbers.

### V2 Hands-only shot list and showcase ideas
**Use it when:** You know what the item is but not how to film it without a face or a model.
**Paste this:**
```text
I film products with my hands only, never faces. Give me 8 short shot ideas I can film in under 20 minutes for the item in my Listing Brief. UK English.

My kit: [PHONE ONLY / PHONE AND TRIPOD]
Where I can film: [E.G. KITCHEN TABLE BY A WINDOW, WHITE SHEET]

For each shot: name, how to set it up (one or two lines), what it proves to the buyer (size, texture, how it works, condition) and roughly how many seconds.

Rules:
- Hands, flat lays, item on a surface, hanger or mannequin only. No mirrors.
- At least one scale shot (common object or tape measure) and one clear flaw shot if the brief lists flaws.
- Only facts from the brief. A shot that depends on something not in the brief gets [CHECK: ...].
- Daylight or one steady light, no colour filters.
- No props that could look included unless the brief says they are.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**Check before you post:**
- Every [CHECK] is answered before you film the shots that depend on it.

### V3 Caption, on-screen text and hashtags for a product video
**Use it when:** You have filmed the video and need the words to go with it.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write the words for my product video in UK English.

My shots, in order: [E.G. "1. FLAT LAY 2. CLOSE-UP OF LABEL 3. FLAW UNDER ARM"]
Platform: [TIKTOK / INSTAGRAM / TIKTOK SHOP]

Give me:
1. On-screen text for each shot, 8 words or fewer, readable on a phone.
2. One caption under 150 characters: what it is, size or key spec, and condition in plain words.
3. 3 to 5 relevant hashtags (item type, its own brand, category).

Rules: only facts from my brief; [CHECK: ...] for anything missing; no hype, "best", health claims, fake urgency or emoji; no trending hashtags unrelated to the item, no platform names, no other brands.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For Levi's 501 jeans, W32 L30, mid-blue, button fly, light fraying at the back hems:

> 1. Levi's 501, W32 L30
> 2. Mid-blue, button fly
> 3. Light fraying at back hems
>
> **Caption:** Levi's 501 jeans, W32 L30, mid-blue. Light fraying on the back hems, shown in the video.
> **Hashtags:** #levis501 #secondhandjeans #denim

**Check before you post:**
- The caption mentions the flaw as clearly as the video shows it.

### V5 TikTok Shop UK product description
**Use it when:** You need a TikTok Shop product description that is clear, scannable on a phone and free of risky claims.
**Paste this:**
```text
Write a TikTok Shop UK product description in UK English for the product in my Listing Brief.

1. One opening sentence: what it is and who it is for.
2. 4 to 6 short feature lines: the feature, then the plain benefit. One fact per line.
3. "What's included", then "Size and specs" (dimensions, weight, capacity, materials), then "Care" if the brief gives it.

Rules:
- At least 30 words, aim for under 500 characters.
- Only facts from the brief. Missing facts become [CHECK: ...]. Never invent materials, certifications, dimensions or safety standards.
- No health or medical claims (cure, treat, prevent, relieve, detox, antibacterial), "clinically proven", "best", brand comparisons, guarantees or fake urgency.
- No vague green claims ("eco-friendly") without a specific, checkable fact.
- No other platforms or payment outside TikTok Shop. No emoji unless I ask.
- Then list every phrase that could count as a claim, so I can check it.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For an invented "Tidy Nook" bamboo drawer organiser set of 6 boxes in 3 sizes:

> Six bamboo boxes in three sizes for sorting cutlery, utensils or stationery in drawers.
>
> - Three sizes: mix and match to fit your drawer.
>
> What's included: 6 boxes (2 small, 2 medium, 2 large). Material: bamboo [CHECK: any lacquer or coating?].

**Check before you post:**
- Measure the product yourself and match the numbers exactly.

### V6 TikTok Shop claims check and safe rewrite
**Use it when:** You have product copy or a video script and want to catch risky claims before TikTok Shop or a buyer does.
**Paste this:**
```text
Check this TikTok Shop UK copy or video script line by line. UK English.

Table columns: Original wording, Why it might be a problem, Safer rewrite.

Flag anything that:
- makes or implies a health or medical claim (cure, heal, treat, prevent, relieve, detox, pain relief, anti-ageing results);
- makes a result or performance claim the brief does not support;
- uses "best", "number one", "better than" or compares with a named brand;
- uses fake urgency or a discount that is not real;
- makes vague green or safety claims (eco-friendly, non-toxic, 100% safe) without a fact in the brief;
- mentions TikTok or another platform in a title, or sends buyers elsewhere to pay;
- states a fact not in the brief (mark [CHECK: ...]).

Then give the full cleaned-up version, adding no new claims. This is a check, not legal advice.

Copy or script to check:
[PASTE TITLE, DESCRIPTION, CAPTION OR SCRIPT]

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a lavender wheat bag:

> | Original | Problem | Safer rewrite |
> |---|---|---|
> | "Relieves back pain and helps you sleep" | Health claim | "Microwaveable wheat bag with dried lavender." |
> | "100% natural and totally safe" | Vague safety claim | "Filled with wheat and dried lavender." |
> | "Only 3 left!!!" | Possible fake urgency, symbols | Remove |

**Check before you post:**
- Heated, electrical, children's and skin products often need extra documents. Check the category rules in Seller Center, and copy the real safety instructions from the label word for word.

### V7 Live-selling run-sheet
**Use it when:** You are planning a TikTok Shop LIVE, or any live selling session, and want a calm, honest plan without showing your face.
**Paste this:**
```text
Plan a faceless live selling session (hands and products only, voice or on-screen text). UK English.

Length: [30 / 45 / 60] minutes
Platform: [TIKTOK SHOP LIVE / OTHER]
Products (short brief for each: name, price, key facts, flaws):
[PASTE PRODUCTS]
A real offer I am running (only if genuine): [E.G. "BUY 2 GET FREE POSTAGE" OR "NONE"]

Run-sheet table: Time, Segment, On camera, What I say or show, Notes. Include a 2-minute welcome (what we are showing and how to buy through the platform's checkout), one segment per product (facts, flaws, scale, price), a questions slot every 10 to 15 minutes, and a 2-minute wrap-up.

Also give me:
- 6 short honest answers to common questions (size, postage time, returns, "is it genuine", "can you do cheaper", "can I pay another way"). Returns answers respect UK consumer rights and the platform's policy. Payment only through the platform.
- A "do not say" list: health claims, "best", fake countdowns or stock levels, invented discounts, other platform names, paying or messaging off the platform.

Only use facts I pasted. [CHECK: ...] for anything missing.
```
**Check before you post:**
- Any offer you mention is set up for real in Seller Center before you go live.
