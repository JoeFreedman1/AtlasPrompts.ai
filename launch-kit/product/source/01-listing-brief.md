# Module 01: The Listing Brief

Every good listing starts here. The Listing Brief is a short block of facts about one item. You fill it in once, paste it into your AI tool, and every other prompt in this kit works from it.

Why it matters: an AI tool can only be as accurate as what you give it. Give it "blue jumper, good condition" and it will fill the gaps with guesses. Give it a proper brief and it has nothing to guess.

The one habit that makes this work: **every prompt tells the AI to use only the facts in your brief, and to write [CHECK: ...] wherever something is missing.** You then fill in the checks yourself. No invented details, no surprises for the buyer.

---

## How to fill in a brief (in about 2 minutes)

1. Have the item in front of you, plus a tape measure.
2. Copy the brief that matches your item (general, or one of the category versions below).
3. Fill in what you know. Leave a line blank if you do not know it. Do not guess.
4. Write flaws in plain words: where, what, how big. "Small pale mark on left cuff, about 5mm" is perfect.
5. Save it in your notes app. You can reuse the same brief for every platform.

**Tip:** blank is better than wrong. A blank line becomes a [CHECK: ...] you can fix. A wrong line becomes a "not as described" return.

**Privacy tip:** a brief is about the item, never about people. Do not paste buyers' names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, order numbers or messages into an AI tool. If you need help with a buyer message, replace their details with [BUYER] first.

## The master Listing Brief (general)

Copy this block into your notes app and keep it as your template.

```text
LISTING BRIEF
Platform(s): [EBAY UK / VINTED / DEPOP / ETSY / AMAZON UK / TIKTOK SHOP]
What it is (plain words): 
Brand / maker: 
Model, style name or product number: 
Category: 
Size (as on the label): 
Measurements (cm): 
Colour (as you would describe it): 
Material / composition (from the label): 
Condition (one word): New with tags / New without tags / Excellent / Very good / Good / Fair
Flaws (where, what, how big): 
What is included (box, cables, manual, dust bag, spare buttons): 
What is NOT included: 
Age or era (only if known): 
Where it came from (only if relevant and true, e.g. "bought new from the brand in 2023"): 
Smoke-free / pet-free home (only if true): 
Postage (service and who pays): 
Price I want: 
Lowest I will accept (private, never shared): 
Anything else a buyer would ask: 
```

The "lowest I will accept" line is for the pricing and offer prompts later in the kit. Every prompt that writes buyer-facing copy is told never to reveal it.

## Category brief variants

Use the general brief for anything. Use a category version when you want the AI to ask the right questions for that type of item. Each variant adds the facts buyers in that category care about most.

### Clothing, shoes and accessories

For clothes, **measurements beat sizes.** Size 12 from a high street shop in 2024 is not the same as a size 12 from 1985, and vintage sizing runs small. Measure flat, in centimetres, and say how you measured.

```text
CLOTHING BRIEF
Platform(s): 
Item type (e.g. jumper, midi dress, straight-leg jeans): 
Brand: 
Size on label (UK / EU / US as printed): 
Fits like (only if you have tried it or compared it): 
Measurements, laid flat (cm):
  Pit to pit (armpit to armpit, across the front): 
  Length (top of shoulder by the collar to hem): 
  Sleeve (shoulder seam to cuff): 
  Waist (straight across, doubled if you want full waist): 
  Inside leg (crotch seam to hem): 
  Rise (crotch seam to top of waistband): 
  Hips / hem width: 
Colour: 
Fabric (copy the care label, e.g. 80% wool, 20% polyamide): 
Fit / cut (e.g. oversized, slim, cropped, high waisted): 
Pattern / details (buttons, pockets, lining, zip type): 
Condition: 
Flaws (bobbling, marks, pulls, fading, stretched cuffs, missing buttons, odour): 
Tags attached? (yes / no / cut label): 
Era (only if known, e.g. "tag style suggests 1990s" is fine, a guessed year is not): 
Smoke-free / pet-free home (only if true): 
Postage: 
Price: 
Lowest I will accept (private): 
```

**How to measure pit to pit:** lay the item flat, buttoned or zipped, smooth it without stretching, and measure straight across from one armpit seam to the other. Double it for an approximate chest circumference, and say you have doubled it.

### Electronics

Buyers want to know it works, what it comes with, and whether anything is locked or missing.

```text
ELECTRONICS BRIEF
Platform(s): 
Item type: 
Brand: 
Exact model name and model number (from the label or settings screen): 
Storage / capacity / specification (only from the label, box or settings): 
Colour: 
Tested? How? (e.g. "powered on, connected to Wi-Fi, played a video for 10 minutes"): 
Anything not tested (e.g. "Bluetooth not tested"): 
Faults or quirks: 
Battery (health figure if shown, or "not checked"): 
Cosmetic condition (scratches, dents, screen marks, where and how big): 
Reset to factory settings? (yes / no): 
Accounts removed? (e.g. signed out of the account linked to the device, activation lock off): 
Network lock (phones only: unlocked / locked to [NETWORK] / not checked): 
Included (charger, cable, box, remote, manual): 
Not included: 
Plug type (UK 3-pin / other): 
Postage: 
Price: 
Lowest I will accept (private): 
```

**Tip:** never describe electronics as "fully working" unless you have tested every function. "Tested and working: power, screen, Wi-Fi, speakers. Not tested: Bluetooth" is honest and protects you.

### Books, music and media

```text
BOOKS AND MEDIA BRIEF
Platform(s): 
Format (hardback, paperback, vinyl LP, 7-inch single, CD, DVD, Blu-ray, video game): 
Title: 
Author / artist / publisher / label: 
Edition and printing (copy from the copyright page or label, e.g. "first edition, fourth impression"): 
Year of this copy (from the copyright page, not the first publication date unless it is the first printing): 
ISBN / catalogue number / barcode: 
Region (DVD, Blu-ray, games, if shown): 
Signed or inscribed? (only if true, say which and where): 
Condition of item (pages, spine, disc, vinyl surface): 
Condition of cover / sleeve / case: 
Flaws (foxing, inscriptions, price stickers, creases, scratches, ring wear): 
Tested? (e.g. "disc plays without skipping", "record played through side A only"): 
Included (inserts, dust jacket, poster, manual, codes, only if checked): 
Postage (large letter / small parcel, and whether packed in a board-backed envelope or box): 
Price: 
Lowest I will accept (private): 
```

**Tip:** "first edition" is one of the most misused phrases in book listings. Only use it if the copyright page supports it, and copy the exact wording.

### Homeware

```text
HOMEWARE BRIEF
Platform(s): 
Item type (e.g. table lamp, casserole dish, cushion cover, vase): 
Brand / maker / backstamp (copy any marks on the base): 
Pattern or range name (only if marked or confirmed): 
Material: 
Colour: 
Dimensions (cm: height, width, depth, diameter, capacity in litres if relevant): 
Weight (if heavy, affects postage): 
Condition: 
Flaws (chips, crazing, hairline cracks, scratches, stains, wobble): 
Safe for oven / dishwasher / microwave? (only if the base or box says so): 
Electrical? (UK plug, tested, how): 
Set or single? (how many pieces, all matching?): 
Smoke-free / pet-free home (only if true): 
Postage (fragile: how you will pack it): 
Price: 
Lowest I will accept (private): 
```

**Tip:** run a fingertip round the rim of any glass or china and hold it up to the light. Hairline cracks and tiny chips are the most common cause of "not as described" claims in homeware.

### Toys, games and collectables

```text
TOYS AND COLLECTABLES BRIEF
Platform(s): 
Item type: 
Brand / manufacturer: 
Name, set number or series (from the box or item): 
Year (only if printed on the item or box): 
Complete? (list what is present, and what is missing): 
Pieces counted? (yes, and how many / no): 
Box (present, condition, sealed or opened): 
Instructions / leaflets (present / not present): 
Condition: 
Flaws (paint wear, sun fading, broken parts, sticker wear, battery corrosion): 
Batteries (included / not included / compartment clean?): 
Tested? (lights, sounds, motors): 
Age guidance on box (copy it exactly, e.g. "not suitable for children under 36 months"): 
Smoke-free / pet-free home (only if true): 
Postage: 
Price: 
Lowest I will accept (private): 
```

**Tip:** never call something "rare" or "limited edition" unless the item or box says so. Collectors check, and it is a misleading claim if it is not true.

### Handmade (Etsy and small makers)

```text
HANDMADE BRIEF
Platform(s): 
What it is: 
Who makes it (your shop, "made by us"): 
Materials (every material, including findings, thread, finish, glaze): 
Size and dimensions (cm or mm): 
Weight: 
Colours available: 
Personalisation options (what can change, character limits, font choices): 
Made to order or ready to post?: 
Time to make (days): 
Care instructions: 
Allergy information (e.g. "nickel-free" only if your supplier confirms it): 
Packaging (gift wrap, plastic-free, only if true): 
Each piece varies? (e.g. hand-dyed so colour varies slightly): 
Safety notes (e.g. "not a toy", "candle: never leave burning unattended"): 
Postage (service, dispatch time): 
Price: 
```

**Tip:** for handmade items, be careful with safety and health wording. "Soothing" candles and "healing" crystals are health claims. Describe what the item is and what it is made from, not what it does to the body.

## The B prompts

These prompts help you build a brief from whatever you have: scribbled notes, a voice note, a photo description, or a product label. The AI's only job here is to organise facts and flag gaps. It does not write the listing yet.

### B1 Messy notes to Listing Brief
**Use it when:** you have jotted down a few scrappy notes about an item and want them turned into a tidy brief.
**Paste this:**
```text
You are helping a UK second-hand seller organise facts about one item. Turn my notes below into a Listing Brief using exactly these headings:

Platform(s), What it is, Brand / maker, Model or style, Category, Size (as on label), Measurements (cm), Colour, Material, Condition, Flaws, What is included, What is not included, Age or era, Postage, Price, Anything else.

Rules:
1. Use UK English spelling and vocabulary (colour, jumper, trousers, postage).
2. Use ONLY facts that are in my notes. Do not add, assume or improve anything.
3. If a heading has no information, write [CHECK: what I need to find out]. Be specific, e.g. [CHECK: measure pit to pit in cm].
4. Keep my flaw descriptions exactly as serious as I wrote them. Do not soften or remove any flaw.
5. Convert inches to cm only if I gave inches, and show both (e.g. 21 in / 53 cm).
6. No sales language. This is a fact sheet, not an advert.
7. At the end, list the 3 most important [CHECK] items a buyer in this category would ask about.

My notes:
[PASTE YOUR NOTES HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For the notes "grey next jumper sz12 wool mix, bit bobbly under arms, vinted, want 12 quid":

> **Platform(s):** Vinted
> **What it is:** Jumper
> **Brand / maker:** Next
> **Model or style:** [CHECK: style, e.g. crew neck, V-neck, roll neck]
> **Size (as on label):** 12
> **Measurements (cm):** [CHECK: measure pit to pit, length and sleeve, laid flat]
> **Colour:** Grey
> **Material:** Wool blend [CHECK: exact composition from the care label]
> **Condition:** [CHECK: choose one word, e.g. Good]
> **Flaws:** Some bobbling under the arms
> **Price:** £12
> ...
> **Top 3 checks:** exact fabric composition, measurements, whether there are any marks or holes besides the bobbling.

**Check before you post:**
- Fill in every [CHECK] with a real fact, or delete the line if it does not apply.
- Make sure the AI has not quietly upgraded your wording (for example "wool mix" becoming "merino").
- Keep your lowest price out of anything a buyer will see.

### B2 Voice note transcript to Listing Brief
**Use it when:** you talked about the item into your phone's voice recorder (quicker than typing) and have the transcript.
**Paste this:**
```text
Below is a transcript of me talking about an item I am selling in the UK. It will be rambling, with "um", repeats and corrections. Turn it into a Listing Brief.

Headings: Platform(s), What it is, Brand / maker, Model or style, Size, Measurements (cm), Colour, Material, Condition, Flaws, What is included, What is not included, Tested (how), Postage, Price, Anything else.

Rules:
1. UK English.
2. Where I corrected myself ("no wait, it's a 14"), use the correction only.
3. Use ONLY facts I actually said. Do not fill gaps with typical details for this type of item.
4. If something is missing or unclear, write [CHECK: ...] and say what was unclear.
5. Voice transcription often mishears brand names and numbers. If a brand, model number or measurement looks odd, keep it but add [CHECK: transcript said "X", confirm spelling].
6. Keep every flaw I mentioned, at the same level of seriousness.
7. No sales language.

Transcript:
[PASTE TRANSCRIPT HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a transcript that includes "it's a Dualit toaster, um, four slice, cream, works fine, I did two rounds of toast, there's a little dent on the back, no, the side, left side, and the crumb tray's a bit stained":

> **What it is:** Four-slice toaster
> **Brand / maker:** Dualit [CHECK: model name or number from the base label]
> **Colour:** Cream
> **Tested (how):** Toasted two rounds of bread
> **Flaws:** Small dent on the left side [CHECK: size in mm or cm]. Crumb tray has some staining.
> **What is included:** [CHECK: is the crumb tray the only removable part? Any manual or box?]
> **Anything else:** [CHECK: plug type, assume nothing]

**Check before you post:**
- Brand names and model numbers are the words transcription gets wrong most often. Check them against the label.
- Confirm the corrected details (left side, not back) are the ones that made it in.
- Photograph any flaw the brief mentions.

### B3 Photo description to Listing Brief
**Use it when:** you want to describe what you can see in your photos (or on the item) and turn it into a brief. This prompt does not need the AI to see images, so it works in any free tool.
**Paste this:**
```text
I am going to describe my item photo by photo, as if I am showing it to you. Build a UK Listing Brief from my descriptions only.

Headings: What it is, Brand / maker, Model or style, Size, Measurements (cm), Colour, Material, Condition, Flaws (with location), Labels and markings (copy exact wording), What is included, Postage, Price.

Rules:
1. UK English.
2. Use only what I describe. Do not guess brand, age, material or value from the type of item.
3. For each flaw, record where it is and how big, if I said so. If I did not give a size, add [CHECK: size of flaw].
4. After the brief, give me a list of any extra photos a buyer would expect to see that I have not described (for example care label, base stamp, flaw close-up).
5. No sales language.

My photo descriptions:
Photo 1: [DESCRIBE]
Photo 2: [DESCRIBE]
Photo 3: [DESCRIBE]
Price: [PRICE]
Postage: [POSTAGE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a ceramic jug described across three photos:

> **What it is:** Ceramic milk jug
> **Brand / maker:** Base stamp reads "Denby, Made in England" (copied as described)
> **Colour:** Blue glaze outside, cream inside
> **Measurements (cm):** 12 cm tall [CHECK: width including handle]
> **Flaws:** Tiny glaze chip on the rim, next to the spout [CHECK: size in mm]
> **Labels and markings:** "Denby, Made in England"
> **Extra photos to take:** close-up of the rim chip with a coin or ruler for scale, inside of the jug, base stamp in focus.

**Check before you post:**
- If you are unsure a stamp is genuine, describe what it says rather than stating who made it.
- Take the extra flaw photos the AI lists. A flaw shown in a photo is much harder to dispute.
- Confirm measurements with a tape, not by eye.

### B4 Gap finder: what a buyer will ask
**Use it when:** you have a brief and want to catch the questions buyers will message you about, before they ask.
**Paste this:**
```text
Here is a Listing Brief for an item I am selling on [PLATFORM] in the UK. Act as a careful, fussy buyer in this category.

1. List up to 8 questions a serious buyer would ask before buying, most important first.
2. For each, say whether my brief already answers it (Yes / No / Partly).
3. For every No or Partly, tell me exactly what to check or measure, in plain words, and how to do it quickly.
4. Do not write the listing. Do not invent answers.
5. UK English, short lines, suitable for reading on a phone.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a pair of Levi's 501 jeans on eBay UK:

> | Question | Answered? | What to do |
> |---|---|---|
> | What are the real waist and inside leg? | Partly | Lay flat, measure straight across the top of the waistband and double it. Measure inside leg from crotch seam to hem. |
> | Button fly or zip? | No | Check and add. |
> | Any wear at the crotch or hems? | No | Look closely at the inner thighs and back hems. Photograph any thinning. |
> | Is it the original hem or taken up? | No | Look for a factory chain-stitch hem versus a home-sewn one. If unsure, say "unsure". |

**Check before you post:**
- Add the answers to your brief, not just to the listing, so every platform version gets them.
- "Unsure" is a valid honest answer. Guessing is not.
- If the same question keeps coming up for a category, add it to your brief template.

### B5 Build my own category brief
**Use it when:** you sell a lot of one thing (bikes, prams, golf clubs, perfume, board games) and want a brief template tailored to it.
**Paste this:**
```text
I sell second-hand [CATEGORY] in the UK on [PLATFORM(S)]. Create a reusable Listing Brief template for this category.

Include:
1. The general fields: platform, what it is, brand, model, size or dimensions, colour, material, condition, flaws, what is included, postage, price, lowest I will accept (private).
2. Up to 10 extra fields that buyers of this category specifically care about, with a short hint in brackets on how to find each fact (e.g. "frame size (printed on the seat tube)").
3. A short "safety and legal" note listing anything I should check before selling this category in the UK, phrased as questions for me to check with the platform's help pages. Do not state rules as fact.

Rules: UK English, plain words, no sales language, formatted as a plain text block I can copy into my notes app.
```
**What good output looks like:**

For children's car seats:

> CAR SEAT BRIEF
> ...
> Safety standard label (copy the exact wording from the orange or white label on the seat):
> Group / size range on the label (e.g. height range in cm):
> Date of manufacture (usually moulded into the plastic shell):
> Ever been in a vehicle collision? (yes / no / unknown):
> Isofix or belt fitted:
> Covers washed? (yes / no):
> ...
> Safety and legal questions to check: Does the platform allow second-hand car seats in this category? Are there any rules on seats with no known history?

**Check before you post:**
- Some categories are restricted or banned on some platforms. Check the platform's prohibited and restricted items page before listing.
- Save the template and use it for every item in that category.
- If a field does not apply to one item, delete it rather than leave it blank in the listing.

### B6 Label or box text to Listing Brief
**Use it when:** you have a new or boxed item (or a clear product label) and want to pull the facts off it accurately.
**Paste this:**
```text
Below is text I have copied from the box, label or manual of an item I am selling in the UK. Extract a Listing Brief from it.

Headings: What it is, Brand, Model / product number, Specification (list), Colour, Material, Dimensions, What is in the box, Safety or age warnings (copied exactly), Condition, Flaws, Postage, Price.

Rules:
1. UK English.
2. Copy technical specifications exactly as written. Do not round, convert or "tidy" numbers.
3. Do not include marketing claims from the box (e.g. "best ever", "clinically proven") as facts. Leave them out and list them separately under "Box claims I have not included" so I can decide.
4. Condition, flaws, postage and price are not on the box: write [CHECK: ...] for them unless I add them below.
5. Copy any safety warnings or age guidance word for word.

Box or label text:
[PASTE TEXT]

My extra details (condition, flaws, whether opened, postage, price):
[ADD DETAILS]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a boxed electric toothbrush:

> **What it is:** Electric toothbrush
> **Brand:** [as printed]
> **Model / product number:** [as printed]
> **What is in the box:** 1 handle, 1 brush head, 1 charging base
> **Condition:** New, box opened to check contents, brush head still sealed
> **Box claims I have not included:** "removes up to 100% more plaque". This is a manufacturer claim. Leave it out of your listing unless you are the brand and can support it.

**Check before you post:**
- Leave out health, performance and "clinically proven" style claims unless you are the brand and can back them up.
- Check whether the item's category has hygiene rules on the platform (opened personal care items often do).
- Photograph the box and label so buyers can read the model number.

### B7 Brief tidy-up and consistency check
**Use it when:** your brief is filled in and you want a final sanity check before you run the title and description prompts.
**Paste this:**
```text
Check my Listing Brief for a UK [PLATFORM] listing. Do not rewrite it into a listing.

Report back, in this order:
1. Contradictions (e.g. condition "Excellent" but flaws listed that sound like "Good").
2. Vague wording a buyer could dispute (e.g. "small mark" with no location or size). Suggest a clearer version using only my facts.
3. Missing facts buyers in this category expect, as [CHECK: ...] lines.
4. Anything that could be a misleading or risky claim (e.g. "rare", "genuine", "fully working", "vintage" without an era, health claims). Explain why in one line each.
5. US spellings or sizes to change to UK ones.

UK English. Short bullet points. Do not invent facts to fix problems. Ask me instead.

My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF HERE]
```
**What good output looks like:**

For a brief for a Radley handbag:

> **Contradictions:** Condition says "Excellent" but flaws mention "corners rubbed through to the lining". That is closer to Good or Fair. Consider changing the condition.
> **Vague wording:** "small mark inside" could become "small blue pen mark on the inside lining, about 1 cm, near the zip pocket" [CHECK: confirm colour and size].
> **Missing facts:** [CHECK: dimensions in cm], [CHECK: strap drop in cm], [CHECK: dust bag included?]
> **Risky claims:** "100% genuine": only say this if you can back it up (receipt, bought from the brand). Otherwise just state the brand and show the labels.
> **US to UK:** "purse" to "handbag" or "bag".

**Check before you post:**
- Fix contradictions in the brief itself so every platform version inherits the fix.
- If the AI flags "genuine" or "authentic", keep the photos of labels, codes and receipts ready.
- Run this check on your first few briefs until the habit sticks.

## Quick reference: what makes a strong brief

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Size M | Size M on label. Pit to pit 54 cm, length 68 cm, laid flat |
| Good condition | Good. Light bobbling under both arms. No holes or marks |
| Small mark | Pale mark on front, left of the buttons, about 5 mm |
| Works | Tested: powers on, connects to Wi-Fi, all buttons respond. Bluetooth not tested |
| Vintage | Care label and tag style suggest 1990s (not confirmed) |
| Genuine | Brand labels shown in photos. Receipt available [only if true] |
| Comes with stuff | Included: charger, original box. Not included: manual |
