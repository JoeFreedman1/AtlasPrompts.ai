THE WELL LISTED KIT: READ ME FIRST

Thank you for buying the kit. Here is what is in this folder and where to start.

1. prompt-library.html
   Open this on your phone or computer (it works without internet once downloaded).
   Every prompt has a two-digit ID (A01, M08), the same in the guide and the library.
   Search for what you need (for example "A01", "lowball" or "eBay title"), fill in the
   [SQUARE BRACKETS], press "Copy prompt" and paste it into a free AI chat tool
   such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Microsoft Copilot.
   On iPhone: save the file to the Files app, then tap it to open.
   On Android: open it from your Downloads folder with Chrome.

2. Well-Listed-Kit.pdf
   The full guide. Start with "Quick start" on the first page of Module 00: one
   all-in-one prompt per platform (A01 Vinted, A02 eBay UK, A03 Depop, A04 Etsy,
   A05 Amazon UK, A06 TikTok Shop UK). You do not need to read the rest first:
   dip into the module you need using the "Which prompt do I need?" table.
   Makers: start with A04, then C11 (safety details) and C10 (returns wording).

3. all-prompts.txt
   Every prompt as plain text, handy for copying into your notes app.

4. templates folder
   - listing-brief-template.txt / .csv : the fill-in Listing Brief
   - stock-and-pricing-tracker.csv : opens in Google Sheets, Excel or Numbers
     (the example Profit cells work themselves out: copy one down)
   - message-snippets.txt : quick replies for common buyer messages
   - photo-shot-lists.md : what to photograph for each type of item

   Opening the .csv files in Google Sheets:
   - On a computer: go to sheets.google.com, start a blank sheet, then choose
     File, Import, Upload, and pick the .csv file.
   - On a phone: tapping a .csv often shows a preview you cannot edit. Upload the
     file to Google Drive, then open it with Google Sheets. If it still will not
     edit, do the import once on a computer and use the sheet on your phone after.
   - Excel and Numbers open the files directly on a computer.

Three rules that make every prompt work better:
- Give the AI facts, not hopes: fill in the brackets or the Listing Brief first.
- Never paste a buyer's name, address or order details into an AI tool.
- Read every result before you post it. You are the seller; the AI is the typist.

Platform rules change often. Check the platform's own help pages if in doubt.
