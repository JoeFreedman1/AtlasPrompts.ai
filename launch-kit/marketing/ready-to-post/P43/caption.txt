Six phrases that can get a listing removed, and what to write instead.

Counterfeit wording is the obvious one, but the quieter problems are claims you cannot back up. "100% genuine" with nothing to show for it. "Relieves back pain" on a wheat bag. "Fully working" on a console you only switched on.

The fix is the same every time: describe what you know. Where you bought it, what is included, what you tested, what the label says.

TikTok Shop UK's own policies say claims must be accurate and must not suggest a product cures, treats or relieves a condition. Other platforms have their own rules on counterfeits and claims, so check the current rule in each platform's help pages.
