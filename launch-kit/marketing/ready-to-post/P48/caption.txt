One handmade tealight holder, 13 Etsy tags, every one counted. No repeats, and the longest is exactly 20 characters.

This is an invented example to show what our Etsy prompts produce. The brief goes in (size, glaze, how it is made, what fits), and out come a readable title and 13 tags that each add something new.

Two things we always check:
1. Every tag is 20 characters or fewer. "wheel thrown pottery" is exactly 20.
2. Nothing claims what you have not tested. A safety line ("never leave a lit candle unattended") is fine. "Fireproof" is not, unless you can prove it.

Etsy's title and tag limits are widely reported as 140 characters and 13 tags, so check the current rule in Etsy's help pages. Save this, and the free UK Listing Cheat Sheet is in our bio.
