Item Specifics Checklist for Clothing Listings on eBay UK

DESCRIPTION:
A quick item specifics checklist for clothing on eBay UK: brand, size type, size, colour, material, style, fit, sleeve length and pattern. Fill them in from the label, not from memory, and leave out anything you cannot confirm.
