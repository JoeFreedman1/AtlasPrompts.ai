Five hashtags on a plain high street top, and four of them are not true.

Depop's help pages say you can use up to 5 hashtags, and to keep them to relevant words, brands and tags (check the current guidance in Depop's help pages).

So:
Your brand only. Other brands' names on your item are keyword spam.
No "vintage" or "y2k" unless the item really is, or clearly says "style".
Use the five on what a buyer would search: brand, item, colour, fabric, style.

Example: Primark white cotton babydoll top, UK 10. Pit to pit 46 cm, length 52 cm. Worn twice, no marks.
