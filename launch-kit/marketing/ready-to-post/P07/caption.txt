"Minor signs of wear" means something different to every buyer. That gap is where "not as described" starts.

Write each flaw as where, what, how big, and which photo shows it:
"Pale mark on front, left of the buttons, about 5 mm (photo 5)."

For electronics, list what you tested and what you did not:
"Tested: powers on, Wi-Fi connects, all buttons respond. Bluetooth not tested."

If you use AI to tidy your notes, add: "Keep every flaw as serious as I wrote it. Do not soften anything." Otherwise it may quietly turn "hole" into "tiny imperfection".
