Monday: "Is this still available?" Tuesday: "£5?" A typical week of buyer messages, sorted with four prompts.

These are invented messages, but you will recognise every one.

Monday, availability: answer yes and add the detail they will ask next.
Tuesday, lowball: counter politely at a figure you set in advance. Never below your floor.
Thursday, bundle: name the items, give one price, explain the postage saving.
Saturday, late parcel: share the tracking status, stay calm, say what happens next.
