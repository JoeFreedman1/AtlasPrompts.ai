Same boots, same scuff, two listings. One is far more likely to end in a return.

Listing A says "great condition" and every photo is from the good side. The buyer finds the scuff when they open the box, and they have a point.

Listing B says: "Good condition. Small scuff on the outer left heel, about 1 cm (photo 5). Light creasing across both toes."

B might get a question. A risks a "not as described" claim. Platforms' buyer protection generally covers items that are significantly not as described, so hiding a flaw rarely ends well.

Photograph it close up in daylight with a coin for scale. Save this.
