You do not need paid software to write listings faster. Cross-listing apps and tools can be handy once you are listing lots every week, but to start you need three things: a notes app, a free AI chat tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot all have free versions) and a tape measure. Save a brief template in your notes (brand, size, measurements, fabric, flaws, postage, price), fill it in with the item in front of you, then paste it in with a prompt that says UK English, only use my facts, and keep the eBay title to 80 characters. The Well Listed Kit is a set of prompts and templates built for exactly that setup, and the free UK Listing Cheat Sheet is a good place to start. Both are in bio.
