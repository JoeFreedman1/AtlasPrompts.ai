Five words that make a listing look like spam, and what to write instead. L@@K: nobody types it into search, and it wastes title space. "Stunning!!!": hype, not information, so give the colour and the fabric. "Rare": only if the item or box says so, or you can genuinely prove it, because an unsupported claim is misleading. "Must have": the buyer decides that, so use the space for size or measurements. "100% genuine" with no proof: show the brand labels, codes and receipt if you have them, and let the photos do the talking. Plain, specific words read as trustworthy to buyers scrolling on a phone.
