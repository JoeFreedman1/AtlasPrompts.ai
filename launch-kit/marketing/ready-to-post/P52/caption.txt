One column shows which of ten listings still need a measurement or a photo. This is the spreadsheet version of the batch method.

1. Start the chat with the rules: UK English, the platform, the title limit, and "write [CHECK] if a fact is missing".
2. Paste ten short item notes, numbered, one per line.
3. Ask for one row per item with columns for title, description, condition, price and CHECK.
4. Ask for comma-separated text, paste it into Google Sheets or Excel and split it into columns.

The check column is the useful bit. You can see at a glance which items still need a measurement or a photo before they go live.

Read every row yourself before listing. The AI drafts, you decide.
