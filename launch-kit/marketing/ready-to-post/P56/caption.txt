Weigh the parcel, not the item. Postage lessons new sellers tend to learn the hard way.

1. Weigh it packed. Box, bubble wrap and tape all add grams.
2. Measure the finished parcel. Size bands matter as much as weight, so check the carrier's current limits.
3. Get proof of postage every time. It is your evidence if something goes missing.
4. Packaging costs money. Include it before you agree a price or accept an offer.
5. Only promise a dispatch time you can keep.

Many apps have their own postage options and prepaid labels, and the rules differ. Check the current postage options in each platform's help pages.
