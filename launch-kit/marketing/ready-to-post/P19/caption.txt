"Would you do all 3 for £10?" Answer with a number, not a sigh.

Before you reply, do two sums:
1. Your list prices added up, and your floors (the lowest you would take for each) added up.
2. The postage saving from sending one parcel instead of three.

Then counter at a price between the two, never below the floors added together, and give the postage saving as the reason. It is true, it is friendly, and it gives the buyer something to say yes to.

"Thanks for asking. I can do all three for £19. They go in one parcel, so it saves on postage for both of us."
