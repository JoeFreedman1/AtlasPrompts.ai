"Nice jumper" is a title nobody has ever typed into a search bar.

eBay UK gives you 80 characters (at the time of writing). Use them for the words a buyer would actually type:
Brand, who it is for, colour, pattern, fabric, style, item type, size, one detail.

Before: Nice jumper (11)
After: Seasalt Womens Navy Striped Cotton Breton Jumper Size 14 Crew Neck Long Sleeve (78)

Every word has to be true: fabric from the care label, size from the label, "crew neck" only if it is one. Leave "nice", "stunning" and "L@@K" out. Buyers do not search for them.
