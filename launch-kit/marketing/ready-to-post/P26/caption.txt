Myth: using AI to write listings gets you banned. eBay, Depop and Amazon all offer their own AI listing tools, so AI help in itself is not the issue. What causes problems is the content: invented details, claims you cannot support ("rare", "genuine", "fully working"), keyword spam, and photos edited so they no longer show the real item. Our three checks for any AI draft: is every fact from my actual item, is every claim provable, and is this exactly what the buyer will get? Etsy has specific rules for AI-generated or AI-assisted items and how to disclose them, so read those if you sell there. Rules change, so check the current rules in each platform's help pages.
