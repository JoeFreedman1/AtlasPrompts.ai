For the evenings when there is a pile on the bed and no energy left. Four prompts that do the tiring bit. One: record a voice note about each item, paste the transcript and ask for a Listing Brief, using your corrections only. Two: titles only, three items at a time, 80 characters each for eBay. Three: draft the descriptions tonight and mark anything uncertain with [CHECK] so you fix it in daylight, with the item in your hands. Four: a shot list per item, so tomorrow's photos are quick. Nothing gets posted until you have checked it. The full versions are in The Well Listed Kit (link in bio), or save this and use the short versions tonight.
