"Is this still available?" "Yes." Then nothing. Sound familiar?

A plain "yes" answers the question but gives the buyer nothing to reply to. Try three parts instead:
1. Yes, still available.
2. One useful fact (size, measurement, or the thing buyers usually ask about this item).
3. When it will be posted, plus "any questions, just ask".

"Yes, it is still available. It is a size 12, pit to pit 52 cm, and I can post it within 2 days of purchase. Any questions, just ask."

No "someone else is interested", no rushing. Only promise a dispatch time you can keep. Save it as a quick reply.
