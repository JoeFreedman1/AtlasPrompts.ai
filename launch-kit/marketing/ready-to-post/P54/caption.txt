Things nobody tells you when you start selling on Vinted, starting with who pays the fee.

1. The Buyer Protection fee is added at checkout and paid by the buyer. Vinted says the amount varies.
2. Your first photo should show the whole item. Use your own photos, no stock images.
3. You can add up to 20 photos, so use them for labels and flaws.
4. Measurements in cm answer the most common question before it is asked.
5. Lowball offers are part of the app. Know your lowest price before you list.
6. If you sell as a business, Vinted says to use Vinted Pro, which comes with its own returns rules.
