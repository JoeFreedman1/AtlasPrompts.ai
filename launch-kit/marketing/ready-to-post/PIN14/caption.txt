TITLE:
Sold Comps Pricing Worksheet for eBay and Vinted Sellers

DESCRIPTION:
Price from evidence, not memory. On this sold comps worksheet, note five to ten matching sold listings, their condition and what was included, drop the odd ones out, then set a price range. Keeps pricing on eBay and Vinted calm and consistent. Printable reseller organisation idea.
