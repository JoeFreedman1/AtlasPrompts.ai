This TikTok Shop description has 5 problems. How many did you spot?

Invented example, a microfibre hair towel wrap:
"The best hair towel on TikTok! Anti-bacterial, dermatologist approved, stops frizz forever. Only 3 left!!!"

What the rule checker flagged:
1. "Anti-bacterial": needs evidence.
2. "Dermatologist approved": needs proof.
3. "Best" and a platform name.
4. "Only 3 left" when that is not true: fake urgency.
5. "Stops frizz forever": a result nobody can promise.

The rewrite: "Microfibre hair towel wrap with a button loop. 65 x 25 cm, grey. Machine washable at 30 degrees."
