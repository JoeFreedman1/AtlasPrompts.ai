Three problems in one TikTok Shop description, and none of them are spelling. "Relieves back pain" is a health claim: TikTok Shop UK's own policies say products must not claim to cure, treat, prevent or relieve a disease or medical condition, in the listing or the video. "100% natural and totally safe" is a claim you cannot back up with a checkable fact. "Only 3 left" is fake urgency unless it is actually true. The fix is plain: say what it is and what it is made from, and let the label handle the instructions. Rules change, so check the current policy in TikTok Shop Seller Center before you list.
