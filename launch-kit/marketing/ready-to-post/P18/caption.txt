Sweater, pants, fall, ships fast. If your AI-written listing sounds like this, UK buyers can tell.

Free AI tools often default to American English, and when they are short of facts they fill the gaps: "merino" instead of wool blend, a US size instead of the label size, "gorgeous" instead of anything useful.

Two fixes:
1. Say "UK English and UK sizes" in every prompt (jumper, trousers, trainers, postage).
2. Give it a Listing Brief and say "use only these facts. Write [CHECK] if something is missing."
