"Good condition, fits true to size, open to offers." Three phrases, zero facts.

Good compared to what? True to which size? And "open to offers" on its own tends to invite the lowest possible number.

The fix is facts in short lines:
Whistles wool-blend coat, size 12 on label.
Pit to pit 54 cm, length 98 cm, laid flat.
Very good: light bobbling on pocket edges (photo 6). Lining intact.

Same coat. Now a buyer can decide without sending you a message first.
