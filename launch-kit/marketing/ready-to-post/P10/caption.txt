A size 12 from 1985 is not a size 12 today, and vintage sizing often runs small. Measurements settle it.

Tops: pit to pit (lay flat, buttoned, armpit seam to armpit seam) and length (top of shoulder by the collar to hem).
Trousers and jeans: waist straight across, inside leg (crotch seam to hem), and rise for high-waisted styles.
Coats: add sleeve (shoulder seam to cuff).

Always centimetres, always laid flat, and say how you measured. If you doubled a flat measurement to get a full chest or waist, say you doubled it.

Save this, and keep a tape measure where you take your photos.
