TITLE:
Words to Keep Out of Your TikTok Shop Product Title (UK)

DESCRIPTION:
Keep these out of TikTok Shop UK product titles: discount wording like "50% off", your shop name or a web address, other platform names, and decorative symbols. An accurate, plain title is easier to trust. Check TikTok Shop's current UK rules.
