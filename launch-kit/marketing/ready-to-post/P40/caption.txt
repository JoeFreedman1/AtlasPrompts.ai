You do not need a paid AI plan to write good listings. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot all have free versions, and any of them can turn a proper Listing Brief into a UK title and description. Some platforms also build it in: the eBay app can draft a listing from a photo, Depop has a Generate Description option, and Amazon offers AI listing tools to UK sellers. Whichever you use, check three things before posting: every fact is from your actual item, it is in UK English, and it fits the character limit. Free plans and features change, so check what each tool includes today and check the current rules in each platform's help pages.
