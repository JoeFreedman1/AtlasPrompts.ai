A photo of a screen switched off tells a buyer nothing. For electronics, these six photos answer most questions before they are asked: the front switched on, the back and all four corners, every port close up, the model label or settings screen showing the exact model, everything included laid out together, and every scratch with a coin for scale. Then say in the description exactly what you tested and how ("powers on, connects to Wi-Fi, played video for 10 minutes; Bluetooth not tested"). Avoid "fully working" unless you have tested every function. Before photographing a settings screen, check it does not show your name, account or location. Save this for your next gadget.
