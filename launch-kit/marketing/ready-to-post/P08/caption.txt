Same jacket, three platforms, three different listings. Pasting identical text everywhere wastes what each platform is good at.

eBay UK: the title does the heavy lifting, so use the space (up to 80 characters at the time of writing).
Vinted: short, clear title and fill in the brand, size and colour fields.
Depop: the opening line works like a title, then up to 5 relevant hashtags (Depop's help pages say 5).

The shortcut: write one Listing Brief, then ask for all three versions from the same facts, so nothing changes between them except the style.

Note the "90s style" wording. The era is not confirmed, so it is not claimed.
