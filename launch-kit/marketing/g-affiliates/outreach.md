# Creator outreach messages

Three short starting points. **They are templates, not copy to paste.** Every message must be rewritten for the person, with a specific, genuine reference to something they actually made.

## Read before sending anything

- **Personalise every message.** Watch, read or listen to their recent content first. Mention one specific, real thing: a video title, a tip they gave, an item they listed, a point they made. If you cannot find something you genuinely liked or found useful, do not message them.
- **Send one at a time.** No mass messaging, no mail merge, no copy-paste to a list.
- **Maximum 5 a day.** Fewer is fine.
- **Use the contact route they publish** for business or collaboration enquiries (email in their bio or channel "About" page, a contact form, or the platform's business inbox). Never message personal accounts, family members, or private contact details found elsewhere. No bought or scraped lists. (Sole traders are individuals under UK email marketing rules, so keep this to genuine one-to-one business enquiries through their published route, and check the ICO's guidance on direct marketing if unsure.)
- **Respect "no".** If they say no, or not now, thank them, delete their details (unless they ask us to keep in touch) and never contact them again about this.
- **Follow up once at most.** If there is no reply, you may send **one** short, polite follow-up after at least 7 days. After that, stop.
- **Faceless and honest.** Sign as "The Well Listed team". Never invent a person's name, and never claim sales figures, reviews or popularity we do not have.
- **No income claims** in outreach either: do not tell them what they "could earn" from commission. Give the percentage only.
- Attach or link `creator-brief.md` (as a PDF or a simple web page) once they reply with interest, not in the first message, unless their contact page asks for full details upfront.

Fill in everything in [SQUARE BRACKETS]. Delete any line that is not true for them.

## 1. A small UK reseller TikTok creator

**Where:** the business email in their TikTok bio, or TikTok's business messages if they have them switched on. Not comments.

**Subject (for email):** Free kit for [HANDLE], no strings

> Hi [NAME OR HANDLE],
>
> We are Well Listed, a small UK brand making AI prompts and checklists for Vinted and eBay sellers. [ONE SPECIFIC, GENUINE REFERENCE, e.g. "Your video on measuring pit to pit before listing was the clearest one we have seen, especially the tip about laying it flat on the bed."]
>
> We would love to send you The Well Listed Kit free to try on your own listings. No obligation to post, and if you do, it is your honest opinion in your own style.
>
> If you like it and want to share it, we run an affiliate scheme through Gumroad (30% commission, clearly labelled as an ad). Happy to send a one-page brief.
>
> Would you like a copy?
>
> Thanks,
> The Well Listed team

**One follow-up (after 7 days or more, only if no reply):**

> Hi [NAME], just a gentle nudge in case this got buried. Happy to send the kit if it is useful, and no worries at all if not. The Well Listed team

> Hi [NAME],
>
> We are Well Listed, an independent UK brand. We make The Well Listed Kit: tested AI prompts, templates and checklists that help UK sellers write accurate titles, condition notes and buyer replies using free AI tools.
>
> [ONE SPECIFIC, GENUINE REFERENCE, e.g. "In your recent video on [TITLE], you said most of your time goes on writing descriptions after the sourcing trip. That is exactly the part the kit is built for."]
>

>

>
> Shall we send it over, with a one-page brief?
>
> Best wishes,
> The Well Listed team

**One follow-up (after 7 days or more, only if no reply):**

> Hi [NAME], a quick follow-up in case this slipped past. The offer of a free kit stands, no strings. If it is not for you, no problem and we will not message again. The Well Listed team

## 3. A UK reseller newsletter or blog

**Where:** the editor's contact email or "work with us" page. If they only accept paid sponsorships, respect that and ask about their rates instead of pitching an affiliate deal.

**Subject:** A resource for [NEWSLETTER OR BLOG NAME] readers

> Hi [NAME],
>
> We are Well Listed, a small UK brand that makes listing tools for eBay, Vinted, Depop and Etsy sellers. We are not affiliated with any of the marketplaces.
>
> [ONE SPECIFIC, GENUINE REFERENCE, e.g. "Your piece on [TOPIC, e.g. writing condition notes that avoid 'not as described' disputes] was really practical, and the point about [SPECIFIC POINT] is one we completely agree with."]
>
> Two things we could offer, whichever suits you:
>
> 1. **A free resource for your readers:** our UK Listing Cheat Sheet, a one-page free download. No payment either way.
> 2. **The full Well Listed Kit free for you to try,** with an optional Gumroad affiliate link (30% commission) if you decide to recommend it. Any mention would need to be labelled as an ad or affiliate link, and we never ask for income claims.
>
> Happy to send either, or to leave it there if it is not a fit.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> The Well Listed team

**One follow-up (after 7 days or more, only if no reply):**

> Hi [NAME], following up once in case this was missed. Either offer stands if useful. If not, thank you for your time and we will not follow up again. The Well Listed team

## Tracking outreach

Keep a short log in `metrics/` (not shared publicly): date, name or handle, platform, the genuine reference you used, date of follow-up (if any), outcome. Delete the details of anyone who says no.
