# The Well Listed affiliate programme

A simple, honest affiliate set-up using Gumroad's built-in affiliate feature. Creators who genuinely like The Well Listed Kit can share it with their audience and earn a commission on each sale that comes through their link.

This file is for us (how to run it). The one-page brief we send to creators is `creator-brief.md`. Outreach messages are in `outreach.md`.

---

## 1. The offer

| | What we offer |
|---|---|
| **Standard commission** | **30%** of each sale made through the creator's link |
| **Dedicated content rate** | **40%** for creators who make a dedicated piece about the kit (a full video, a newsletter section or a blog post), agreed with us in advance |
| **Free kit** | Every accepted creator gets the full kit free, so they can try it properly before deciding whether to share it. No obligation to post, and no obligation to be positive |
| **Minimum** | None. No fees to join, no targets |

### What that means in money (planning maths, not a promise)

Commission is worked out on the sale price. Money kept per sale after Gumroad and card fees is about **£15.95 at £19** and about **£9.86 at £12** (see `MONEY.md`). Roughly:

| Price | Commission | Creator gets about | We keep about |
|---|---|---|---|
| £19 | 30% | £5.70 | £10.25 |
| £19 | 40% | £7.60 | £8.35 |
| £12 (launch, days 1 to 14 only) | 30% | £3.60 | £6.26 |
| £12 (launch, days 1 to 14 only) | 40% | £4.80 | £5.06 |

These are rough. How Gumroad calculates the affiliate share (before or after its fees and VAT) must be checked on Gumroad's help pages before we tell any creator a pound figure. **Only ever tell creators the percentage**, not what they "could earn".

We never go above 40%. Reviewed after 3 months.

## 2. How it works (Gumroad's affiliate feature)

We could not open Gumroad's help pages overnight, so check each step against Gumroad's current help centre (search "affiliates") before setting up.

1. **The owner turns it on.** In the Gumroad dashboard, find the Affiliates section (check the current menu name). Add the product and set the commission percentage.
2. **We add each creator by email.** The creator needs their own Gumroad account (free) to receive the commission. They may need to accept an invitation.
3. **Gumroad gives the creator a unique link.** The creator can find it in their own Gumroad account. We never make links for them.
4. **A buyer clicks the link and buys.** Gumroad records the sale against that creator.
5. **Gumroad pays the creator their share** directly, on Gumroad's usual payout schedule (check how payouts work for affiliates and any minimum payout).
6. **Refunds:** if a buyer takes the 30-day refund, the commission for that sale is normally reversed (check how Gumroad handles this, and tell creators).

### Cookie and tracking notes

- Gumroad tracks affiliate sales with a cookie set when someone clicks the link. The cookie length is commonly reported as 30 days, but we have **not verified** this. Check Gumroad's help pages and tell creators the real figure, or say "tracking lasts for a limited time after the click" if unsure.
- Tracking can fail if the buyer uses a different device or browser, clears cookies, or uses an ad blocker. Creators should know this upfront so there is no bad feeling.
- If a sale goes through our own site first and then to Gumroad, the affiliate link must point straight to the Gumroad product page (or a page that keeps the affiliate tag). Test one link end to end before a creator posts.
- Keep a simple sheet of creators: public handle, platform, date added, commission rate, date of their post, sales reported by Gumroad. Keep it in a private spreadsheet in the brand's own Google account, **not in this repo**: names and email addresses are personal data, and the repo may be shared. The daily operator only needs handles and numbers.

## 3. The rules (every affiliate agrees to these)

Send these with the invitation and ask the creator to reply "agreed" before we add them.

### Disclosure: always, and upfront

UK advertising rules (the CAP Code, enforced by the ASA) say that paid or affiliate content must be obviously identifiable as advertising. See the ASA's influencer guidance: https://www.asa.org.uk/resource/influencers-guide.html (we could not open this page overnight; check it before launch).

- Put a clear label **at the start** of the post or caption, before anyone clicks "more": **#ad** or **Ad**.
- Next to the link itself, add "affiliate link" (for example: "Affiliate link: [link]"). On its own, "affiliate link" may not be clear enough for the ASA, so use it **as well as** #ad, not instead of it. Check the ASA guidance for the current wording.
- Use the platform's own paid partnership tool as well where one exists (TikTok's content disclosure setting, Instagram's "Paid partnership" label, YouTube's "includes paid promotion" box).
- For video, say it or show it on screen at the start too, for example "Ad: this is an affiliate link, I get a commission if you buy."
- A free kit alone, with no commission, still needs labelling, for example "gifted" or "free copy from Well Listed". Once they have an affiliate link, it is an ad and needs #ad.

### No income or sales claims

- Never say or suggest the kit will make buyers money, help them sell more, sell faster, earn a certain amount, or replace a job.
- Creators must not share their own affiliate earnings as a reason to buy.
- They can say what the kit does: helps write clearer, more accurate listings faster, with UK English, honest condition notes and polite buyer replies.

### No spam

- No mass messages, DMs or comments with the link.
- No dropping the link in Facebook groups, Reddit, Discord or forums that ban self-promotion, or in the comments of other creators' posts.
- No emailing people who have not signed up to their list.
- No coupon or deal sites, cashback sites or link-sharing groups without our written agreement.

### No fake reviews or fake anything

- Only honest opinions from their own real use. They can mention things they did not like.
- No made-up results, screenshots, testimonials or "a friend of mine sold..." stories.
- No posting reviews of the kit on Gumroad or anywhere else in exchange for commission, and no asking others to. (Fake and incentivised reviews are banned under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024; see `research/platform-rules-uk.md`.)

### No bidding on our brand

- No paid search ads, social ads or shopping ads on the words "Well Listed", "Well Listed Kit", "The Well Listed Kit" or misspellings of them.
- No paid ads that send people to their affiliate link at all without our written agreement.
- No registering domains, handles or pages that use our brand name.

### Brand and marketplace rules

- Do not present themselves as Well Listed or as speaking for us.
- Do not say or suggest we are affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by eBay, Vinted, Depop, Etsy, Amazon or TikTok, and no marketplace logos in affiliate content about the kit.
- Do not share the kit's files, prompts or PDF publicly or with others (they are for the creator's own use).
- No false urgency. The £12 launch price may only be mentioned while it is genuinely running (days 1 to 14).

### If a rule is broken

We ask once for the post to be fixed (for example, adding #ad). If it is not fixed within 7 days, or if the breach is serious (income claims, fake reviews, spam), we remove the creator from the programme in Gumroad. Commission on sales before removal is paid as Gumroad normally would.

## 4. How we run it week to week

- **Who we invite:** small UK creators whose content is about reselling, decluttering or small marketplace shops, and whose tone fits ours (practical, honest). Size does not matter. We never invite anyone whose content is about income claims or "get rich" reselling.
- **How we find them:** by watching and reading their content first (see `research/where-buyers-hang-out.md`). No bought lists, no scraped emails.
- **How we contact them:** one personalised message at a time, through the contact route they publish for business or collaboration enquiries (see `outreach.md`). Maximum 5 a day.
- **Checking posts:** when a creator tells us they have posted, check it has #ad at the start and no income claims. Save the link in the private creator sheet.
- **Being fair:** reply to creators within 2 working days. If Gumroad tracking misses a sale they can reasonably show came from them, we will look at it.
- **Privacy:** only keep what we need (name or handle, contact email, Gumroad email, rate, posts). Delete details of anyone who says no.
