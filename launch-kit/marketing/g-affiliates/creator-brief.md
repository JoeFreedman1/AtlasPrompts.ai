## Who we are

Well Listed is a small, independent UK brand. We make practical tools for people who sell on eBay, Vinted, Depop, Etsy, Amazon and TikTok Shop. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by any of those marketplaces.

## The product

**The Well Listed Kit** is a downloadable pack of tested AI prompts, fill-in templates and checklists. Sellers fill in a short "Listing Brief" with the facts about an item, paste it into a free AI chat tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot), and get back a UK-ready title, description, tags, a price check from sold listings they look up themselves, and polite replies to buyer messages.

Every prompt tells the AI to use UK English, stick to the facts given, and write "[CHECK: ...]" instead of inventing details.

- **What is inside:** a PDF guide (13 modules), a copy-and-paste prompt library, and spreadsheet templates (Listing Brief, stock and pricing tracker, message snippets).
- **Price:** £19, one-off payment, no subscription (£12 during the first 14 days of launch only).
- **Refund:** 30 days, no fuss.
- **Sold through:** Gumroad.
- **Free taster:** The UK Listing Cheat Sheet (free, by email sign-up).

## Disclosure rules (UK)

UK advertising rules (the ASA and the CAP Code) say affiliate content must be obviously an ad, **before** people engage with it. So:

1. **#ad** or **Ad** at the start of the caption, title or post. Not hidden in a pile of hashtags at the end.
2. In video, say or show "Ad" near the start.
3. "Affiliate link" next to the link itself.
4. Switch on your platform's paid partnership or disclosure setting.
5. If you only use the free kit and have no affiliate link, still say so, for example "gifted" or "free copy from Well Listed".

The ASA's guide for influencers explains this in detail: https://www.asa.org.uk/resource/influencers-guide.html
