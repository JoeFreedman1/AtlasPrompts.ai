# Free tools and the 10-minute post workflow

Everything Well Listed needs to make faceless posts for £0: which free tools to use, a one-time 30-minute template setup, a minute-by-minute routine for turning one script from `marketing/b-short-form-posts/` into a finished post in under 10 minutes, and a privacy check before anything goes live.

**About costs:** free tiers, limits and licence terms change often, and we could not open these providers' pricing or terms pages this session (see `research/tools-and-fees.md`). Every "free" note below means "free at the time of writing as far as we know: check current terms" before relying on it, especially for commercial use (we are a business, so commercial use matters).

## 1. The tool list

### Slideshows and carousels

| Tool | What for | Cost (check current terms) | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Canva (free plan)** | Carousel slides, parcel-label cards, before and after graphics, Pinterest pins, cover images. | Free plan with templates and export. Brand Kit, background remover and many premium elements are Canva Pro. Free content is generally allowed for commercial use under Canva's content licence, with limits: check https://www.canva.com/policies/content-license-agreement/ . | Build one master carousel (section 2) and duplicate it for every post. Use the "Resize" option only if you have Pro; otherwise keep separate 1080 x 1350 and 1080 x 1920 masters. |
| **CapCut templates** | Quick slideshow videos with text and transitions, using saved project templates. | Free editor, but many features and templates have moved to Pro. CapCut updated its terms of service in June 2025 with a broad licence over user content (reported to include voice and likeness): read https://www.capcut.com/clause/terms-of-service before use. | Only use templates without watermarks or Pro-only elements, or the export will carry a watermark or fail. Save your own project as the reusable template instead of relying on public templates. |
| **TikTok photo mode** | Native carousels on TikTok (swipeable images with sound). | Free, built into TikTok. | Export the Canva slides as PNGs (1080 x 1920 or 1080 x 1350), then upload them in TikTok as photos. Put the search phrase in the caption's first line. |

## 2. One-time setup: reusable Canva and CapCut brand templates (30 minutes)

Do this once. After it, every post is "duplicate, replace text, export".

### Minutes 0 to 5: colours and fonts

1. In Canva, create a blank 1080 x 1920 design called "WL master: vertical".
2. Add six small squares on a spare page and set them to the brand colours: Ink `#1F2A44`, Cream `#FAF6EF`, Kraft `#C9A27E`, Sold Green `#1E7F55`, Label Yellow `#F2C84B`, Returns Red `#C4453B`. They will now appear under "Document colours" in this design and any copy of it.
3. Add three text boxes styled as: Heading (Archivo 800 or Montserrat ExtraBold, Ink, large), Body (Inter 600 or Open Sans, Ink), Prompt box (JetBrains Mono or a monospace fallback, Ink on Cream, inside a rounded rectangle). This page is your style sheet.

### Minutes 5 to 13: the parcel-label card

1. New page. Background: Cream.
2. Add a rounded rectangle at about 85% of the width. Fill: Cream (slightly lighter or pure `#FAF6EF`). Border: Kraft, 6 to 8px.
3. Add a dashed line across the top third of the card (Canva line tool, dashed style, Kraft). This is the "cut line".
4. Optional: a Kraft "tape strip" (a slightly rotated rectangle at 70% transparency) across the top edge.
5. Inside the card: a heading text box (max 12 words) and a smaller line underneath.
6. Add a small "Well Listed" text logo with a Sold Green tick (a tick element from Canva's free elements) at the bottom.
7. Group the card. This is the hook slide and the end slide.

### Minutes 13 to 19: the other slides

1. **Before and after slide:** duplicate the card page. Top half: grey text with a Returns Red strikethrough line and a small "Before" label. Bottom half: Ink text with a Sold Green tick and an "After" label. Add a tiny "Example" label.
2. **Tip slide:** Ink background, Cream heading text, one word highlighted with a Label Yellow rectangle behind it (at 80% transparency).
3. **CTA slide:** the parcel-label card with "Free cheat sheet: link in bio" and a small "Save this for your next listing".
4. **Fact line:** a small text box at the bottom of any slide showing a limit: "Check the current rule in the platform's help pages."

### Minutes 19 to 22: carousel and pin versions

1. Make a copy called "WL master: carousel" and change the size (or rebuild) to 1080 x 1350.
2. Make a copy called "WL master: pin" at 1000 x 1500 for `c-pinterest/pins.md`.
3. Put all three in a Canva folder called "Well Listed templates".

### Minutes 22 to 30: CapCut template project

1. Export the parcel-label hook slide and CTA slide from Canva as PNGs.
2. In CapCut, start a 9:16 project called "WL template".
3. Track 1: the hook PNG for 2 seconds, a placeholder clip in the middle, the CTA PNG for 3 seconds at the end.
4. Add a text layer in the brand heading font (import Archivo from Google Fonts on desktop if CapCut allows it, otherwise Montserrat ExtraBold), Ink on Cream, and save it as a text preset or favourite.
5. Set auto captions style once (font, size, centre position above the bottom UI) and save it as a preset if CapCut offers it.
6. Choose the brand voice in text-to-speech, play a test line, and write the voice name in your notes and in `01-brief/BRAND-GUIDE.md`.
7. Save the project. For each new video, duplicate this project rather than starting from blank.

## 3. One post in under 10 minutes (minute by minute)

Before you start: choose today's post ID from `marketing/a-content-calendar/calendar.csv`, and open its script in `marketing/b-short-form-posts/`. Phone on Do Not Disturb.

**Minute 0 to 1: read and decide.**
Read the post's Hook, Script and Visuals. Decide: carousel (slides) or video (VO plus screen recording or stock). Open the matching master template (Canva "WL master" or CapCut "WL template") and duplicate it.

**Minute 1 to 3: the slides or overlays.**
Copy the "On-screen text" lines from the script into the slides, one line per slide or overlay. Keep the one highlighted word in Label Yellow. Add the "Example" label to any invented price or listing, and the "check the current rule" line where a limit appears.

**Minute 3 to 5: the footage (video posts only).**
Either: screen record the AI chat doing the prompt in the script (crop to the chat window), or drop in the stock clip using the search term in the script's Visuals (Pexels, Pixabay or Canva free). One clip per scene, trimmed to the timings. For carousels, skip this and spend the time checking the slides on your phone.

**Minute 5 to 6: the voiceover (video posts only).**
Paste each VO line into CapCut text-to-speech using the brand voice. Line up each clip with its timing from the script.

**Minute 6 to 7: captions.**
Run CapCut auto captions and apply the saved style. Read every caption and fix mishearings and American spellings. For carousels, check every slide is readable at phone size.

**Minute 7 to 8: export and privacy check.**
Export (1080 x 1920 video, or PNG slides). Watch or swipe through once on the phone and run the privacy checklist in section 4.

**Minute 8 to 9: upload and paste.**
Upload natively to each platform in the calendar row (no other platform's watermark). Paste the Caption and Hashtags blocks from the script exactly. Put the search phrase in the first caption line (it already is in the scripts). Add alt text on Instagram if offered.

**Minute 9 to 10: publish or schedule, then log.**
Post now or schedule (Meta Business Suite, TikTok Studio, Pinterest). Add a note to the metrics sheet that the post is out. Done.

If a post takes longer than 10 minutes, it is usually the screen recording. Record three or four walkthroughs in one sitting at the weekend and keep them in a "WL raw clips" album.

## 4. Privacy checklist before posting

Run through this for every image, video and screen recording before it goes live. If anything fails, crop, blur or re-record.

- [ ] **No notifications.** No banners, message previews or badges (Do Not Disturb was on).
- [ ] **No account names.** No AI tool account name, email address, profile picture or initials; no marketplace username; no browser profile icon.
- [ ] **No other tabs or bookmarks.** Browser tabs, bookmarks bar and address bar are cropped out.
- [ ] **No faces.** No face in any shot, including in stock footage used as if it were "us".
- [ ] **No reflections.** Check shiny surfaces: phone screens, mirrors, windows, kettles, glossy boxes, glasses. Nothing reflects a person or room.
- [ ] **No home details.** No recognisable room, view from a window, house number, post, letters, parcels with addresses or labels, family photos, pets, car or number plate.
- [ ] **No location tags.** Location tagging off in the post; photo location data (metadata) removed or location services off for the camera. Never tag a town or venue.
- [ ] **No real listings or buyers.** No screenshot of anyone's real listing, username or message without permission. Example listings are ours and labelled "Example".
- [ ] **No personal voice or handwriting.** Voiceover is the brand text-to-speech voice. No handwritten notes in shot.
- [ ] **No marketplace logos** used as if we are affiliated.
- [ ] **Words checked.** No em dashes or en dashes, no income claims, no invented numbers, UK spelling, "check the current rule" where a limit appears (`01-brief/WRITING-RULES.md`).
- [ ] **Music is licensed.** Only the platform's commercial-use library or a royalty-free source.
