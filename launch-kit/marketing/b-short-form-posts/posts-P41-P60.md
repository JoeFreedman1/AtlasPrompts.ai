## P43: Phrases to keep out of your listings

- **Format:** Listicle (carousel)
- **Best on:** Instagram carousel, TikTok photo mode, Pinterest
- **Length:** 8 slides
- **Call to action:** Soft (save this)

**Hook (first 2 seconds):** "Six phrases that can get a listing removed. 'Replica' is only the first."

### Script
1. Slide 1: "Six phrases that can get a listing removed. 'Replica' is only the first."
2. Slide 2: "'Replica', 'dupe', '1:1', 'inspired by [brand]'. Counterfeit and lookalike wording breaks marketplace rules. If it is not the real brand, do not list it as that brand."
3. Slide 3: "'100% genuine' when you cannot show it. Say what you actually know instead: 'Bought new from the brand's shop in 2023, receipt included.'"
4. Slide 4: "'Cures', 'relieves', 'heals', 'detox'. Health claims are not allowed on TikTok Shop UK and are risky everywhere. Describe the item, not a medical result."
5. Slide 5: "'Fully working' on something you only switched on. Write exactly what you tested."
6. Slide 6: "'Not Nike' or 'like Zara' in the title. Other brand names in your title muddle search and can break the rules. Name only what it is."
7. Slide 7: "'Non-toxic', '100% safe', 'eco-friendly' with no proof. Only say it if you can show the specific fact behind it."
8. Slide 8: "Rules differ by platform, so check the current rule in the help pages. Save this list for your next draft."

### Caption
```text
Six phrases that can get a listing removed, and what to write instead.

Counterfeit wording is the obvious one, but the quieter problems are claims you cannot back up. "100% genuine" with nothing to show for it. "Relieves back pain" on a wheat bag. "Fully working" on a console you only switched on.

The fix is the same every time: describe what you know. Where you bought it, what is included, what you tested, what the label says.

TikTok Shop UK's own policies say claims must be accurate and must not suggest a product cures, treats or relieves a condition. Other platforms have their own rules on counterfeits and claims, so check the current rule in each platform's help pages.

Save this and read it once before your next listing goes live.
```
