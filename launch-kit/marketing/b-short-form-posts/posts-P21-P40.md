## P21: Three photos vs a proper shot list

- **Format:** Before/after (carousel, 7 slides)
- **Best on:** Instagram carousel, TikTok photo mode
- **Length:** 7 slides
- **Call to action:** Soft (save / free cheat sheet)

**Hook (first 2 seconds):** "3 photos: front, box, sole. The buyer's first question? 'Any scuffs?'"

### Script
1. Slide 1 (hook, parcel label card): "3 photos: front, box, sole. The buyer's first question? 'Any scuffs?'"
2. Slide 2 (BEFORE, grey, Returns Red crosses): "Before. Black Chelsea boots, UK 6. Photo 1: side on. Photo 2: the box. Photo 3: one sole. No inside, no heels, no flaw close-up."
3. Slide 3: "Every missing photo becomes a message. 'What size is stamped inside?' 'Any wear on the heels?' 'Can I see the other sole?'"

6. Slide 6 (prompt box, JetBrains Mono): "The rule we use: every flaw gets its own photo. Prompt: 'Write a numbered shot list for this exact item. Give every flaw in my brief its own shot. Never drop a flaw shot.'"
7. Slide 7 (parcel label card): "Photo limits differ. At the time of writing Vinted allows up to 20, Depop up to 8. Check the app. Save this for your next pair."

### On-screen text
3 photos. First question? "Any scuffs?"
Before: side, box, one sole
Every gap = a buyer message
After: an 8-shot list
Every flaw gets its own photo

Save this

### Caption
```text
Three photos is not a listing, it is a question waiting to happen. For a pair of boots, buyers want both soles, both heels, the size stamp inside and a close-up of every scuff. We plan the shots before picking up the phone: cover, front, back, soles, inside, one photo per flaw, then details. The prompt on slide 6 turns your item notes into a numbered shot list in about a minute, using any free AI chat tool. Photo limits vary by app (at the time of writing Vinted allows up to 20 and Depop up to 8), so check the current limit in the app when you list. Save this for your next pair, and grab the free UK Listing Cheat Sheet from the link in bio.

### Caption
```text

Our reply: thank them, say sorry it is not right, state the policy once, point them to the app for any item problem. If you sell as a business, the rules are different: online buyers usually have 14 days to cancel, plus rights if an item is faulty. The Well Listed Kit has reply prompts for both, link in bio. ### Caption
```text
Guessed £45, but the last five matching pairs sold for £18 to £30. A price from memory ("they cost a lot new") is still a guess.

The fix takes about ten minutes:
1. Turn on the Sold items filter on eBay and copy five pairs that match: same boot, same size.
2. Note the postage each one charged.
3. Compare like with like. In our example, two pairs had splits at the ankle and sold low, so they are not fair comparisons for a pair without splits.

What is left gives you a range, a list price and a floor. Paste the good matches into any free AI tool and ask it to show the sums if you want the maths done for you.

Every figure here is invented for illustration. Save this for the next thing you are not sure how to price.

## P25: Myth: longer descriptions always sell better

- **Format:** Myth (carousel, 6 slides)
- **Best on:** Instagram carousel, TikTok photo mode
- **Length:** 6 slides

4. Slide 4 (AFTER, Ink, 5 lines): "M&S navy slim-fit chinos, W34 L31. Cotton blend (check label for %). Waist 44 cm flat, inside leg 79 cm. Good condition: light fading on both back pockets (photo 5). Posted within 2 days."

### On-screen text

BEFORE: 60 words of "gorgeous"

### Caption
```text
Myth: longer descriptions sell better. What buyers want is their questions answered before they have to message you, and that is about being complete, not long. Our five-line test for a clothing description: what it is, the size on the label plus real measurements laid flat, what it is made of (from the care label), the condition with every flaw and where it is, and when it will be posted. Anything that does not answer one of those, like "perfect for smart-casual days", can go. On a phone, five clear lines beat a paragraph of adjectives every time you scroll. The example here is invented. Save this and try the five-line test on your next listing.
