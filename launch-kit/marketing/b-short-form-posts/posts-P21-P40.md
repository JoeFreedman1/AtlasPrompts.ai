## P21: Three photos vs a proper shot list

- **Format:** Before/after (carousel, 7 slides)
- **Best on:** Instagram carousel, TikTok photo mode
- **Length:** 7 slides
- **Call to action:** Soft (save / free cheat sheet)

**Hook (first 2 seconds):** "3 photos: front, box, sole. The buyer's first question? 'Any scuffs?'"

### Script
1. Slide 1 (hook, parcel label card): "3 photos: front, box, sole. The buyer's first question? 'Any scuffs?'"
2. Slide 2 (BEFORE, grey, Returns Red crosses): "Before. Black Chelsea boots, UK 6. Photo 1: side on. Photo 2: the box. Photo 3: one sole. No inside, no heels, no flaw close-up."

7. Slide 7 (parcel label card): "Photo limits differ. At the time of writing Vinted allows up to 20, Depop up to 8. Check the app. Save this for your next pair."

### On-screen text

### Caption
```text
Three photos is not a listing, it is a question waiting to happen. For a pair of boots, buyers want both soles, both heels, the size stamp inside and a close-up of every scuff. We plan the shots before picking up the phone: cover, front, back, soles, inside, one photo per flaw, then details. The prompt on slide 6 turns your item notes into a numbered shot list in about a minute, using any free AI chat tool. Photo limits vary by app (at the time of writing Vinted allows up to 20 and Depop up to 8), so check the current limit in the app when you list. Save this for your next pair, and grab the free UK Listing Cheat Sheet from the link in bio.
```

### Caption
