# Plan for the 60 faceless short-form posts

Post IDs P01 to P60 are used everywhere (calendar, dashboard, ROUTINE.md). The full scripts are in `b-short-form-posts/`.

**Calls to action:** most posts end with a soft line ("Save this for your next listing" or "Free cheat sheet: link in bio"). About 1 in 4 mention the paid kit, never with pressure or fake scarcity. The free lead magnet is **"The UK Listing Cheat Sheet"** (10 free prompts plus a character-limit table).

**Honesty rule for "walkthrough" and "wish we knew" posts:** walkthroughs show what a prompt produces for an example item. They never show sales, earnings or results we do not have. "Things nobody tells you" posts share general seller knowledge; the brand does not claim a personal history.
