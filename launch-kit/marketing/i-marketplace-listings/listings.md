## Where we are not listing, and why

- **Etsy: not allowed.** Etsy's Seller Handbook article "What is Etsy's stance on AI creations?" says lists of prompts for generative AI may not be sold on Etsy. The kit is largely AI prompts, so listing it there would break Etsy's rules and risk the shop. (Source: `research/platform-rules-uk.md`, SECONDARY leaning official, https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1275449912004 . Recheck before any future Etsy decision.) We still help Etsy sellers with our content; we just do not sell there.
- **TikTok Shop UK: not supported.** Digital media and digital downloads are reported as unsupported products on TikTok Shop UK. (Source: `research/platform-rules-uk.md`, SECONDARY, with the official policy page to recheck: https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753824408307457&lang=en-GB .) TikTok is for organic content (and possibly ads later) pointing to our own sales page, not for selling the file itself.

If either rule changes, check the platform's current help pages in full before doing anything.

### Read this before publishing

- **Check KDP's current rules yourself.** We could not open KDP's content guidelines, pricing or AI pages this session. Before uploading, read KDP's Content Guidelines, the Metadata Guidelines (title, subtitle and keyword rules), the pricing and royalty page, and the AI content section in KDP Help. Rules on links in books, minimum content, and what counts as "disappointing content" may affect this book.
- **The AI question must be answered honestly.** When you publish, KDP asks whether the book contains AI-generated content (text, images and translations are asked about separately). The honest answer for this book is **yes: the text is AI-generated or AI-assisted** (written with AI tools and then edited and checked by us). If any cover or inside image is made with an AI image tool, answer yes for images too. Never tick "no" to get through faster.
- **Marketplace names:** the title and subtitle describe use ("for eBay and Vinted sellers") and must not suggest the book is official. Check KDP's metadata guidelines on using other brands' names in titles and keywords; if in doubt, keep brand names out of the title and in the description only.
- **Links:** a single, plain line at the end of the book pointing to the full kit may be acceptable, but books that exist mainly to advertise are generally not. Check KDP's current content guidelines, and make sure the book is complete and useful on its own.
- **Do not claim sales, reviews or rankings** in the description or inside the book. Never ask for or offer anything in exchange for a review (Amazon's policies and UK law ban this).
