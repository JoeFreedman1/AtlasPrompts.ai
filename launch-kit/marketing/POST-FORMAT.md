# Exact format for every short-form post

The dashboard reads these files automatically, so keep the headings exactly like this (same words, same order). One post per `## P..` heading.

````markdown
## P01: Messy notes to an 80-character eBay title

- **Format:** Tutorial (screen recording plus text overlay)
- **Best on:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
- **Length:** 20 to 30 seconds (or 6 slides for a carousel)
- **Call to action:** Soft (save / free cheat sheet) or Kit

**Hook (first 2 seconds):** The exact words on screen and spoken in the first 2 seconds.

### Script
Slide-by-slide text (for carousels/slideshows) OR voiceover lines with timings, e.g.
1. (0 to 2s) VO: "..." | On screen: "..."
2. (2 to 6s) VO: "..." | On screen: "..."

### On-screen text
The short text overlays in order, one per line.

### Caption
```text
The full caption, ready to paste.
```

### Hashtags
```text
#tag1 #tag2 #tag3
```

### Visuals
Exactly what to show: which screen to record, which stock clip (search term for Pexels/Canva), which template, colours from the brand guide, what to blur.
````

Rules: UK English. No em dashes or en dashes. No income claims, no fake results, no invented statistics. The brand speaks as "we" and never shows a person. Hooks must be specific, not "You won't believe this". Captions 50 to 150 words, first line repeats the hook idea. 3 to 6 hashtags, mixing niche and broad, no spammy tags.
