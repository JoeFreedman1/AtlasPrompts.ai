# Vinted message templates: polite replies for every buyer question

Good Vinted message templates are short, friendly and factual, and they keep everything on the platform. Answer the question in the first sentence, add one useful fact (when you post, or a measurement), and point to the app's own buttons for buying or offers. Below are 14 copy-paste replies, from "is this still available?" to returns, plus a prompt for anything unusual. They work as eBay buyer message templates too.

## Three rules behind every template

**1. Polite, brief, factual.** Write as if the platform's support team will read the thread, because in a dispute they might.

**2. Stay on the platform.** Every message and payment goes through the app. It protects the buyer and you.

**3. Never promise what you cannot control.** You can promise when you will post. You cannot promise when Royal Mail will deliver.

Fill in the [SQUARE BRACKETS] and check every fact is true for your item before you send.

## Reply to "is this still available?"

Answer it, then make the next step easy.

**1. Yes, still available**
> Hi, yes it is still available. I can post [NEXT WORKING DAY] after purchase. Any questions, just ask.

**2. Sold already**
> Hi, sorry, this one has sold. I have a few similar items listed if you would like a look.

**3. Sold on another app (if you cross-list)**
> Hi, sorry, this has just sold elsewhere and I have removed it.

If an order has come in on a second platform, cancel it through that platform's process and check its rules, as some count seller cancellations against you. Better still, remove cross-listed items the moment one sells.

## Questions about the item

**4. Measurements**
> Hi, measured flat: pit to pit [52] cm, length [68] cm, sleeve [60] cm. I have added these to the description too.

Get this often? Your descriptions need measurements. Our [Vinted description template](/blog/vinted-description-template.html) has a measurements block built in.

**5. More photos**
> Hi, of course. I have added [A CLOSE-UP OF THE LABEL] to the listing photos. Let me know if you need anything else.

Add photos to the listing rather than sending them by message, so every buyer sees them.

**6. Smoke-free or pet-free?**
> Hi, thanks for asking. [ONLY IF TRUE: Yes, it comes from a smoke-free and pet-free home.]

## Offers and bundles

**7. Friendly counter-offer**
> Thanks for the offer. The lowest I can do is £[AMOUNT]. I will send it through the offer button if that works for you.

**8. Polite decline**
> Thanks for your interest, but I cannot go that low on this one. You are welcome to make another offer.

**9. Bundle price**
> Happy to do [ITEMS] together for £[AMOUNT] plus postage, all in one parcel. If you add them to a bundle, I will send you an offer.

For the harder cases, see [how to respond to lowball offers on Vinted](/blog/how-to-respond-to-lowball-offers-vinted.html).

## Postage and delays

**10. Postage timing**
> I post [MONDAYS AND THURSDAYS]. It usually takes [2 TO 3 WORKING DAYS] after that, but I cannot guarantee a delivery date.

**11. Posted**
> Hi, your parcel went out today. Tracking is in the app. Thanks again.

**12. Delayed in transit**
> Hi, sorry for the wait. The tracking shows it at [A DEPOT] since [TUESDAY]. I have asked the carrier to check and will update you as soon as I hear. You are still covered by the app's buyer protection.

Say sorry once, give the facts, say what happens next.

## The ones that need care

**13. "Can I pay you directly?"**
> Hi, thanks, but I only sell and take payment through the app, so we are both protected. You are welcome to buy or make an offer here.

Always decline. Paying outside the platform removes buyer and seller protection, and it is how many scams start. The same goes for requests for your phone number or links to "confirm your payment". Report anything odd through the app.

**14. Item not as described**
> Hi, I am sorry about that, and thanks for telling me. Could you send a photo of the [PROBLEM] here? I will sort it out through the app.

Reread your listing first. If you did not mention the flaw, the fair thing is usually to put it right through the app. If you did mention and photograph it, you are on firmer ground. As a private seller, change-of-mind returns are generally your choice, but "not as described" claims may be covered by buyer protection whatever your policy says. This is general information, not legal advice: check the platform's help pages, and gov.uk or Citizens Advice if you might count as a trader.

## Using these as eBay buyer message templates

Every reply above works on eBay with small changes: "the app" becomes "eBay", and "offer button" becomes a counter-offer. eBay has its own rules on contact outside eBay and on returns, so check its current help pages. ## A copy-paste prompt for any other message

For anything else, paste this into an AI chat tool. **Remove the buyer's name, username, address and order number first.** It is not your data to share.

```text
Help me reply to a buyer on [PLATFORM]. I have removed their
name and personal details.

Facts about my item: [PASTE HERE]
Buyer's message: [PASTE, WITH NAMES REPLACED BY [BUYER]]
What I want to say, in my own words: [ROUGH NOTES]

Write one reply.
Rules:
- UK English, friendly and brief (under 60 words). No emojis.
- Only state facts I have given. If the buyer asks something I
  have not answered, write [CHECK: ...] instead of guessing.
- Keep everything on the platform. Never suggest paying,
  messaging or meeting off the platform.
- Do not promise delivery dates I have not given.
- Do not mention feedback or reviews.
```

## FAQ

**How do I reply to "is this still available?" on Vinted?**
Answer yes or no first, add when you can post, and invite questions.

**What is a polite reply to a buyer asking for a discount?**
Thank them, give one specific counter price, and offer to send it through the offer button. Or decline kindly and invite another offer.

**Should I ever give a buyer my phone number?**
No. Keep all contact and payment inside the app so you both stay covered.
