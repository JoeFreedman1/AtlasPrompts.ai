# eBay item specifics (UK): how to fill them in properly

**The short answer:** item specifics are the structured fields on an eBay listing (brand, size, colour, type, material, style and so on). Buyers use them as filters, so an empty field can keep your item out of a filtered search. Fill in every specific that applies, using the simple standard wording eBay offers, take facts from the label or the item itself, and leave a field blank rather than guess.

## What item specifics are, and why they matter

A buyer who searches "womens jumper" often narrows the results with filters: Size 12, Brand Next, Colour Grey. Those filters run on item specifics, and eBay's UK Seller Centre advises adding them because they help listings appear in filtered search. Fill in only the required fields and your listing can drop out the moment a buyer taps a filter. A great title cannot fix that.

Specifics also give buyers facts they can scan and check against the photos.

## How to fill in item specifics on eBay

### 1. Choose the right category first

The specifics eBay shows depend on the category: a jumper category asks for neckline and sleeve length, a console category for storage capacity. Pick the most accurate category, then see what it asks for.

### 2. Get the facts from the item, not from memory

- **Brand, size, material:** the care label or base.
- **Model number:** the label, box or settings screen.
- **Colour:** plain words matching eBay's options.

If a label is missing or cut, leave the field blank. Never guess a brand, material or year.

### 3. Use eBay's suggested values where they fit

Where a field offers a dropdown, use it. Buyers filter by "Grey", not "dove grey heather". If you type your own value, keep it short and plain (third-party guides commonly quote 65 characters per value, so check the form).

### 4. Fill in the optional ones too

Optional specifics such as pattern, fit, features and sleeve length are often exactly what buyers filter by. If it applies and you know it, fill it in.

### 5. Leave blank rather than guess

A wrong specific is worse than an empty one. Tick "Wool" when the label says acrylic and you have a "not as described" case waiting to happen.

### 6. Check it all agrees

Title, specifics, description and photos should tell the same story.

## eBay item specifics for clothing: a worked example

Invented example: a grey Next wool-blend jumper, size 12, crew neck, long sleeves, care label says 60% polyester, 30% acrylic, 10% wool.

| Item specific | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | Next | Neck label |
| Size | 12 | Neck label |
| Size type | Regular | Label (no "petite" or "tall") |
| Department | Women | Label |
| Type | Jumper | The item |
| Colour | Grey | Looking at it in daylight |
| Neckline | Crew Neck | The item |
| Sleeve length | Long Sleeve | The item |
| Material | Polyester, Acrylic, Wool Blend | Care label |
| Pattern | Solid | The item |
| Style | Pullover | The item |
| Country of manufacture | Leave blank | Label cut, so unknown |

Notice what is not there: "vintage", "cosy", "Zara style" or any other search word that does not describe the item.

### Clothing specifics buyers often filter by

- **Size and size type** (Regular, Petite, Tall, Plus).
- **Colour.** Use eBay's list (navy may sit under Blue).
- **Material.** Copy the care label. "Wool blend" is not "wool".
- **Fit and style** (slim, oversized, cropped), only if true.
- **Sleeve length, neckline, closure** for tops; **waist and inside leg** for trousers.

## What about eBay's own AI?

eBay's app can draft a title, description and item details from a photo. It saves typing, but a photo cannot show a care label, a model number or measurements, so read every suggested specific before accepting it.

## FAQ

**Are item specifics required on eBay UK?**
Some are required in most categories. The rest are optional, but they power the filters buyers use, so fill in every one that applies.

**Can I add my own item specifics?**
In many categories, at the time of writing, yes (check the listing form). Keep it factual, and do not use it to add extra search words.

**Do item specifics replace the description?**
No. Specifics are short facts. Condition, flaws, measurements and what is included still belong in the description.
