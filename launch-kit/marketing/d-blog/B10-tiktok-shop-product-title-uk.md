# TikTok Shop product title UK: rules, formula and examples

A good TikTok Shop product title in the UK follows a plain formula: brand, product type, material, one or two key features, then size, colour or quantity. It must be accurate and at least 15 characters. TikTok Shop UK's rules say titles must not include promotions such as "20% off", your shop name or web address, any mention of TikTok or other platforms, or symbols and special characters. Aim for 60 to 100 characters that a shopper can read at a glance on a phone.

## TikTok Shop listing rules UK sellers need for titles

TikTok Shop UK's own listing guidance says this about titles (checked September 2026; rules change, so check the current rule in Seller Center before you list).

| Rule | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Minimum 15 characters | "Wheat bag" alone is too short |
| Accurate and concise | Product type, material, key features, quantity, size |
| No promotional wording | No "sale", "20% off", "free P&P", "limited offer" |
| No seller URL or name | No shop name as a sales pitch, no web address |
| No platform names | No "TikTok", "as seen on TikTok", "cheaper than Amazon" |
| No symbols or special characters | No !!!, stars, pipes, tildes or emojis |

The maximum is commonly quoted as 255 characters. Seller Center shows a counter, so trust that.

Only put a brand in the title if it is yours or you are authorised to sell it.

## The formula

**Brand + product type + material + key feature + size, colour or quantity**

Plain words, in the order a shopper would say them, one fact per phrase.

## Before and after: two worked examples

Both products are invented for this article.

### Example 1: a bamboo drawer organiser

**Before (71 characters):**
> BEST Bamboo Drawer Organiser!!! 20% OFF + Free P&P | TidyNookShop.co.uk

Problems: "BEST" is an unsupported claim, "!!!" and "|" are symbols, "20% OFF" and "Free P&P" are promotions, and web addresses are banned.

**After (67 characters):**
> Tidy Nook Bamboo Drawer Organiser Set of 6 Boxes in 3 Sizes Natural

Brand, product type, material, quantity, sizes, colour. "Tidy Nook" stays because it is the product's own brand, printed on the boxes.

### Example 2: a lavender wheat bag

**Before (79 characters):**
> Relieves Back Pain Lavender Wheat Bag \*\* Viral on TikTok \*\* Cheaper than Amazon

"Relieves back pain" is a health claim, and TikTok Shop UK's policies say products must not claim to cure, treat, prevent or relieve any disease or medical condition, anywhere in the listing or video. It also mentions two platforms and uses symbols.

**After (71 characters):**
> Microwavable Wheat Bag with Dried Lavender Cotton Cover 35 x 15 cm Sage

Describe what it is made of, not what it does to the body. Heating and safety instructions belong in the description.

## Words to keep out of titles and descriptions

The claims policies cover titles, descriptions, images and videos. Leave out:

- **Health and medical words:** cures, heals, treats, relieves, prevents, detox, boosts immunity, anti-inflammatory.
- **Weight loss claims:** the policies ban weight loss claims made without diet and exercise, before and after weight images, and claims that weight loss is easy, guaranteed or permanent.
- **Unsupported comparisons:** best, number one, better than a named brand.
- **Vague safety or green claims:** 100% safe, non-toxic, eco-friendly, without specific evidence.
- **Fake urgency:** "only 2 left" when it is not true.

If in doubt, leave it out.

## Your TikTok Shop product description

The title gets the tap; the description answers the questions. The maximum is reported as 10,000 characters, but on a phone, shorter wins. A simple structure:

1. One line saying what it is and who it is for.
2. Four to six short feature lines, one fact each.
3. What is included.
4. Size and specs: dimensions, weight, capacity, materials.
5. Care, and any safety instructions from the label, word for word.

The same claim rules apply, and your measurements should be your own.

## FAQ

**How long should a TikTok Shop product title be?**
At least 15 characters. The maximum is commonly quoted as 255, but 60 to 100 reads better on a phone.

**Can I put a discount in my TikTok Shop title?**
No. Promotional wording is not allowed in titles. Set up genuine offers through Seller Center's own promotion tools.

**Can I use commas or brackets in a TikTok Shop title?**
The rules ban symbols and special characters, so we keep titles to plain words and numbers. Check Seller Center before using punctuation.
