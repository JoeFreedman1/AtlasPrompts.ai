# ChatGPT prompts for eBay listings (UK versions you can copy)

**The short answer:** the best ChatGPT prompts for eBay listings give the AI a short list of facts about your item, tell it the eBay UK rules (an 80-character title at the time of writing, UK English, UK sizes), and forbid it from inventing anything, asking for [CHECK: ...] where a fact is missing. Below are three prompts that do exactly that, for the title, description and item specifics. They work in the free versions of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot.

## Why most AI eBay listings go wrong

Ask ChatGPT for "an eBay listing for a blue jumper" and you get something long, gushing and partly invented. UK sellers on the eBay Community have been blunt about it: AI descriptions that miss size and condition, add items that are not included, and open with "a stunning addition to any collection".

The problem is the input. Give an AI a vague request and it guesses. Give it facts and rules and it becomes a fast, tidy typist. So every prompt below follows one habit: **facts first, then rules, then "do not invent".**

## Step 1: write a quick item brief

Spend two minutes writing the facts down. A short version of our kit brief:

```text
ITEM BRIEF
What it is:
Brand:
Model or style:
Size (as on the label):
Measurements (cm, laid flat):
Colour:
Material (from the label):
Condition: New with tags / New without tags / Excellent / Very good / Good / Fair
Flaws (where, what, how big, photo number):
Included:
Not included:
Tested? How? (electronics):
```

Blank is better than wrong: blank becomes a [CHECK] you can fix, wrong becomes a return.

Invented example: *Barbour men's wax jacket, Bedale, olive green, size 40, cord collar, tartan lining. Good condition: wax worn thin on both elbows, small repair to the inside pocket lining. No hood.*

## Prompt 1: the eBay UK title

```text
Write 3 eBay UK listing titles for the item in my brief below.
- Brand first, then item type, then model, size, colour, material.
- Aim for 70 to 80 characters. Never over 80 including spaces. Show the count after each.
- UK English and UK sizes.
- Use ONLY facts in my brief. Write [CHECK: ...] if a key word is missing.
- No hype words, exclamation marks, emoji or whole words in capitals.
- No rare, vintage, genuine or new unless the brief says so.
- No brand other than the item's own. No flaws in the title.

My brief:
[PASTE YOUR BRIEF]
```

A good result: `Barbour Mens Bedale Wax Jacket Olive Green Size 40 Cord Collar Tartan Lining` (76 characters). AI tools often miscount, so trust eBay's counter. Our guide on [how to write an eBay title in the UK](/blog/how-to-write-an-ebay-title-uk.html) explains the word order.

## Prompt 2: the description

```text
Write an eBay UK item description for the item in my brief.
Structure: one opening line (what it is); measurements in cm ("measured flat"); condition, then every flaw on its own line with location, size and photo number; material, features, what is included and not included.
Rules: UK English (colour, jumper, trousers, postage). Use ONLY facts in my brief. Write [CHECK: ...] for anything missing. Never invent measurements, materials, dates or test results. Include every flaw without softening it. No hype words, no exclamation marks, no emoji. Do not say rare, vintage, genuine, fully working or smoke-free unless the brief says so. Nothing about returns (I set those in eBay's settings). Short lines, under 200 words.

My brief:
[PASTE YOUR BRIEF]
```

Returns belong in eBay's settings, and business sellers must respect buyers' rights under UK consumer law whatever a description says. Not sure which condition grade to pick? Our [eBay condition description examples](/blog/ebay-condition-description-examples.html) help.

## Prompt 3: item specifics

```text
I am listing on eBay UK in the category [CATEGORY]. Here are the item specifics fields eBay shows me, and my brief.
Fill in each field using ONLY facts from my brief. If the brief does not answer it, write [CHECK: ...] and tell me where to look (care label, base, settings screen). Never guess brand, material, model or year. Use plain standard wording ("Grey", "Crew Neck"). No other brand names or extra keywords. Output a two-column table.

Fields: [COPY FIELD NAMES FROM THE FORM]
My brief: [PASTE YOUR BRIEF]
```

Real field names stop the AI inventing fields. See our [eBay item specifics UK guide](/blog/ebay-item-specifics-uk.html).

## How to use ChatGPT to list on eBay: a quick routine

1. Photograph the item and every flaw.
2. Fill in the brief with the item in front of you.
3. Paste Prompt 1, pick a title, check the count in eBay's form.
4. Paste Prompt 2 in the same chat. Fix every [CHECK].
5. Paste Prompt 3 with the real field names.
6. Read the whole listing next to your photos. Every flaw in a photo should be in the text.

## AI eBay listings in the UK: a few honest cautions

- **You are the editor.** "The AI wrote it" does not excuse a wrong description. Check every fact.
- **eBay has its own AI.** The eBay app can draft a listing from a photo, but a photo cannot see a care label or a stiff zip, so the same checks apply.
- **Photos must be real.** eBay's picture rules require photos of the actual item, so use your own, not AI images. Check the current rules in eBay's help pages.
- **Privacy.** Never paste buyers' names, addresses or messages into an AI tool.
- **Keep it human.** If a sentence would sound odd said out loud at a car boot sale, cut it.

**Why does ChatGPT get the character count wrong?**
AI tools read text in chunks, not letters, so counts can be a few out. Check the title in eBay's form.
