Where the evening goes

Listing takes forever. Then the messages start.

It is rarely one big job. It is lots of small ones, every night:

Typing the same measurements, condition notes and postage line for the fifteenth time this week.
Trying to squeeze an eBay title into 80 characters, then doing it again for Vinted.
"Is this still available?" Yes. Then radio silence.
A cheeky offer at 11pm, and twenty minutes deciding how to say no nicely.
Asking the AI for help, then fixing the charger and box it invented.
Guessing a price, then second-guessing it, then leaving it for tomorrow.

Is it worth the hassle? It can be, if the repetitive writing stops eating the time. The kit does not do your selling for you. It takes the typing and the "what do I say to this?" off your plate.

How it works

An evening with the kit, in three steps

  1. Jot the facts once

    As you photograph each item, fill in a Listing Brief: brand, size, measurements, condition, every flaw, postage. Scribbled notes are fine. One prompt turns a whole stack of messy notes into tidy briefs in one go, and marks anything missing with [CHECK] so you know exactly what to look up.

  2. List the batch in one chat

    Save your "shop style" once (how you post, what you always say), then list up to 10 items in a single chat session in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot. Need it for another platform? One prompt rewrites a finished listing for the others without starting again. Want a spreadsheet? Another gives you a table or CSV.

  3. Answer buyers from a ready set

    Decide your offer rules before the messages arrive, then use the message prompts or the 20 ready replies for availability, lowball offers, bundles, postage, late parcels and returns. Polite, short, on the platform, and within buyers' rights.

You still read every listing next to the item before it goes live. The AI drafts. You are the editor.

Example
An invented item, written to show the method. It is not a real listing or a real sale.

Before: your notes, typed at 9pm

m&s kids raincoat navy age 7 to 8 hood, poppers + zip, 2 pockets bit of wear on cuffs no rips fleece lined?? check pit to pit 40 length 55 vinted

Plus a buyer message waiting: "Is this still available?"

After: brief, W2 and M2 prompts

Title:
M&S navy hooded raincoat, age 7 to 8

Description:
Navy hooded raincoat from M&S, age 7 to 8 years (on the label). Zip and popper front, two front pockets.

Condition: good. Light wear on both cuffs (see photo 4). No rips or marks that I can see.

Measurements laid flat: pit to pit 40 cm, length 55 cm.
Lining: [CHECK: is it fleece lined? Look at the label]

Posted within 2 working days.

Reply to "Is this still available?":
Hi, yes it is still available. I can post it within 2 working days of purchase. Happy to send a photo of the lining if useful.

Nothing retyped

The facts go in once, as a brief. The title, description and reply all come from the same notes, so they match each other.

Nothing invented

The notes were unsure about the lining, so the AI flagged it with [CHECK] instead of guessing. Thirty seconds with the label fixes it.

Nothing to decide at 11pm

The reply answers the question first, adds one useful fact and keeps things on the platform. No fake "lots of interest".

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot. Nothing to install, and nothing connects to your marketplace accounts.

Do I need paid ChatGPT?

No. Every prompt works in the free versions: no plugins, no browsing, no paid features. Free versions have usage limits that change. If you hit one mid-batch, carry on later or switch to another free tool.

How much time will it save me?

We are not going to put a number on it, because it depends on your items and how you work. What we can say: you write the facts once per item instead of retyping them for every platform, the prompts do the drafting, and you have replies ready for the messages that come up again and again.

Is it for traders or private sellers?

Both. Where the rules differ (returns, buyer rights, Vinted Pro), the prompts ask whether you sell as a private seller or a business and word things to match. Business sellers' buyers keep their legal rights on returns and faulty items, and the kit never writes anything that tries to take them away. Unsure which you are? The kit points you to GOV.UK. It is not tax advice.

Will it guarantee sales?

No. Nobody can honestly promise that. The kit helps you write clear, accurate listings and replies faster. What sells still depends on the item, the price and the buyer.
