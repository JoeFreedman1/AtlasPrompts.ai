The UK Listing Cheat Sheet

Free from Well Listed. A Listing Brief, 10 starter prompts, a platform limits table and a check before you post. For UK sellers on eBay, Vinted, Depop, Etsy, Amazon and TikTok Shop, using the free versions of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot.

How it works: fill in the brief for one item, paste it into your AI tool with one of the prompts below, then check the result against the item before you post. The prompts tell the AI to use only your facts and to write [CHECK: ...] wherever something is missing, instead of making it up.

Privacy: never paste a buyer's name, address, order number or username into an AI tool. Items, yes. People, no.

2. Ten free starter prompts

Replace anything in [SQUARE BRACKETS AND CAPITALS] with your details. Paste the whole prompt: the rules at the bottom are what keep the AI honest.

[F1] Messy notes to Listing Brief

Use it when: you have scrappy notes about an item and want a tidy brief. Paste this:

Turn my notes below into a Listing Brief with these headings: Platform, What it is, Brand, Size, Measurements (cm), Colour, Material, Condition, Flaws, Included, Not included, Postage, Price.
Rules: UK English. Use ONLY facts in my notes. If a heading has no information, write [CHECK: what I need to find out]. Keep every flaw exactly as serious as I wrote it. No sales language.
My notes:
[PASTE YOUR NOTES]

What good output looks like: "Material: [CHECK: composition from the care label]" rather than a guess such as "100% wool". Check before you post:

  • Fill in every [CHECK] from the item itself.
  • Make sure nothing got upgraded ("wool mix" must not become "merino").

[F2] eBay UK title

Use it when: you want an eBay UK title that uses the space well without padding. Paste this:

Write 3 eBay UK titles for the item in my Listing Brief. Each must be 80 characters or fewer, with the character count shown.
Order: brand, item type, key detail (model, style or material), size, colour.
Rules: UK English. Only words that are true of this item. No other brand names, no "L@@K", no hype words, no exclamation marks.
My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

What good output looks like: "White Stuff Oatmeal Cable Knit Jumper Size 14 Cotton Wool Crew Neck (67)". Check before you post:

  • Count the characters in eBay's own form. AI tools often miscount.

[F3] Vinted title and description

Use it when: you are listing clothes, shoes or homeware on Vinted. Paste this:

Write a Vinted title and a short description for the item in my Listing Brief.
Title: short and plain: brand, item, size, colour.
Description: what it is; measurements in cm; condition with every flaw (where, what, how big); what is included; postage. Short lines for a phone screen.
Rules: UK English. Use ONLY facts in my brief and write [CHECK: ...] for gaps. No hype words, no emoji, no other brand names.
My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

What good output looks like: "Condition: very good. Light bobbling under both arms (see photo 5). No holes or marks." Check before you post:

  • Fill in Vinted's own fields (brand, size, condition, colour, material) to match.

[F4] Depop description and hashtags

Use it when: you are listing on Depop, where the first words of the description act as the title. Paste this:

Write a Depop description for the item in my Listing Brief. Start with one line: brand, item, size, colour. Then measurements in cm, condition with every flaw, and postage. Keep it short.
End with up to 5 hashtags, using only words that genuinely describe this item (no other brands, no unrelated trends).
Rules: UK English. Use ONLY facts in my brief and write [CHECK: ...] for gaps. No hype.
My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

What good output looks like: "#levis #denimjacket #90s" only if your brief says it is from the 1990s. Check before you post:

  • Every hashtag is true of the item.

[F5] Etsy title and 13 tags

Use it when: you are listing a handmade, vintage or craft supply item on Etsy. Paste this:

For the item in my Listing Brief, write:
1. An Etsy title of 140 characters or fewer, readable first: what it is, then material, colour and size.
2. 13 tags, each 20 characters or fewer, each one different, all true of this item.
Rules: UK English. Only facts in my brief. No other brand names. Do not call it vintage unless my brief says so.
My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

What good output looks like: tags such as "stoneware tealight" and "sage green candle" rather than 13 versions of "gift". Check before you post:

  • Check the tag and title limits in Etsy's listing form.

2. Remove hype (stunning, must have, timeless, perfect for any occasion, exclamation marks, emoji).
3. Remove any claim not in my Listing Brief, and list what you removed.
4. Keep every flaw, with where and how big.

What good output looks like: "Removed: 'fits true to size' (not in the brief). Measurements given instead." Check before you post:

  • If it removed something you know is true, add it back yourself.

[F7] Flaw wording that protects you

Use it when: you wrote your flaws in a hurry and want them clear and specific. Paste this:

Rewrite each flaw below so it says where it is, what it is, how big it is and which photo shows it. Do not soften anything and do not add flaws I have not mentioned. If a size or photo number is missing, write [CHECK: ...]. UK English, one line per flaw.
My flaws:
[PASTE YOUR FLAW NOTES]

What good output looks like: "Pale grey mark on the front, below the left pocket, about 5 mm (see photo 4)." Check before you post:

  • Every flaw has a photo.

[F8] Price from my sold listings

Use it when: you have looked up sold listings for similar items and want a sensible price. Paste this:

Below are sold listings I found myself for items like mine, with condition and sold price. Using only these, suggest a list price, a realistic range and a lowest price I should accept, and explain your reasoning in 3 short points. Weigh the closest matches in condition and size most. Say if there are too few comps to be confident.
My item (Listing Brief):
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]
Sold listings I found:
[PASTE TITLE, CONDITION AND SOLD PRICE FOR EACH]

What good output looks like: "The three closest matches sold between £11 and £15. Yours has light bobbling, so list at £14 and accept down to £10." Check before you post:

  • Use sold prices, not asking prices.
  • Allow for postage and any fees (check the platform's current fees).

[F9] Reply to a buyer message

Use it when: you get "is this still available?", a low offer or a question and want a polite reply. Paste this:

Write a short, friendly reply to this buyer message on [PLATFORM]. Answer the question first. Under 50 words.
If it is an offer: my list price is £[PRICE] and my lowest is £[LOWEST]. Never go below my lowest, never reveal it, and suggest using the platform's offer button.
Rules: UK English. Only facts from my Listing Brief. No fake urgency ("lots of interest"). Keep everything on the platform.
Buyer's message (name removed):
[PASTE MESSAGE]
My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

What good output looks like: "Hi, thanks for the offer. That's a bit low for this one, but I could do £12. I'll send it through the offer button if that works." Check before you post:

  • The item really is still available everywhere you list it.

[F10] Accuracy check before you post

Use it when: your listing is written and you want a second pair of eyes. Paste this:

Compare my listing with my Listing Brief. List:
1. Anything in the listing that is NOT in the brief (invented or assumed).
2. Any flaw in the brief that is missing from the listing.
3. Any US spelling, hype word or claim I cannot back up (rare, vintage, genuine, new, fully working).
Do not rewrite the listing. Just give me the list.
My listing:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING]
My Listing Brief:
[PASTE YOUR LISTING BRIEF]

What good output looks like: "1. 'Comes with box': the brief says no box." Check before you post:

  • Fix each point, then read the listing next to the item once more.

4. The 7-point check before you post

  1. Every fact matches the item. Read the listing with the item in front of you. Size, colour and material come from the label, not memory.
  2. No [CHECK] notes left. Each one is filled in with a real fact, or the line is deleted.
  3. Every flaw is in, with where, what, how big and a photo. If you are between two condition grades, pick the lower one.
  4. Measurements are in cm (for clothing, measured flat), or dimensions for everything else.
  5. UK English, no hype. Jumper, trousers, trainers, postage. No "stunning", no "must have", no exclamation marks.
  6. Nothing you cannot back up. No "rare", "vintage", "genuine", "new" or "fully working" unless you know it is true. No other brand names in the title or tags.
  7. Within the platform's limits and rules, and everything stays on the platform: no contact details, no off-platform payment.
