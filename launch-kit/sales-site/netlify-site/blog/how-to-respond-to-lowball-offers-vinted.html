How to respond to lowball offers on Vinted (politely, and without caving)

The best way to respond to a lowball offer on Vinted is to decide your floor price before any offers arrive, then choose one of three short replies: counter at a price you are happy with, decline politely and invite a better offer, or accept if it clears your floor. Keep it under 30 words, stay friendly, give one true reason if you give any, and send any counter through the app's offer tools. Never lecture the buyer about what the item is worth.

You list a coat at £30. Within the hour: "£8?" Here is how to handle it in ten seconds.

First, decide what "lowball" means for you

A lowball is not an offer that feels rude. It is an offer below your floor: the lowest price you would genuinely accept, after thinking about postage, the time you have spent and what similar items sell for. If you have not set one, that is why offers feel stressful. Every one becomes a fresh decision.

Our guide to how to price items on Vinted UK covers setting a list price and floor from comparable items.

Make an offer plan before you post

For each listing, write three numbers in your notes. Worked example (invented): a Seasalt yellow raincoat, size 12, good condition, listed at £30, floor £20.

OfferWhat we do
£26 or moreAccept
£20 to £25Counter at £26
Under £20Decline politely

Now "£8?" takes no thought at all. It is a polite decline, and you can get on with your evening.

Three replies you can copy

1. The friendly counter

Thanks for the offer. £22 is a bit low for me, but I could do £26. I will send it through the offer button if that works for you.

This is for offers between your floor and your target (here, £20 to £25). Counter with a real number, not "make me a better offer". A specific figure gives the buyer something to say yes to.

2. The polite decline

Thanks for your interest, but I cannot go that low on this one. You are welcome to make another offer.

This is how to decline an offer on Vinted politely without closing the door. Some buyers open low to see what happens, then come back with a sensible figure.

3. The accept

Thanks, happy to accept. I post the next working day after purchase.

Only say "next working day" if you can.

How to negotiate price on Vinted without the back and forth

Counter once, then hold. If they come back below your counter but above your floor, you can meet them or stand firm. What you want to avoid is five rounds over £1.

Give a reason only if it is true. "Similar ones have sold for more" is fine if you actually looked. "It is heavy to post" is fine if it is. Do not invent another interested buyer or a deadline. Fake urgency is the kind of thing that gets screenshotted.

Offer a bundle instead of a discount. If they have favourited other items of yours, a bundle price can work for both of you, because they save on postage. "I cannot go lower on the coat alone, but I could do the coat and the scarf together for £32."

Do not reply at 11pm. Offers will still be there in the morning. Replies written when tired are the ones you regret.

The awkward ones

"I can get it cheaper elsewhere"

That is fair enough. This one is £30, as it is in good condition with no marks. Thanks for looking.

No argument needed. If they can, they will.

The buyer who makes an offer and vanishes

Normal. Some buyers send offers on several items at once and buy the first one accepted. Do not chase more than once.

"Can I pay you directly to save the fees?"

Thanks, but I only sell and take payment through the app, so we are both protected. You are welcome to buy or make an offer here.

Always say no. Paying off the platform removes the buyer's protection and yours, and it is how many scams start. More replies like this are in our Vinted message templates.

What not to do

  • Sarcasm. "LOL" or "do you think I am a charity?" feels good for about four seconds.
  • Lecturing. "This cost £90 new" rarely changes anyone's mind.
  • Going below your floor because you are fed up. That is what the floor is for.
  • Blocking everyone who offers low. Keep blocking and reporting for genuinely abusive messages.

FAQ

Is it rude to decline an offer on Vinted? No. A short, friendly decline is normal, and inviting another offer keeps things pleasant.

Should I always counter a lowball offer? Only if the buyer might realistically reach your floor. For a very low offer, a polite decline is quicker and just as courteous.

How much should I counter with? A specific figure at or above your floor, usually a little below your list price. Decide it before offers arrive so you are not working it out on the spot.
