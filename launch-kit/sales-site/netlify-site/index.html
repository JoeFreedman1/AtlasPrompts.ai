The problem

You asked the AI for a listing. It gave you a brochure.

Sellers are not against AI. They are against AI that makes things up. If any of these sound familiar, the kit is built for you.

"A stunning addition to any collection." It is a jumper.
It said "barely worn". It was worn plenty. Now the buyer wants a refund.
The description is three paragraphs long and still does not say the size.
It added a box and a charger I never had. That is an instant "item not as described".
Colour, sweater, fall, "fits true to size". Very American, and none of it checked.
By the time I have fixed what it made up, I could have written it myself.

The fix is not a cleverer AI. It is better instructions: give it only the facts, tell it what it may not do, and make it flag the gaps. That is what every prompt in this kit does.

    Fix the [CHECK] notes, or run the checker prompt to compare the draft against your brief and the platform's rules. Then read it next to the item and post it yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Which AI tools does it work with?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot. You copy a prompt, paste it into the chat with your Listing Brief, and the tool does the writing. Nothing to install and nothing to connect to your marketplace accounts.

Do I need paid ChatGPT?

No. Every prompt is written for the free versions: no plugins, no browsing, no paid features. Free versions have usage limits that change from time to time. If you hit one, try again later or switch to another free tool; the prompts work in all of them.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The prompt library is a single page that opens in your phone's browser, works offline once downloaded, and has a search box and a copy button on every prompt. The PDF and text files open on any phone too.

Is it for traders or private sellers?

Both. Most of the kit is about writing accurate listings, which matters either way. Where the rules differ (returns, buyer rights, Vinted Pro), the prompts ask whether you sell as a private seller or a business and word replies to match. If you sell as a business, buyers keep their legal rights on returns and faulty items, and the kit never writes anything that tries to take those away. If you are unsure which you are, the kit points you to GOV.UK; it is not tax advice.

Will it guarantee sales?

No, and anyone who promises that is guessing. What the kit does is help you write clear, complete, honest listings and replies more quickly. Clear, accurate listings are what buyers look for, but what sells depends on the item, the price and the buyer.

Anything I should know about Etsy?

The Etsy prompts write titles, 13 tags and descriptions for handmade, vintage and craft supply items, warm but factual. Etsy has its own Creativity Standards: if AI was used to create or design the item itself, Etsy asks you to say so. Using AI to help write the listing text is a different thing, but the Etsy checker prompt reminds you of the rule. Check Etsy's current rules in its help pages.

Anything I should know about TikTok Shop?

The TikTok Shop UK prompts are for sellers with products to sell: compliant product titles (no promo wording, symbols or other platform names), descriptions, a claims check that catches health and "guaranteed" style wording, faceless video scripts and a live-selling run-sheet. TikTok Shop's rules change, so the prompts remind you to check the current policies in Seller Center.

Is it safe to paste buyer messages into an AI tool?

Only the words of the question, never the person. The kit shows you how to remove names, addresses, order numbers and usernames first (replace them with [BUYER]). Items, yes. People, no.
